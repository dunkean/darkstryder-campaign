Every character must make an Easy sneak roll to avoid alerting the soldiers to their presence in the ancient irrigation canal. If the characters remain quiet, the squad charges through the alley and out onto the next intersecting street.

Makezh tells his newfound companions that this irrigation system once provided water to the farms that covered the lands where Eror Zeen now stands. The tunnels have remained empty aside from the murkystreamof rainwaterthat continues to collect as years pass for almost two decades. Makezh'sfather worked the irrigation system, and Makezh still remembers its basic layout. He be­ lieves he can lead the characters to a point some­ where near their ship in less than 20 minutes.

At this point, gamemasters can panic the player characters a bit. Makezh can make a wrong turn that leads them into a dead end, or a sudden downward-slope in the tunnel plunges everyone whofails a ModerateDexterilyroll into the murky water. The atmosphere of the tunnel is claustro­ phobic and oppressive, and the opportunity for players to roleplay their characters in these un­ pleasant conditions should be explored.
