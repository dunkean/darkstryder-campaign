**Operation: Chillweaver

*Genesis***

While in the Kathol Republic, Captain Kaiya Adrimetrum of the *Farstar*, at the
request of First Officer Gorak Khzam, assigned the Senior Lieutenant Zebin Moyato and his
Red Squad, 1st Platoon, A company, 5th Regiment, 1st Division "The Night
Terrors" to "hurt Chuborro the Hutt.  The crime boss ruled over a large
organization that was a large part of the Kathol Sector.  He was making things
difficult for the *Farstar* in the Outback.  He had just posted a bounty on
the crews' heads, and anyone that helped the *Farstar* in its mission.  First
Officer Khzam seemed to have his own motivations to take down the Hutt, but was not
forthcoming with those issues.  The Captain was getting tired if the so called "*Lance
of Endor*" giving the Farstar and the New Republic a bad name in this sector.
  At the time, Sarne was no where to be seen, so it was an opportunity to take care
of other problems.

Senior Lieutenant Moyato chose 7 of his team for the job with his second, Master
Sergeant Din Wilkes, in command and requested that Mavia Tanatis,  Lady Amber
"The Tigress" Comark, and Breslin "The Dragon" Drake to be assigned to
his command as well. He saw use for the assassin and the intelligence agents.  He
also pooled all local intelligence he could on the situation he was sending his men into.
  

The area they were going to was a not-well-known area of the Kathol Outback, called the
Demon's Handle.  It included three primary systems and a few dead systems.  
Chuborro, according to Khzam, resided on a miserable planet called Yog'H'Toth.   That
planet was too hot for insertion, however, a neighboring world was more suitable for their
staging area of Operation: Chillweaver (*A name chosen by Moyato himself after a
snake-like worm that strikes very quietly from the grasses of some dismal world he once
was assigned to*).  This world was called Ambrox, and was the headquarters of
another organization they had heard a little about; the Sabiador Slave Ring. 
Loh'Khar said he could persuade a few free traders to insert them there and retrieve when
they needed out.  This sounded  good to Moyato.

Moyato's first instinct was to find allies.  The Sabiador known past with Chuborro
was shaky at best, according to what the Lt. could find.  Because the Sabiador
dominated in this sector in the one business Chuborro had no holding in, there had been
known conflicts in the past.  However, the group recently had a leadership shake up
after their leader died of unknown causes.  It was unknown who was leading them now.
  Moyato wanted to investigate alliance possibilities with them first.

The Skandrei Bandits were also another choice, however, they would more-then-likely not
be friendly to any New Republic interests.  They had proven that by siding with the
anti-NR free traders, who were convinced by the *Lance of Endor*-attacks that the
NR was no better than the Empire.  The Skandrei Bandits could be a valuable ally if
they could be convinced the Night Terrors they weren't NR.

Moyato at that moment decided that the Night Terrors would not go in as a covert NR
team.  Loh'Khar had to work to get them uniforms, and equipment, but the Night
Terrors were soon suited up as the best facsimile of an Imperial SpecOps team they could
put together.  If Sarne wanted to make them look bad, they were going to reverse the
tables on him.  Rumors had already circulated that the Hutt had made an alliance with
the Moff, so that was icing on the cake.

This was all that they could gather.  Anything else would have to be gathered when
the operation was initiated on Ambrox, after insertion.

**Operation: Chillweaver

*Initiate***

The first thing to do was to put out feelers. While the two and three man teams, spread
across the town of Abroxia settled in and prepared for their part of the operation, Mavia,
Drake, and Comark gathered intelligence on Ambrox, and on Yog'H'Toth.  Transit
between the two worlds proved to be easier than they thought, on an individual basis.
  Loh'Khar had supplied them with enough contacts that they could get whatever
documents and IDs they needed to be a normal citizen of the two worlds.

Mavia, Drake and Tigress both went out to the 3 area worlds to see what they could find
on Chuborro, his local deputies and the other organizations of interest (The Sabiador
Slave Ring, and the Skandrei Bandits). They also took it upon themselves to boost the
Night Terror's disguise as Imperials, and laid subtle evidence that there was some kind of
secret Imperial operation going on. They used known Imperial contacts, known Imperial
techniques.

Yog’H’Toth is an unfriendly world. Its atmosphere is
very active and turbulent. The Society is one of the most corrupt anyone has seen, thanks
to Chuborro’s presence. This is the seedier side of the Free traders. It’s a
smuggler’s paradise, by Kathol standards. There are even signs that read "The
Millenium Falcon docked here!!" 

The world is also hazardous to high tech equipment, due the
over-abundance of electrostatic charges in the atmosphere. During the harshest storms,
people wear protective suits. Ball lightning is quite common on this world.

Attached is intelligence on major population centers, Areas
that Chuborro seems to concentrate his business on Yog’H’Toth, and the location
of his palace. His palace is heavily guarded (estimates included) and heavily armed. It
has several planetary-based Turbolaser batteries installed in the mountains around his
palace.

**Intel: Chuborro’s background**

Chuborro is one of the oldest Hutts alive. At one time, before
Jabba’s death, he was retired. He came out of retirement when Jabba died, and seized
power of his interests in the Minos Cluster and the Kathol Sector. For some reason, he
wanted this particular portion of Jabba’s fortune and nothing else.

Chuborro is eccentric and boisterous but very reclusive. He is
old and paranoid as well. The intel includes some of his known activities, when he meets
with his deputies and what ships he likes to use. There is no intelligence on a regular
schedule, but there are clues to what he likes to do, and when he likes to leave his
palace. One thing to note is that Chuborro holds occasional galas in his palace 

**Intel: Sarne Alliance**

It has been confirmed that Chuborro is allied with Sarne, but
it seems it’s a shaky one. He has been keeping the alliance a secret from many of his
deputies, only telling them that he has made a deal that will solidify their organization
position in the Rim. Some in his organization would frown upon it.

In particular, on that last note, there are strong
anti-Imperial sentiments in his own security force, the Hagnar Guild. There already
resides a strong rumor of the alliance, but nothing has been confirmed enough to cause
unrest. Many of the mercs are former Imperial slaves, or escaped Imperial prisoners.

Further information later comes in of a meeting with someone
high up in Chuborro’s organization with someone high up in Sarne’s organization.
The when and where is unclear, but there was talk that *the Lance of Endor* might be
involved.

Chuborro has had a passion for owning a piece of Darkstryder
recently. Whether this is part of the alliance or not is unknown. Whether he has gotten a
sample of DS Tech is unknown. This may be what Sarne promised him Or if Chuborro already
had some, this maybe why Sarne made the alliance.

There is also a rumor that Chuborro has made another
alliance… "An outside interest…".

steal what the can, enslave captives, and devastate the rest. They have a strong hatred of
Chuborro.

While on planet Yog’H’Toth, an informant met with Mavia, and dropped
information about the Skandrei Bandits. This informant approached Mavia.  Mavia did
not recognize him but heard him out.

Mavia asked how this particular individual became so well informed, and all he said was
*"Word is out that a mission to strike the worm is in the works around here, and I
heard you were a line into them. If I am wrong, consider it just an easy way to get a free

The Informant then proceeded to educate Mavia on the absolutely heinous activities that
the Skandrei Bandits and it’s leader, Vok Gan, has engaged in, including raids on
innocent settlements, and conscription of young males and females into their ranks. He
suggested that it would be better if the Skandrei Leader were, in fact...eliminated. The
informant even went as far as to offer the bounty of 23,000 Cr. He suggested to Mavia that
the Skandrei are potentially dangerous enough to the strike against Chuborro that their
complete removal from the picture might make things go more simply for them. She in turn
accepted the job, unbeknownst to Wilkes.

*"Of course, if the Skandrei can't be completely eliminated, I understand...but
surely the pirate leader can be taken out of the picture by someone of your renowned
skills?"*

She inquired further, and the informant said that her reputation proceeded her, and

Chuborro the Hutt,  that is eager to see both the Skandrei and the worm get spiked.
This rival organization is described to Mavia as lying in wait to pick up the pieces and
has a well-placed informant among the New Republic *Farstar's* crew. She pressed for
more information about this agent, but she was assured that the agent was quite
benevolent.

This information proved valuable to Wilkes and Moyato's
overall plan. His plan was to put a wedge between the two deputies, and manipulate it to
start a little internal underground war for Chuborro the Hutt.  This required special
manipulation of each of the deputies surrounding, and dissemination of certain information
at the right time.  It was all a part of the game, and the Night Terrors were the
visiting team.

**Operation: Chillweaver

*The Game***

The plan revolved around the Sabacc night Jag and Ug had
regular.  Once they found out the next game night, things were set in motion.  
Misinformation was distributed in key places to start rumors of a major Imperial action,
as well as information about an action taken by either Jag or Ug.  The plan was to be
well executed and come to a head the night of the sabacc game.  Jag's fleet would
play a part in the whole plan, as did several of Ug's Lieutenants.  Wilkes broke it
down into stages.

(Stage 1) To create a black hole around a specific area
impotan to Ug that is owned by Jag.  They had to "disconnect" Ug's spies
and informant in and around a given shipyard or similar facility owned by Jag.

They found a facility; a dead moon Jag uses as a training
facility for his mercenaries. It's also a port for a majority of his convert supplies. It
was a small insignificant place which is why Jag used it. It's name was Alparan Mox, a
small world near Ambrox in a otherwise dead system. The moon orbited a gas giant, the only
planet in the system. There were 6 other moons. Jag's fleet used it as a resupply
point often, and a portion of the fleet uses it as a base. 

(Stage 2) Research An Ug facility that could support the
Jag'Nar fleet

At the appropriate time, this would be transmitted in a manner that Ugnog's agents can
intercept it. Ug used a facility on a moon of Gadella, the
gas giant in the Sapellan system. It was a major outlaw tech facility, very well hidden.
It is hidden among a series of old mines. Landing was a trick for larger cap-ships, but it
could be done. 

(Stage 3) A place the Night Terrors  could use as the
"gathering place for the Jag'Nar fleet"  There was no real plan to get the
fleet there, but it was to be used as misinformation nexus; an enigma.  Sensors could
not penetrate the area, and the only way to conform the rumors of a fleet gathering was
too risky.

They had already encountered a small highly active nebula
near Sapella, where the *Farstar* misjumped into.

(Stage 4) Plant information that Jag is looking for a
Whiphid specific poison.

(Stage 5) On the night of the Sabacc game, assassinate,
detain, and/or simply disconnect the majority of Ug's military leaders and lieutenants.
  

Direct from Wilkes' Op-Plan:

The general plan is this: Ugnog is to be convinced that
Jag'Nok is going to seriously assault him the night of their regular Sabbac game. Evidence
will be fabricated suggesting that the Jag'Nar fleet is readying for a full deployment. We
will also plant the impression that Jag is planning to kill Ug at the game. This should be
enough to make any info-broker mad with paranoia.

At the same time, rumors will be spread to the effect that
there is an Imperial strike team working with Chuborro to eliminate some of the weaker, or
less productive bosses, and redistribute their influence among the remainder.

The setup would take weeks, and the actual execution would
happen a few nights before the game or on the night of the game.  Ug will find out
about the information-black hole, and the fleet movement on the night of the game, and
soon after, not be able to contact any of his lieutenants.  This will start the rift,
and perhaps enough tension that the already precarious organization will begin to crumble.
  All things waited for the night of the game.

On the night of the game, things had settled into place
rather smoothly,with only a few hang-ups.  Mavia disappeared for a significant period
of time.  She was assigned to take out specific lieutenants on the night of the game,
all of which were on Yog'H'Toth.  She seemed to arrange it that way, with a  
little prodding to Wilkes. It became apparent she had her own agenda.

It was that night, they say that the Skandrei bandit
leader was also assassinated.  The eveidence that was left behind poined to none
other than Chuborro's own assassin, the Falleen Uluala. The Night Terrors and the *Farstar*
never knew about the heated past Mavia had with the Fallen, and now, probably never would.

*Operation: Chillweaver

*The Finale***

The Night Terrors ceased all operation once the Sabacc game night came
and went.  The operation that night went off with little problems, and all returned
in one piece.  The rift was started but they had no idea how wide it would get.

Chuborro was planning a meeting with Sarne and his minions at some
point. After the sabacc game, and things started loosening up, it became known that the
worm had plans to meet the Imperials soon, on a specific date, but did not plan to use his
own fleet as escort.  He had made peace with the Skandrei bandits, and planned to
secretly use them as escort to the Qektoth Confederation.  That information was soon
disseminated into his organization.  This only bolstered the rift already forming.
  The fact that was already strong anti-Imperial sentiments with in his fleet and his
security force helped.   

The final stone was the assassination of the Skandrei bandit leader.   
Evidence pointed to Chuborro's own personal assassin, Uluala, but this didn't come out
until Chuborro was already on board and on his way to meet Sarne, Skandrei Bandits in
escort.  Of course, Wilkes had spent enough time with Mavia to at least recognize her
handy-work.  He wasn't aware that she new anyone specific in Chuborro's organization,
least of all his personal assassin.

It all came to a head when Chuborro arrived at Qek'Mas in the Qektoth Confederation.
  He was to meet Sarne there, but soon after his arrival, the Jag'Nar Fleet arrived,
demanding an explanation.  The engaged the Imperials before the got an answer, and
the melee was on.  Soon afterwards.  the Skandrei bandits loyal to the dead
leader broke off and attacked both the Chuborro loyal factions within their ranks and the
Jag'Nar Fleet. The Qek joined in after, followed by a militant group of free trader ships
bent of the destruction of the Bandits. It escalated into a chaotic battle.  The
Imperials regrouped and began to make jumps out of the Qek confederation towards the Rift.
  They left the others to fight it out.

This battle destroyed Chuborro's command structure.  His five deputies broke off
from his organization to form their own organizations, some legitimate while others
remained illegal.  Those that went legitimate, aligned with the New Republic.  
The others crawled off in the shadows, waiting for their opportunity to arise. 

Operation Chillweaver was a success.
