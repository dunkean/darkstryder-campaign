# Commander Lofryyhn

## Chief Engineer and First Officer, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Gamesmaster character, played by [Baz McAlister](credits.html#bazatron)

 

![Loffryhn](../images/b319846a54af6cec72c4d5784e4ccd31021b64c8648e58a0a78f7751733b0360.jpg)

{Whuuuaarrrn!}

 

This reddish-brown furred Wookiee is an old friend of the *FarStar's* original First Officer Kaiya Adrimetrum - the two served in the same Rebel unit after Kaiya left her homeworld of Siluria III to join the Alliance. The two developed a close friendship through several infiltration missions. Lofryyhn always provided technical support for missions, and wasn't too bad in combat, either. Although Kaiya and Lofryyhn rarely speak of their adventures together, it is rumoured that Lofryyhn saved Kaiya's life on many occasions. Kaiya claims that the Wookiee knows a very specialised form of Kashyyykian martial arts known as Wrruushi which makes him downright devastating in close combat - although Lofryyhn tends to avoid the subject.

 

Lofryyhn later put his technical knowledge and expertise to work aboard the Rebel Blockade Runner *Rancor's Bane* - a modified Corellian Corvette transporting special ops teams throughout the Outer Rim Territories. Unfortunately, the ship was captured by pirates, and Lofryyhn was sold to slavers. To this day he still wears the brand of the Sabiador Slavers, who sold him to Moff Kentor Sarne on Kal'Shebbol. Rather than pitting the Wookiee against the other large aliens in his gladiatorial arena, Sarne recognised Lofryyhn's technical talents and put him to work in his docking bays, repairing transports and small capital ships under close guard.

 

Now that he's free, Lofryyhn wants to help the New Republic however he can. Right now, that's serving as the *FarStar*'s Chief Engineer. He spent some time working on the *FarStar *when it was the *Renegade,* Moff Sarne's ship, and he's very familiar with the old vessel. Lofryyhn knows the idiosyncrasies of the *FarStar*, from the proper hum of the ion engines to the glitches in the electrical systems. He has slung a hammock in a small engineer's station aft, but he's seldom there - the *FarStar* requires his almost constant attention to stay operational. 

 

Lofryyhn is also a bit of a tinkerer, and is fond of using his roll of duratape and some wiring he keeps in his satchel to string the *FarStar's* systems together. In what little spare time he has, Lofryyhn is fond of constructing small gadgets out of the scraps he finds on board the ship, or out of useless starship parts. Due to the lack of new parts, not everything he constructs is practical. Lofryyhn once constructed the equivalent of a tracking bug about as big as his fist - he could have made it smaller, but he didn't have the proper parts to work with.

 

Lofryyhn commands several technicians whose sole job is to maintain the *FarStar*. Most of them seem to have some knowledge of Wookiee, or at least get the gist of what he's saying half the time. Kaiya, of course, can communicate freely with Lofryyhn, too. The immense Wookiee is a fairly chummy being, and enjoys laughing with the crew and listening to their stories from far-off systems.

Season Three Update: 

 

***

When the *FarStar* travelled back in time to the Clone Wars, its crew was astonished to find a Major Lofryyhn serving as Chief Engineer on the Jedi Warship *Freedom’s Angel*, under Colonel Obi-Wan Kenobi. When questioned about this, Lofryyhn said that between the Clone Wars and his service in the Rebel Alliance, he had resigned his commission in the Republic Armed Forces and returned to Kashyyyk to be with his family. When he joined the Rebels, he felt it dishonourable to seek recognition for past glories, and thus served as a Sergeant for many years. Captain Ciro recently promoted the Wookiee to First Officer to replace Thyte, demoted for brawling with Dajus in a public place. Following the trial of the two brawlers, Lofryyhn extended his Life Debt to both, saying that if either of them let him down, he would have to kill them in order to preserve honour. Lofryyhn uses his new position of authority to the best of his abilities, now freely calling upon his immense experience of the military in general, and of Corellian Corvettes in particular. Lofryyhn has now discovered that it was Gorak who enslaved him and sold him to Sarne – he paid back the Rodian with a few broken ribs and left it at that. However, imagine the Wookiee’s surprise when a few days later, Gorak swore a crude approximation of a Wookiee life debt to him! Perhaps the Rodian was worth something after all…
