## Special Skills:

Strength skills:

Shape-shifting. Time to use:varies, depending on shape;generally 1-10 minutes.Consider this skill advanced for advancement purposes. This skill allows the Protrean to assume complex shapes.Simple geometric shapes,like cubes or spheres,are Very Easy or Easy to replicate.A basic humanoid form is Difficult to replicate, additional details add more modifiers to the difficulty. Protreansroll this skill when assumingthe new form.They don't have to roll to maintain the new form or to resume their normal state.
