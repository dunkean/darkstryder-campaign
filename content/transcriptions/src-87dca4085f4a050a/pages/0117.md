## Trap Rooms

Aside from the minor traps and the hunters,to reach the center of the Chambers,theRebels will have to deal with one or more of the Trap Rooms.These are special rooms that testthe Rebels'skills and resourcefulness.Each Trap is unique;each provides a different kind of challenge.When the Rebels enter aTrap Room, a blast door slams shut behind them,shutting off their retreat.The Rebels have to pass through or solve the Trap to escape.The computers controlling the Trap Rooms are programmed not to activate when everyone in the room is wearing silver helmets.
