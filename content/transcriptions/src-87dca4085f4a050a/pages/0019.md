The Rebels'flight from the detention block has led them to the upper levels of the engineering section. With Walex Blissex's help, they begin a journey that will take them to the heart of the Star Destroyer-to the engine controlcore.On the way,theymust circumvent collapsing engines and other hazards, while trying to ascertain exactly what is going on outside the ship.
