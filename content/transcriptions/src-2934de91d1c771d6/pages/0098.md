The average sizes below assume the average size for the lines which make up the squadron. Larger than normal lines or more lines than normal can easily double the size of a squadron.

Troop squadrons have two troop lines, an attack line, and a skirmish line for an average of 25 to 30 vessels.A troop squadron is most often used as a transfer point for Army units, reinforcing or reorganizing the Army complement of other squadrons.
