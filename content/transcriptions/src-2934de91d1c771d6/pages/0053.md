## Escort Carrier

The need for an Escort TIE Carrier became apparent after the Battle of Ton-Falk. In that battle, two Imperial Frigates and a Dreadnaught were lost due to, as Naval Command reported,"inadequate TIE support."So the Navy commissioned Kuat Drive Yards to construct a vessel that would serve the specific function of carryingTIE fighters into battle.

The main duties of Escort Carriers are to augment the starfighter strength of the fleet they areattachedto,andto transportnon-hyperspace support shuttles from system to system. Box-like and inelegant in appearance, KDY's Escort Carrier performs these missions admirably.

Each Carrier bases an entire TIEwing in its cavernous bays. Imperial policy mandates that at least one squadron of TIE Interceptors be included in the mix. Additional bays can carry up to six shuttles.
