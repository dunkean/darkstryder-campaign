## Regiment

Regiments are commanded by a lieutenant colonel who runs an HQ identical in structure to that of a battalion with the exception of a larger compliment of Droids, usually upwards of 200. Regimental HQ is where the most extensive repairs are performed, and some HQs even have a capacity to machine or nanomanufacture precise spare parts from very basic components. Regimental HQs often stay aboard the mother vessel, as orbital command control is as good in most instances as control on the ground. If orbitalcommandis disrupted theHQcan bequickly dropped (thereis a record of at least one conflict where the regimental HQ was dropped in life pods as landing barges were unavailable).If regimental HQ is dropped with the attacking forces, it is an indication that the Empire expects to maintain a ground force for at least a month and that the naval presence will be not be continual.
