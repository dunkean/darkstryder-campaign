## ZERO G STORMTROOPER

Maintenance technician demonstrates entry

into Zero G assault armor.Once inside, the operator is supported by the saddle; additional support is provided by an internal harness (omitted for clarity). Designed to be worn around standard stormtrooper armor, the Zero G assault armor is awkward for an unarmored being to use, although not impossible.
