# Lieutenant Carne Lopak

## Starfighter Pilot, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Played by [Phil Rodger](credits.html#phil)

 

![](../images/4131b917ceb12dae413952fafe0316257aacdadf7b61e833bd88203669f0a2d3.jpg)

"It’s a trap! It smells like an ambush!"

 

Carne Lopak is a steady New Republic Mon Calamari man, he served first aboard the Frigate *Ivory Tower* for three months before he was transferred to the *FarStar* where he has spent the last 14 months as Outrider 5.  Originally it was rumoured amongst the other pilots and techs that he ended up on the Farstar mission because he was a liability, a danger to himself and others and in the most part this was true, in the beginning.  The last few months have seen Lopak mature into a fair pilot although he still has an impetuous streak both in, and out of the cockpit which has on several occasions led him into danger.

 

In his off-duty hours he spends most of his time in one of the cargo bays honing his Slamball skills, although given the lack of space aboard the ship his chances of ever forming a team are remote.  The rest of the time is spent in the ship's bar although his antics when under the influence have led to a determined attempt to cut down on this particular activity.  His few friends aboard ship include Tal Achilleos, Brogge Portabjarn and ‘Sticky’ Bereliostik from the squadron.  He also takes a certain pleasure in tormenting Heppy (and balancing things on him).

 

Lopak spent his early years on the Mon Calamari homeworld, with his father, a wealthy councillor, his mother, who worked as a designer in the shipyards and his older sister Kara.  Carne was a popular figure at school, although his academic performance was below average he was regarded by many as a future star of Slamball, his only passion in those days.  All this changed when he was 10 and the Empire came to Mon Calamari. Carne was devastated to learn of his father's role as a collaborator and he and his sister fled the planet with their mother.  Lopak resolved to fight the Empire any way he could and vowed to find his father again and force him to face up to his crimes, after giving him a severe beating.  Lopak was too young to join the rebellion but after the defeat of the empire he was one of the first of a new generation of New Republic fighter pilots although his skill was eclipsed by his enthusiasm.  He still keeps in touch with his mother and his sister although he has been unable to contact them since the beginning of the mission.

 

Lopak is short, even for a Mon Calamari, with brown skin. He has a nervous air about him, and his incapacity for alcoholic beverages is legendary.
