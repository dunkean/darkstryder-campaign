Page,

 

The council would like to offer our condolences to you on the apparent loss of Captain Keleman Ciro and Sgt. Major Kaiya Adrimetrum. I know you were personally very fond of these two, and I had followed their work for some time now. 

 

As you and I both know, the  chances are that the *FarStar *has been destroyed in the Battle for Kathol system. The black box flight recorder we recovered from the Bothan craft found drifting in the Kathol Outback was damaged by its passage through the Kathol Rift, but sufficient information was retrieved to indicate that Outrider Squadron had some part in the battle, with several of their craft being destroyed.

 

The flight recorder also shows some interesting information regarding the other participants in the battle, namely the "Qektoth Confederation", and the "Sanhedrim" referred to in the comm-chatter. Rest assured that this matter will not go uninvestigated. I am dispatching a team of highly trained agents to Kathol Sector within the next few days, with orders to proceed beyond the Rift and find out what happened to the *FarStar*, and if there is any trace of Moff Sarne’s survival. Your inevitable request to accompany them will be denied, my friend – I have need of you to head up the operation to bring down Warlord Zsinj in the Outer Rim Territories.

 

I – and the others on the council – know that the *FarStar *crew did an outstanding job, and came very far in a desperate mission that, I admit, we should have paid more attention to. Whether or not the crew has perished, there will be no honours ceremony – official or otherwise, private or public – for those who found the Kathol system. Your request has been taken under advisement, my friend, and my answer remains the same.

 

The council still believes this to be a delicate matter. The presence of a planet which produces "Darkstryder Technology" such as that used by Moff Sarne at Kal’Shebbol is too tempting a target for any greedy Imperial Warlord to pass up. If the public were made aware of this place, the Empire would be made aware as well.

 

And then, we’d have to fight the same battle all over again.

 

If you honour the *FarStar’s *mission, it is best to let the matter disappear – at least for the moment, until complete investigations have been made. The incident is still sensitive, and Councillor Fey’lya’s role in sending the Bothan Combat Response Element after the *FarStar *is still unclear, and requires further scrutiny. New Republic Intel teams are now deleting every record of the mission, and all those who knew about it have been sworn to secrecy. We have buried sensitive material in the past, and now we must do so again. It pains me that we cannot grant the crew of the *FarStar *the recognition that they so richly deserve, and Councillor Organa Solo is particularly distressed also.

 

On a personal note, I’d like to congratulate you – you picked a fine crew for the mission, and we are once again in your debt. I will be in touch again when our investigations bear fruit.

May the Force be with you.

 

Mon Mothma.
