# The Mission Reports


During the FarStar's mad dash across the Outer Rim and beyond, a lot of events not detailed in the published campaign have occurred. It is fair to say that once Baz got his hands on those hallowed books, the campaign was never the same again. It is our intention to present here in the Mission Reports section some of the subplots that the Darksytryder campaign threw up - subplots that have essentially taken over the campaign during its three-year realtime run. Thus there may be some sensitive information being discussed here from time to time, so if you're playing in the Darkstryder campaign at the moment, READ NO FURTHER AS THERE MAY BE SPOILERS!


By the way, I wasn't joking about the subplots taking over the whole thing - if someone had asked, say, Mr. Thyte at the beginning of the campaign "Where do you see yourself in a year's time?", it's probably safe to suggest that he wouldn't have mentioned time travelling to the Clone Wars, fighting shapeshifters plotting to take over the galaxy, tangling with Mandalorians, being promoted to Commander and finding out that Han Solo is his little brother. Epic stuff.

- [The DarkStryder Incident](dsincident.html): The communique from Mon Mothma that ended season two of the campaign when the *FarStar* was thought to be lost.

- [Kathol Resurection](katholresurrect.html): The communique that began the first "Spin-off series" : DarkStryder *Resurrection*.

- [Incom-Qektoth "NEMESIS" -Class Modified X-Wing Fighter](nemesis.html): Technical schematics for the ultimate Star Fighter.

- [Gorak's Season One Mission Log](gorak_logs.html): Gorak's security assesments during season one before possesion by the Dark Jedi Vee'Khar caused him to betray the ship and flee.
