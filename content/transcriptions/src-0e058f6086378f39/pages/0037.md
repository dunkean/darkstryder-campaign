## Other Ground Forces

While Fest and Mantooine's Rebel organizationsreceivethemostattention,therearecountless other ground forces on the other worlds of Atrivis Sector.Some were deliberately set up by the Alliance,while others spontaneously arose and thenjoined up with theAlliance.While none oftheother unitsare as largeor prominent,they make important contributions to the war effort. The actions of each organization varies due to local circumstances,but they often perform small guerilla actions,from sabotage to raids on small outposts,to acts of "random violence" which accomplish their political or military objective without drawing undue attention to the existence of the Rebel organization itself.
