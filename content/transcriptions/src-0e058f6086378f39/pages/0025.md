## Line Admirals

Theline of battleis the main unit of maneuver in the Fleet.The battle line consists of four elements.At the core are the ships of the line, usually heavy cruisers.These are surrounded byclose support vessels,frigates and corvettes. In turn, those are surrounded by picket craft, fast-attack and patrol ships.Finally, the battle line is guarded by one or more wings of starfighters.

For more details on starships and the battle line,see Chapter Four,"Combat Starships.
