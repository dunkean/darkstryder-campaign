## Battle of Tiems

## The Situation:1300 Hours

The Imperial force,consisting of four regiments of infantry,attacks the Alliance force of three regiments of infantry.

## The Attack:1330 Hours

![Image](../images/image_000001_54541a455313fcd60af07125f4394631831ad7721cf98300fbf2c6e5bb16d538.png)

## The Trap

The Rebel front line is manned by only a single regiment.Two regiments have taken up defensive positions well to the rear of the line. The Rebels in the forest and behind the river have orders to stand firm:the Rebels in the center gap have orders to fall back when strongly attacked.

![Image](../images/image_000002_bda85c9ffe6424d2356b0116543f6910420369bf866e355c47b52c984d996e6a.png)

The Imperial forces attack:1st and 3rd Regiments probe the Alliance lines,pinning the Alliance forces in position.2nd Regiment assaults the weakest part of the line without river or forest cover.4th Regiment stands ready to exploit a breakthrough

![Image](../images/image_000003_0d4dfc82a6d5da33d89f56f58a57edb0597a1870fc0d8b11a37d152218d3cc4d.png)

## The Execution

When the Imperials attack the Rebels in the center flee to the town and dig in. The Imperial 2nd and 4th Regiments follow up.The two hidden Rebel regiments attack,hitting the Imperials in the flanks and rear,surrounding and nearly annihilating them
