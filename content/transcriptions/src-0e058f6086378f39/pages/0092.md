## The Mobile Defense

Under most circumstances,you will not be defending a specific point or line with the hope of holding onto it.As discussed above,the enemy can almost always eventually bring overwhelming force to bear against you.You usually go on the defensive because you haven't got enough power to attack,or to buy time for your soldiers,supply trains and headquarters units to retreat and escape.

"Here,as in all of your military endeavors,do not match your strength against the enemy's. Hit him where he is weak,make him attack you where you are strong. Give up ground when necessary;the enemy's forward progress may give you the opportunity to launch a devastating counterattack.
