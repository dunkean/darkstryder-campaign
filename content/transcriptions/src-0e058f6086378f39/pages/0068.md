Combat starfighters are one of the Alliance's primarytools for denyingcontrolofspaceto the Empire.As our main fleet must avoid combat except where absolutely necessary,starfighters are virtually the only forces in daily contact with the enemy.Roughly 35 percent of the Alliance's military budget is dedicated to the creation and maintenance of starfighters.
