## The Rebel Infantryman

The job of the infantry soldier,or dirtsider, has changed very little throughout history.His jobis totake and hold the ground.His is theleast glamorous job in warfare -starfighter jocks and spies get most of the glory, while he does most of the dying.

To the Alliance strategists, there is nothing more flexible, practical or dangerous than a well-trained,reasonably-armed trooper,willing and able to operate alone or in small groups. Thelmperialsrelyuponspecializedheavyequipmentthe Alliance counters with its best asset the bravery and intelligence of its soldiers.

Though his job has changed little over the centuries,the environment in which he must accomplish his job has changed enormouslyand for him,changed much for the worse.Huge terrible machines of war roam the battlefield, virtually invulnerable to his weapons.Supersonic airspeeders scream overhead,killing him before he knows they are there.Laser artillery bombard him from kilometers awayor from orbit. Yet,despite these awesome technological advancements in the "art" of war most specifically designed to kill him, the soldier is still king of the battlefield and the cornerstone of the Alliance's ground combat doctrine.
