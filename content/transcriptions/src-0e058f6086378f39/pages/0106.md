## Above-Ground Sensors

TheAlliancegenerallyemploysthreetypes ofabove ground sensors at its bases and outposts.Each type has its advantages and its weaknesses.Rebel military experts have found that the best coverage is provided by combining all three sensor types.

Focused uni-directional sensor dishes maintain a tight,focused beam.The technology used is similar to a starship's dedicated energy receptor DER. This type of sensor ls excellent for relaying detailed, specificinformationona previously detected target. It is almost completely useless as an early detection sensor because it covers so little territory.
