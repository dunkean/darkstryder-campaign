## lasterBook

Most rule systems are good for

a trip to one world. But MasterBook is a doorway to multiple universes. Inside you'll find all the basics you need to bring a roleplaying setting to life. Whether you're exploring the pyramids with Indiana Jones, trailing a shapeshifter gunman in Bloodshadows, or creating a world of your own, MasterBook is the key that lets you set your imagination free.
