## Honoghr.

Very little of the planet Honoghr remains fit for habitation. That they have avoided extinction illustrates the determination of the Noghri people.Honoghr,whilenever abountifulplanet, is now little more than a brown, dust-caked wasteland.

Thesmall section of arable lands that remain, the Clean Land, is where the Noghri have been forced to settle.Times are lean, and the traditionally antagonistic clans have been forced to abide by a truce to survive.The city of Nystao and a number of small villages are nestled in the Clean Land.o

NowthattheNoghriareawareoftheEmpire's treachery, the cleanup effort of Honoghr has begun in earnest (with assistance from the New Republic).Judging by their success in the Hidden Valley, there is a glimmer of hope for the near-dead world,though the road to Honoghr's salvation will be long, indeed.
