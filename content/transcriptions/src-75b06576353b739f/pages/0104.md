## Clawbird

Type: Avian Predator DEXTERITY ID Dodge4D+2,flight 4D PERCEPTION ID STRENGTH 1D Special Abilities:

Poison Spines: Clawbird poisonous spines do STR+1 damage and inject a slow-acting poison into a target. (the victim must roll Strength or stamina to resist the poison).The intensity of the poison increases by+1Dfor each hour,up to a maximum of 6Dthe poison can be counteracted by medicines found in any medpac.When rolling for the poison,if the poison beats the character's roll by 0-3 points,the character is stunned (as per page 62 of Star Wars, Second Edition).If the poison beats the character by 4-9 points,the character is paralyzed and is incapable of voluntary movement.If the roll beats the character's resistance roll by 10 or more points, the character becomes mortally wounded, and if the roll beats the character by 15 or more points, the character dies.
