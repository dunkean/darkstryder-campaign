## A-wing Slash (Optional Rule)

In game terms,the A-wing slash requires two elements of starfighters. The first element may consist of any starfighters.The second element must consist of fighters that are faster than the attacking enemy ships.

A central commander directing the assault is preferred, though a starfighter pilot can order one.If a starfighter pilot attempts an A-wing slash, he or she suffersfromacombinedactionspenalty:flyingastarfighter and coordinating a tricky assault maneuver is not easy.
