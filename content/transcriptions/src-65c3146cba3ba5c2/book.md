# Sergeant Brophar Tofarain

## Repulsorpool Chief / Head Mechanic, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Gamesmaster character, played by [Baz McAlister](credits.html#bazatron)

 

![Brophar](images/0cc513d75bd3799103fb6726266048b392fde30c9ebb081ce3dd3f1f075283d0.jpg)

"Besides, cap' - with me you also get an expert shuttle pilot. And a dash' fine shuttle."

 

Brophar Tofarain was enjoying the latter years of his life running his own repair facility in Kal'Shebbol's starport until the New Republic stepped in. His repair hangars and machine shops were a casualty of the fighting that forced the former Imperial governor, Moff Kentor Sarne, to flee Kal'Shebbol. A mistargeted concussion missile landed right in the middle of Tofarain's repair bay, obliterating his storehouse of spare parts, most of his machinery, and several small starships and repulsorlifts he was repairing. Luckily, Tofarain and his crews had already taken shelter from the attack in a local tavern down the street.

 

So, when the New Republic began recruiting a crew for the *FarStar*, Tofarain was near the front of the line. He demanded some sort of compensation for the loss of his repair business. And if the New Republic wouldn't compensate him with credits, he insisted that the *FarStar* take him along on its journey since he had nowhere else to go. Offering his services as transport chief and shuttle pilot, Tofarain also ensured his place aboard the *FarStar* by 'lending' the New Republic his personal shuttle, the *Muvon*, for transport duties. Fortunately it had been docked far from his ill-fated repair facility at the time of the New Republic's orbital bombardment.

 

Tofarain had done his fair share of travelling when he was younger. He gained his military experience - and the rank of sergeant, which he's carried around ever since - serving with a planetary militia in the Mid-Rim, then travelled the space lanes as a starship mechanic for many years. Tofarain eventually arrived on Kal'Shebbol after earning a small fortune in the free-trader and smuggling markets. He invested his credits in his repair bays, fixing ships for passing traders and later handling some repairs for Moff Sarne.

 

Brophar Tofarain's greatest goal is to see as much of the galaxy as he can before he takes that Final Jump - and maybe make enough credits on the way to settle down on some nice quiet world and retire for good. Tofarain's so concerned with seeing new worlds that he insists he's the only one qualified to pilot the *Muvon*. He claims it's because he's modified it so much that he's the only one who knows how to handle it properly, but it's mostly an excuse to get off his repair duties, see some new terrain, get involved in some action and maybe find some opportunity for fortune.

 

Along with the Wookiee Lofryyhn, Tofarain maintains the *FarStar's* starfighters, support ships and ground vehicles, and provides some technical support for the ship's ground teams. He looks very much like the stereotypical mechanic - he's pudgy and gruff and his work coveralls are always covered in grime and sweat. He's a pushy individual, but he's also very practical. Tofarain is convinced he's the best mechanic to work on the *FarStar's *support ships and fighters, and he often insists on rolling his sleeves up and doing major repair jobs himself. He's not satisfied trusting others to do the job correctly, and he often finds fault in others' work. He's always giving unsolicited advice - and not always just on repair jobs. Since he feels the New Republic owes him for destroying his repair bays on Kal'Shebbol, Tofarain often doles out his free advice on the bridge. He feels he knows exactly how missions should be run (point-by-point), and what kind of strategy the command crew should be following in any given situation. While most crew members respect his advice on technical matters, they tend to try to ignore his loud proclamations about how other areas of the FarStar's mission should be handled.

Lieutenant Page and Syla Tors walked together through the Sorbiss Valley shipyards. It was now the morning after the attack, and as they neared the imposing silhouette of the newly-christened FarStar, Syla was explaining the status of the Corvette's hangar and maintenance decks to Page. "... and since the hangar was designed for TIE Interceptors, we'll only be able to fit in five X-wings. Good news is, with the docking tubes we can dock up to nine more externally. Tofarain checked over the support equipment and says it's fully functional."

 

Page focused on the civilian standing by the FarStar, hollering instructions and advice to the repair crew working on the ship's docking ramp. Brophar Tofarain had latched onto Syla Tors yesterday in the damaged starport, loudly demanding reparations for his property which had been damaged in the attack. Syla had thus brought Brophar here to the Sorbiss Valley and put him to work.

 

"Tofarain!" Page hailed him. "Tors here tells me you checked out the hangar."

 

"A-OK, Lieutenant," the mechanic said. "'Course, it's still got some work left to be done on it. Whole ship does. 'S a mess."

 

"I know," Page sighed. "Question is, can Lofryyhn keep the systems up and running while that work gets done?"

 

"By hisself?" Tofarain snorted. "Not a chance. Sorry, Wook', but not a chance." He lifted a finger, cocking his head to the side. "But the Wook' and me - well, now, that's another story. Besides-" he thumped his chest and pointed his finger at Page "- with me, you also get an expert shuttle pilot. And a dash' fine shuttle. The Muvon."

 

Page looked at Syla, lifting his eyebrows in question. "I saw his shuttle at the starport. It looked at least flyable," she confirmed.

 

"It's a mother-beaut'ful shuttle," Tofarain protested in a tone of wounded pride. "Got lots of mod'fications - put 'em in myself. Flies like a dream. Hire me along, and it comes for free with the package."

 

"I'll take the offer under advisement," said Page dryly ...
