Characters in engineering should be similarly engrossed in repairing the engines. Those player characters that do well during the attacks soon find themselves facing more mutineers, as the gamemaster characters fall victim to the escapees' tactics.

Player characters in other areas may hear word of the mutiny, but by the time they arrive at the cee-cee or engineering, it is too late. The blast doors are sealed and the gravity fluctuating. As the adventure progresses, the characters must constantly contend with ship's erratic systems and sudden jolts as the rift storm buffets the drifting ship. Gamemasters should use the storm to give the characters a sense of impeding doom. They must succeed before the storm destroys the ship. Not only does the storm give them a rough ride, but affects the ship's systems throughout the adventure.
