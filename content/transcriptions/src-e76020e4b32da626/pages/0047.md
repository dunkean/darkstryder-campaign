Once in control of the FarStar's life support system, the mutineers quickly adjust engineering's air supply to incapacitate the crew there. The escapees also use the gravity field generators to defend against counter attacks. Tactics include rapidly switching between zerog and heavy gravity, changing field orientation and setting areas to high gravity. However, the gravitational system has sustained damage in the storm and is not functioning correctly in all areas.
