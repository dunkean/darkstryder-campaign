Minimum Damage

---

**STAR WARS RPG** 

**Mimimum Damage Rules**

In conjunction with The SW RPG 2nd Ed. R&E Scaling
rules

The Scaling in SW RPG is lacking realism when going from a
higher scale to a lower scale. A Laser Cannon of 1D Speeder scale, does as much as a
HoldOut blaster to a Character. To compensate for this with minimum damage rules. Every
weapon, when going down scale, should have a minimum damage. If, after calculating the
damage using the Scaling rules in SW RPG 2nd Ed. R&E, the damage is less than the
minimum damage for that situation, then use the minimum damage.
