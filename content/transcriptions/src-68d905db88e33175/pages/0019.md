## The Empire

Imperial lackeys come in all shapes and sizes.Not all of them wear stormtrooper armor and stomp around in walkers.Occasionally the Empire requires someone with specialized skills:bounty hunters,outlaw techs and even smugglers.Imperials can be employers, too. Look what the Empire's done for the bounty hunting business.While they won't always admit it, the Emperor's servants often hire outsiders better suited to enforcing their will. High Imperial officials use smugglers to sneak cargoes, personnel and data through hostile areas,past rival political factions and into the hands of less-than-savory allies. Sometimes this transport work is for official Imperial business, but it's more frequently for their own hidden agendas.
