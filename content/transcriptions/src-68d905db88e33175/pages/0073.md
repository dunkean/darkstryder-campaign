## Locals

Not everyone you know has to have under worldconnections andcriminalexpertise.Many times the average citizen can be a helpful con tact,especially if you spend a lot of time in the area he works or lives in.Business owners do favors for their loyal customers.Service workers don't mind offering extra assistance for big tippers.A kind word and a few credits can turn any street beggar into a faithful lackey for a day.The colonists you just delivered cargo to might offer you shelter when you're in trouble. Theminor bureaucrat you flattered might overlook your next official misdemeanor.As longas you're not an arrogant nerf-head, most folks are willing to help you out for a smile and a handful of credits.
