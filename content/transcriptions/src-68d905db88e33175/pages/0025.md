## Blood Razors Swoop Gang

Biivren is a minor industrial world in the Expansion Region controlled by the Empire. The spaceport, business and residential quarters are surrounded by kilometers of automated factories,chemical plants and refineries, all of which are gradually contaminating the planet's water supply.Despite frequent Imperial patrols,an entire society of vagrants, petty criminals and refugees has flourished among the greasy alleys which honeycomb Biivren's industrial sector.

While Imperial and local corporate authorities official govern this area,any real order and justice comes from a local swoop gang. The Blood Razors may have atough name,but their chief purpose is to provide for the industrial sector's outcast inhabitants.Theytraffic black market goods and re-distribute materials stolen from Imperial forces.Those within the factory area are under their protection.Most intruders are subject to their harassment. The gang frequently forays fromits territoryto raid Imperial supply depots,attack corporate convoys and annoy starport officials. Like any swoop gang,the members spend lots of time hanging out,antagonizing the authorities,and zooming around the starport's industrial areas.
