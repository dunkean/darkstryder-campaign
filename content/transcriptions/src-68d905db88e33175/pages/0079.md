## How Do You Meet These Nasty People?

Sooner or later you're going to make rivals and enemies.Rivals just happen.They're the natural opposition in a very competitive line of work.You meet them while unloading cargo in a docking bay,when boasting of your last smuggling run in the local bar,or flying around the starport traffic pattern with total disregard for safety.Rivals will come out of the background soon enough.

Making adversaries requires a little more workbut not much.You make enemies in the normal course of business.These guys don't want you to succeed for whatever reason: they're Imperials,you owe them money,you embarrassed them,you shot their best friend.
