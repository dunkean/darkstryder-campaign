## The JAN Camp

Episode Objective:Infiltrate the JAN camp and obtain more information on the JAN strike on the torpedo sphere.

Obstacles:JAN operatives,Imperial strike team.

The JAN camp is located in an underground complex on the isolated coast a few hundred kilometers from Mrlsst Academy.The characters can travel there by flitter or over land via a landspeeder. Note that the land gets quite marshy near the coast, and there are no roads leading anywhere near the camp.
