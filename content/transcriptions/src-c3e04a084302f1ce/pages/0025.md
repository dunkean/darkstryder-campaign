The Meeting. The characters may have occasion to meet a noble for some reason (to exchange information, give or receive some object,etc.),and the Gardens makes an excellent backdrop for this sort of activity.This option works best if the characters can blend in with nobles,either because they are nobles themselves, high-ranking outsiders, the servants ofnobles,or havefakeidentitiesallowing them to pass for one of the above.

Such interactions make the Gardens an excellent setting to introduce the characters to new gamemaster characters,plot threads,and new information.Roleplaying and social skills will likely be more important in the Gardens than combat.Herearetwoideas onincorporating the Gardens in your adventures:
