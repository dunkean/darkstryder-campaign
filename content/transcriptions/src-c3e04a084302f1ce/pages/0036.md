## Crew Quarters

Atypicalcrew quartersis,in reality,acollection of five rooms.The central circular chamber serves as a mess hall, complete with a droid-staffed kitchen and a food storage room. Four short corridors lead to crew living quarters.Theseareas are dividedinto four separate rooms with a common room in the middle.A collection of five of these sprawls is termed a Crew Cluster.
