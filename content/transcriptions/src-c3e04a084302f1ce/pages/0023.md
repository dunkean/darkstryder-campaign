## The Alaphoe Gardens

Alaphoe Gardens is a private park located on the gentle rolling hills of Lalos Island on Procopia.The Gardens,as the seaside park is known, is owned by House Cadriaan. Lady Bathos of Cadriaan holds her immensely popular parties here during capital season.
