## House Mecetti

Lady Damara Decrilla represents House Mecetti at the Vor-cal.Damara clearly doesn't want to be here.She's a delicate yet ruthless woman who prefers her creature comforts to mucking around in a dank forest.Rumor has it that she's here because she got on the bad side of amore powerful noble who knew how much she'd hate this, and sent her here to show her her place.

She is here to bag an animal (by any means) and leave as soon as possible.She resists performing foolish "male-bonding" pre-hunt festivities, and until she's on the first shuttle departing,she's rude, curt, and generally in a bad mood, especially to members of houses that aren't allied with Mecetti.
