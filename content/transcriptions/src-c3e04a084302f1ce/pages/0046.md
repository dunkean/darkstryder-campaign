Capsule:LordVaskelis a very tall man with short brown hair, which is pulled back into a short

ponytail.Hehaslongsideburns and roguish good looks. He favors shortsleeved shirts, allowing room for his bulky arms. Except in extremely formal situations, Lord Vaskel keeps the upper buttons of his shirt unbuttoned,revealing a hairy chest.

Lord Vaskel is a highranking lord in House Melantha who sits on the HighLord'sprivy council. He is the mastermind of the sphere conspiracy, and its primary architect. He has carefully manipulated the other conspirators into doing the dirty work,leaving himself clean enough to deny all, shouldit come down to it.

Lord Vaskel is a boisterous,cocky individual, but he can back most of his boasts with action.He is a crack shot with a blaster, and an accomplished big game hunter. To increase the thrill of the hunt,he often hunts with archaic guns or bows.A skillful storyteller, Lord Vaskel can spin an exciting yarn out of any hunt,no matter how mundane.

Although he sits on High Lord Bal's privy council, Lord Vaskel spends most of his time hunting.He enjoys hunting on Vycinyth's forest moon,but he also travels the galaxy for other hunting locales.
