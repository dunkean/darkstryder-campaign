**Wild Die House Rule

Getting a '1' on the Wild Die**

---

Despite the rulebooks insistants that the GM
    decide whether the '1' on the Wild die counts as a complication, or a "take away the
    highest" or nothing, my players were convinced that they had a one-in-six chance of
    having something bad happen to them.  I have been forced, partially by their
    complaining, but also because I felt it was logical, to enstate the foillowing rules into
    my game for purposes of a '1' on the Wild Die.

When a
    '1' is rolled, roll another die. Follow the chart to determine what happens.

| **Roll** | **What happens...** |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | *"Really bad"*
<br>        Complication |
| 2 | Complication |
| 3-5 | Take away the wild die and the
<br>        highest die rolled |
| 6 | Nothing |
