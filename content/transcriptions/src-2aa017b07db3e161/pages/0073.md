## Population

Note:Populationsin frontier and wilderness sections of space tend to be lower.Gamemasters are encouraged to apply a modifier of-1 to the population roll.

## Roll ID.

- 1 Population is 1-999
- 4-5 Population is in the millions
- 2-3 Population is in the thousands
- 6 Population is in the billions.

Roll 1D to determine whether in the singles, tens or hundreds ofthousands,millions or billions.If the population of a planet is in either the tens or hundreds of billions, the system must either be multi-planetary in its inhabitants or have a sapient population that takes up very little space.

- 1-2:Population is in singles.
- 5-6:Population is in hundreds.
- 3-4:Population is in tens.
