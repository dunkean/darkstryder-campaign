## The Marketplace

The Varno Market contains treasures and trinkets that find their way there from all sections of the galaxy.Most scouts wandering through the area barter the interesting trash they've picked up on their travels here and there for food and fuel. Nearly anything imaginable can be found in one of the murky booths, if one is lucky and patient.

The only point ofinterest on the whole planet is the Varno Market,which surrounds the area of the spaceport.Since alcoholic beverages are illegal on Vengler, nearly all the business on the planet is transacted in this one square kilometer area.
