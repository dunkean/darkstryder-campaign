## Benefits of being a Corporate Scout

Pay for a corporate scout is, of course, dependent on the company.The average monthly pay is around 1,000 credits per month with bonuses paid for especially valuable discoveries.A discovery bonus can be as high as .01% of the market value of a planet.

The benefits of becoming a corporate scout are many and varied.The pay scale for a beginning scout is generally very high, the risks are moderate to low and,in many cases, equipment and a ship are provided.

The company maintains a large scouting branch which provides the company with the raw materials and manufacturing sites that the expanding companyneeds to continueits reign astopproducer in the field.
