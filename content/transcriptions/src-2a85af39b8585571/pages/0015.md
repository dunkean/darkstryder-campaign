## Rewards

Aftertheattack,Ambassador Makinawill thank the characters for their help.If they were able to capture Byrg alive,he will inform them that Byrg will stand trial on various charges.If the pouch Byrg was carrying is not destroyed, the Ambassador will be very happy indeed, as it provides information onthestatus ofthe Imperialforcesin the sector. It also has a list of potential safe havens for Byrg, and Makina is considering raiding several of them to help bring down the Empire in this sector.
