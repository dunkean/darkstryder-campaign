# Major Breslin Drake

## Tactical Advisor/Counterintelligence Specialist, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Played by [Jeff ‘Jefferson’ Rodger](credits.html#jeff)

 

![Drake](images/4e496bf3c821200da0ed04f52a344ecc0856b9ddb2ec63f9837295c7a130dec5.jpg)

"Come to think of it, this plan didn’t work on Horst VII, either."

 

Breslin Drake is a tall, heavily muscled human with short, black hair cut in a military style. He has a number of scars criscrossing his face and hands, and always wears a heavy blaster pistol at his side, prominently displayed on his right hip. Once at home in rumpled business suits, Drake has re-adapted to the uniform of the New Republic with ease.

 

Drake is extremely agile, and a skilled combatant in both armed and unarmed combat. His mental abilities rival his physical prowess, too. He has a self-deprecating sense of humour (he claims he owes allegiance solely to the ‘state of inebriation’, but he possesses a ruthless streak and an air of grim determination that make him an extremely dangerous opponent - or a very useful ally. 

 

Drake was a New Republic Major whose life’s work was compiling a network of intelligence gatherers and collation centres known as the ‘Archive System’. Drake had served for a long time, and had been decorated many times for bravery as both commando and intelligence agent. He was then transferred at his own request to an administrative detail, in order to compile his network of spies. While developing the network he discovered that an Imperial agent had penetrated one of the hidden installations, jeopardising the security of the remaining Archive stations. In his investigation of this disaster, Drake came to suspect that the Imperial agent was a former contact, a shadowy operative and master of disguise known only to Drake as ‘The Wraith’. The two had never met before or after The Wraith’s defection to the Empire.

 

Drake petitioned his superior, Admiral Drayson, for permission to return to the field and track down the Wraith, who was now on the run. Permission was refused. In a moment of rash misjudgement, enraged at the damage the traitor had done to the New Republic and to his colleagues, Major Drake stole a shuttle and set out in pursuit. He tracked the Wraith all the way to Kathol Sector, and discovered that he had gone to work for the local Moff, one Kentor Sarne.

For two years, Drake posed as a tech merchant, dealing from the shadowport Tanquilla Beach, where he made friends with a tech named Spang. He also travelled around the sector sniffing out the Wraith’s activities, discovered that the Wraith was serving Sarne as assassin and saboteur, and trailed him to various planets in the sector. Not once did their paths cross, nor did Drake glimpse his quarry, but he saw much of the Wraith’s handiwork - it seemed that Sarne had him engaged primarily in loyalty purges and political assassinations. The Wraith’s calling card was his wide variety of trademark poisons, many of which he obtained locally and all of which were quite deadly, killing instantaneously or allowing the target to waste away over a period of time.

 

One of the planets that the Wraith visited several times was Sebiris III, an isolated place inhabited by primitive aliens. Drake visited the aliens in an attempt to learn more about the Wraith, but only managed to learn that the Wraith went by the name of Petrivoor there before Drake was run off by the natives for violating their religious ground in his pursuit of the Wraith. Drake was forced to open fire on the natives as he withdrew to his ship, but the light and noise of his blasts was sufficient to scare them off with no casualties.

 

Then, several months ago, the Wraith went to ground. On his way back to the capital world of Kathol sector, Kal’Shebbol, to pick up the trail anew, Drake stopped over on the planet Pembric II, a notorious smugglers’ haven and world of ill repute. He was caught by one of the local crimelord/planetary governor’s pressgangs, and was forced to work for his organisation. This crimelord, a Corellian named Crev Bombaasa, recognised Drake’s potential and put him to work as manager of the successful bar, nightclub and casino, the  Thrusterburn Tapcafe. After a while, Drake figured, he could gather up enough money to make it off-planet and stay ahead of Bombaasa’s bounty men. He came to enjoy working there, actually - he was essentially his own boss, there were few worries and little danger due to the protection the club received from Bombaasa. In fact it was one of Bombaasa’s favourite gambling haunts, and the snivelling little man was often seen there, surrounded by muscular Jarell bodyguards. The Wraith faded to the back of Drake’s mind for a time, and he began to make the best of his situation and use the time to rest and recuperate from months of hunting.

 

But the shadow of the past hung over Drake like he could never have known. The news came through that the sector had fallen to New Republic forces, and Bombaasa began to order the planetary security to tighten. Word was that Moff Sarne was on the run out into deep space, past Pembric II and into the Kathol Outback. And then the *FarStar* arrived.

 

When a beautiful woman showed up in the club, asking for Drake by name, he was intrigued. When she said his old friend Spang the tech sent her, and started asking questions about where she could get in touch with the Keiffler brothers, computer slicers of ill repute, he was even more interested. During their discussion, both Drake and the woman twigged that the other was a New Republic Intel agent, due to their use of NRI slang. Drake volunteered to lead the woman, who revealed herself as Captain of the *FarStar*, Kaiya Adrimetrum, to one of the Keiffler brothers’ students, Gaelin, who also worked for Bombaasa.

 

Just then, they were summoned by Bombaasa, who knew Kaiya’s secret also. Bombaasa made to arrest her, and Kaiya coolly shot him between the eyes. All hell broke loose, and Drake was lucky to escape Pembric II with Kaiya and her crew, with the slicer Gaelin in tow. Drake’s chief bouncer, Drenn, also came with him off-planet.

 

After that, Drake signed on with the *FarStar’s* crew for the duration of their mission. After all, it was probable that the Wraith was with Sarne now, and the *FarStar* was chasing Sarne’s fleet. Since then, Drake has once again encountered the Wraith - Captain Adrimetrum was poisoned by a small dart concealed in one of her security droids, and fell sick. Drake recognised the Wraith’s handiwork, and suggested Sebiris III as a possible source of antidote. While there, the Wraith was recognised by one of the natives - the village headman said to Drenn, Drake’s bouncer, "You come back, Petrivoor, yes?".

 

The Wraith had been working with Drake all along, and he never knew! By a strange twist of fate, the Wraith had been sent to infiltrate Bombaasa’s operation and had worked his way in as a bouncer! He, too, had decided to leave Pembric II now the New Republic was in control of the sector, and planned to rejoin Sarne - luckily, he found a ship that would take him right to his boss! Well, now the Wraith’s cover was blown, and a furious Drake gave chase. The pursuit ended with Drenn/The Wraith stealing the *FarStar’s* shuttle from the surface, killing the pilot, and making a break for space. He was shot down by one of the *FarStar’s* X-Wings, but his body was never located in the wreckage of the *Muvon* shuttle. Drake still has his doubts...

 

For now, though, Drake will stay with the *FarStar* and help them get their difficult job done. He realises he can be very useful to them - he’s actually the highest-ranking crew member, but is content with his intelligence position and doesn’t want to throw his weight around. Besides, he feels he needs to do something to redeem himself in the eyes of the NRI after being AWOL for so long. Now the Wraith is gone he has to consider his future once more. And besides, if the Wraith escaped that wrecked shuttle, who knows? Maybe Drake’s nemesis will return to haunt him, or threaten the *FarStar...    *

 

Drake believes the ship to have a capable crew, but they are a little more diverse and disorganised than he is used to. The recent loss of Captain Adrimetrum came as a blow, because Drake got on well with her - perhaps she was even a romantic prospect. He has sworn a personal oath to rescue her. The new captain - well, Drake has glimpsed him briefly in sickbay, and hearsay has it that he’s capable enough, but Drake feels uncertain about trusting him fully. The rest of the crew are fine - Drake likes Vallens, the ex-commando; Lofryyhn, the Wookiee, and Kl’aal the Defel scout. He finds Jessa Dajus intriguing - she’s much more than she seems to be. He has little time for reg-bashers like Thyte, self-important loudmouths like Brophar Tofarain, or annoying moneygrabbers like Loh’Khar the Finder, but he finds the Twi’lek’s social skills to be worthy of study. The ship’s Doctor, Akanseh, is a comforting individual to Drake - a good, simple being in the heart of all this chaos.

Season Two Update:

 

***

Major Drake was crippled by friendly fire on one of the *FarStar’s* last missions in Kathol sector, and spent his last days in a repulsorchair, having lost the use of his legs. However, Drake’s feud with the Wraith finally ended. On the planet Demonsgate, the last planet but one in the galaxy, Drake finally caught up with the Wraith, who had been captured by the bounty hunter Zuckuss. Drake and the Wraith sat across from each other in a tent at twilight; a single lamp sat between them; the harsh Demonsgate winds scoured the planet outside. They mutually decided that the chase ended there, and Drake offered the Wraith a chance at single combat. The Wraith accepted, thinking he’d have an easy victory over the cripple. As the Wraith’s hands closed around Drake’s throat, Drake detonated the thermal detonators hidden in his repulsorchair. and the uncaring Demonsgate winds scattered their remains across the blasted planet’s surface…
