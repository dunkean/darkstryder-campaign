# Captain Kaiya Adrimetrum

## First Officer (later Commanding Officer), New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Played by [Malcolm Collie](credits.html#malcolm) in Season One

 

![Kiaya](images/87109fb951e98fc724d28dd3ae89f74057adb9e58eac7019e5d39b511af09dc7.jpg)

"You'll find the *FarStar'*s weaponry to be quite impressive. For a few seconds."

 

Kaiya Adrimetrum is a strong-willed and intense woman who has been moulded by events beyond her control. She has come to expect more of the same aboard the *FarStar.*

 

Kaiya originally began her fight against the Empire because she blamed the Empire for the destruction of her life. Her husband, Fargo, was killed by Imperial forces during the occupation of Siluria III. Kaiya organised her friends and relatives into a revolutionary cell, and led an attack on the palace of the Imperial governor of Siluria. She and her freedom fighters were recruited into the Rebel Alliance by the famed Jedi and scout Corwin Shelvay, who convinced them that their revolutionary efforts could have greater effects elsewhere.

 

Since then, Kaiya has been a warrior, plain and simple, fighting the Empire and struggling to protect those who fight beside her. She tends to develop strong personal ties with those in her commando teams, and while this has increased efficiency and teamwork, it has also made death a greater sorrow to bear. Kaiya served with several teams during the Rebel Alliance's fight against the Empire, and distinguished herself in combat so much that she was recently invited to join Page's elite commandos. Since joining Lieutenant Page she has earned his respect and trust, and gravitated to the rank of Sergeant.

 

Kaiya is an extremely serious woman in her early thirties, but in times of stress she displays a quirky and black sense of humour. Although she still retains her youthful appearance, with short-cut black hair, pale skin and chiselled, aristocratic features, her wide, deep brown eyes betray her battle-weary heart.

 

Kaiya is a born leader, the kind who inspires loyalty in her followers. She is an excellent complement to Captain Keleman Ciro's subdued command style. She is strong and firm, and knows when to unleash her aggressive nature to achieve her goals. Kaiya likes to negotiate from a position of strength, and is usually not aggressive unless she sees a need for such drastic action.

 

Watching out for the safety of her unit is paramount for Kaiya, and figures into many of her command decisions. She is protective of her crew in an almost motherly way; she feels the pain when they are hurt, and accepts the responsibilities and risks of sending her people into combat. However, like a mother defending her young, when she or her crew are placed in jeopardy or threatened, she can become a dangerous and terrifying opponent, and use any means necessary to complete her mission.

 

Kaiya maintains a facade of a cool, calm leader in the face of danger. Sometimes, however, the burden of being responsible for the lives of others digs deep into her heart and fires her sense of security and authority. Deep inside she feels she's no hero - she feels average and normal, like the loving wife she used to be on Siluria III - but she knows she has to be larger than life in front of the crew. Kaiya focuses her energies through her concern for her crew and her pursuit of the FarStar's objectives.

Kaiya has made many friends over the years, including the Wookiee technician on her former commando team, Lofryyhn. The two are very close, but rarely speak of their adventures together. After hearing of her friend's imprisonment on Moff Sarne's capital world of Kal'Shebbol, Kaiya requested that she be part of the task force sent in to liberate the planet, and in a typically personal gesture, she made sure she was one of the elite commandos sent to free the Moff's prisoners. Kaiya's position on the *FarStar* mission carries with it the rank of  Sergeant Major, and her posting as First Officer is a hefty promotion in its own right - nonetheless, Kaiya is used to the rigours of command due to her time as a resistance leader. 

 

***

Season One Update: 

 

***

Following the abduction of Captain Ciro on the mission to Kolatill, Kaiya was promoted to Captain and instructed by Governor Monjai to take command and see the mission through. She led the crew through trouble after trouble admirably well, letting her natural sense of justice make many of the more critical command decisions for her. At the end of season one, Kaiya and her crew located Sarne and his prisoner, Ciro – and Sarne gave Kaiya the opportunity to trade herself for Ciro. She jumped at the chance – after all, Ciro was much more capable of command than she was, in her opinion. Ciro told her he loved her as the trade was made, and the two shared a passionate kiss before Kaiya was led away. Thankfully, she was able to thwart Sarne’s double cross when she used one of Jessa’s command codes to turn off the tractor beam with which Sarne had ensnared the *FarStar,* allowing her people to escape.

 

***

Season Two Update: 

 

***

Kaiya escaped captivity as Sarne’s shuttle *Oubliette *crashed on a frontier world. Her fellow prisoner was a shapeshifter called Derris. When the *FarStar* rescued Kaiya and Derris. Kaiya, who had gotten used to the idea of loving Ciro, was heartbroken to discover that Ciro and Dajus were pursuing a relationship. Then, in a moment of relative calm, Derris attacked Dajus, stole a Darkstryder artifact from the *FarStar *and used it to travel back in time. Kaiya used a similar artifact to follow, at great risk to herself.

 

***

Season Three Update:

 

***

When the rest of the crew travel back in time, they discover that Kaiya has been there for a year already and is serving aboard the *Freedom’s Angel *(sister ship to the *Freedom’s Messenger*, which will later become the *FarStar*). Kaiya’s C.O. is none other than Colonel Obi-Wan Kenobi, and she is serving with Major Lofryyhn. Not only that, but she is also romancing a young pilot named Anakin Skywalker. Kaiya remains in the past when the FarStar leaves again, having found her niche.
