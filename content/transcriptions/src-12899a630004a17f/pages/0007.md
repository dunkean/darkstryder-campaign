## The Underground Cluster

(Systems just below Binaros: Minaros, Yog'H'toth and Ambrox)

## Number of Systems : 3

In a small region below Binaros, just "north east" of the Qektoth Confederation is 3 worlds that make up what I later labeled the Underground Cluster.  It contains three worlds used as HQ's for two major organizations in the Kathol Underground.  Because of Operation Chillweaver, I had to expand on Yog'H'Toth.  It is the HQ of Chuborro the Hutt (see document Chuborro.DOC )
