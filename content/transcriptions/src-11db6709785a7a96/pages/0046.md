Not long after the journey through hyper­ space, the characters reach the mottled brown and green moon of Nightsinger'sOrb. Most of the surface details are shrouded by a dense layer of clouds, but a landing beacon directs the characters to the only major structure on the moon: Hejaran Castle. The Castle is a veri­ table fortress, poised on the rim of a vast canyon. The sheer slopes of the canyon wall make any approach nearly impossible.
