## Latching onto Hamar's Ship

Thefinal step in the escape involves docking with Hamar'scontainer ship and escaping into hyperspace. The actual docking requires a Moderate starfighter piloting roll if the target ship is moving slowly. This difficulty increases to Difficult if the attempt is made during com­ bat. Failure results in 1D+2 damage to the star­ fighter, 2D+1 damage to the cargo ship, and an unsuccessful docking.
