## Episode Three

Hamar meets with the characters to explain his plan of attack on the target bacta transport.

Using Lamuir VII'smoon for cover, the char­ acters attack the transport with the Mantas. The transport is escorted by six TIE fighters. After dealing with the TIEs, they land the Man­ tas behind the cockpit but in front of the bacta holding modules. Using plasma cutters, the characters are to enter the transport, subdue any guards, and take control of the vessel before escaping to hyperspace and another rendezvous with Hamar.
