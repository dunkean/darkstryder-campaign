**Where did they go?  **

**Player **

**Russel Briggs**

- **Uta T'Cha,  Chief Droid Tech 

**Uta chose to travel along with Gorak and Slave to pick up the pieces of what was
        left of the Kathol underground.

- **Thanis Gul-Rah - Human Bounty Hunter

**Remained in the Kathol Sector, hunting down the remnants of Sarne's minions,
        Chuborro's allies, and anyone else that would bring a price.

- **Delmon , "Alien" Explorer

**Stayed on the Darkstryder world to help in the restoration of the world.  He
        discovered later that his species' origins are closely linked to the Rift Disaster and the
        Gate.

**Chris Fisher **

- **Kaiya Adrimetrum, Human New Republic Commander 

**Retired from the New Republic Military and ran and won a seat in the Republic
        Senate.  Became a powerful advocate against the Imperial warlords, and their reign.

- **Gunthar - Lost Alien 

**Remained beyond the Rift, helping Dajus and his people rebuild the Kathol world.

- **Dervious, Mandalorian Bounty Hunter.

**Left known space in search of his homeworld.  He talked of reuniting his
        people, and stopping a long war.

**Mike Goines **

- **Mjr. Kla'al, Defel Scout

**No one knows...

- **Unda Lagor, Aqualish Heavy Weapons Spec. 

**Remains in the NR military, always putting himself in the toughest campaigns,
        seeking to destroy all Imperial Warlords.  Played a significant role in the Thrawn
        campaign.

- **Trev Haynor,  Human Pilot

**Joined Gorjaye in her newly formed E-Wing squadron and fought in many battles
        against Imperial Warlords.

**Christian Hine**

- **Lt. Ranna Gorjaye, Wing Commander of the* Forsaken Stars

***Was given a E-Wing squadron, and now is engaged in many battles against
        Imperial Warlords.

- **Scoryn,  Security Deputy Chief

**Remained in the Kathol Sector and retired from all adventurous activity.  
        She was cleared of all charges she was wanted for.

- **Rep. Sir Thane B'Dorbeck of House Ik'Tal

**Given a seat in the Senate, representing the Houses of Kathol.

**Matt Holmquist **

- **Dr. Akenseh,  Mon Calamari Doctor

**The Doc remained on Q'Maere, where the New Republic established a facility to
        heal all of the areas' victims of Sarne's experiments.  Akenseh was  an integral
        part in discovering the techniques to remove the Darkstryder Implants from the Nuniok Dak
        Sullustans.

- **Lt. Jessa Dajus, Human Ex-Imp. "Shuttle pilot"

**Dajus returned to Shantuun with Hasslock and they both worked with their master
        to help bring back the Kathol Precursors.  Using knowledge gained from the Precursor
        archives, and technology supplied by the Yvarema and the Majvara, they were able transfer
        the energies stored in the Well of Souls to cloned organic bodies for the precursors.
          She was also given highest honors with House Shador.

- **Edly "Pot-shot" Fasalk,  Gunner 

**Remained in New Republic service, as Thyte's First officer on the NRS *RedBlaze.*

- **Sooni Nubera, Sullustan Pilot/Scout 

**Remained in the Kathol Sector, serving as Doctor Akenseh's pilot and scout on
        Q'Maere, while he worked on freeing the surviving  Nuniok Dak Sullustans from their
        Darkstryder bonds.  Once they were cured, he returned to his homeworld to
        re-establish the Noble Houses.

**Chris Jarrett **

- **Sgt. Brophar Tofarain, Human Starship Mech

**Remained behind in the Kathol Sector after turning down a New Republic position
        on the NRS RedBlaze.  He acquired a new ship, and became a major political figure
        with the Free Traders of Kathol.  He also formed a similar organization for the Minos
        Cluster.

- **Brandis Turgah, Droid Tech

**After being vindicated of any wrong doing on the *Farstar*, and being
        freed from Bothan service, he remained in the Kathol Sector and surrounding areas, taking
        advantage of the new markets opening up there.

- **SLAVE,  Le-Vo Law Enforcement

**Joined up with Gorak on his quest to recover the pieces of the Kathol
        Underground.

**John Reavis **

- **Lt. Darryn Thyte, Human Bridge Officer

**Was given his own command (and a new cybernetic arm) on board the Nebulon-A
        Assault Frigate NRS *RedBlaze*.

- **Krudar, Duros Gunner

**Joined the crew of the *RedBlaze*

- **Cobb Unser, Human Pilot

**He and his sister remained in the Kathol Sector to join the Free Traders
        Alliance.  Was given honorary position in the Noble House Brakia.

- **Samarion, [Kalvessan Starwolf](../starwolv.htm)
        Scout/Warrior 

**Returned to his "homeworld", somewhere in unknown space, to "stop
        a war before it gets started"

**Jennifer Spencer**

- **Analia, Rodian Arms Dealer

**After finding her ship of Uukaablis, she remained in the Kathol Sector and
        surrounding areas as part of the Free Traders Alliance.

- ***Mavia Tanatus of House Ik'Tal, Human
        Female Noble House Liaison to the Farstar

***Joined up with the Night Terrors as a Intel and covert operations
        specialist.

**Neil Spicer **

- **Gorak Khzam, Rodian Sec. Officer

**Remained in the Kathol Sector to "pick up the pieces" of the Kathol
        Underground", and start a new era of free business in the Kathol Sector. He and
        Slave, along with Uta, have great adventures as Kathol's secrets continue to emerge.

- **Dr. Genna Seedar, Field Medic

**Joined the NRS Redblaze as her Chief Medical Officer.

- **Dr. Raso Lao Hasslock, Human Field Scientist 

**Remained in the Kathol Sector with Dajus, helping to revive the Kathol Precursor
        civilization.

**Ed Stokes **

- **[The Night Terrors, NR Commando
        team](DSNightTerrors.htm)

**Returned to the Core to get reassigned to a new challenge... somewhere else.

- **Bifzik, Verpine Tech 

**Bifzik returned to the Core to work for the New Republic on cleaning up the
        Imperials "black projects".
