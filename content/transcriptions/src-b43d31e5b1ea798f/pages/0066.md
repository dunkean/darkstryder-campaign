## Chapter Nine Swoop Gangs

Swoops are among the most dangerous vehicles available to civilians. A good swoop pilot needs to have daring, amazing reflexes and almost no fear of death. In recent years, swoop gangs have risen to prominence in outlying areas;increasingly,they are compared to the most fearsomepirate gangs.Whilenot all swoop gangs are alike, most have a callous disregard for life, property and the rule of law: they take what they want when theywant,and have caused countless civilian casualties.
