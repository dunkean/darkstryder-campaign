## Ahnjai Rahmma

His muscular build makes him quite intimidating; his sharp fangs and ferocious appearance arethoseofa born hunter and predator.Because of his jaw structure,speaking Basic is very difficult for him.However,if he speaks slowly,he can produce simple words (his species' native language,Hras'kkk'rarr,isaverysophisticatedcombination of growls,snarls and other sounds that are easy for his species to make).Often,a slow growl with a mumbled,"Kneel ...now,and a flexing ofhis taut muscles is morethan enoughto bring a potential combatant to his knees.

He generally prefers to remain silent, answering Tremayne only when necessary "As...you ..growl.wishLord..His species uses a combination of body language and gestures for communication, but he refuses to use this form of communication as an expression of his involuntary servitude to Tremayne.
