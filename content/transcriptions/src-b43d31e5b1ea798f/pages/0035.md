## Torel Vorne's Organ Donation Policy

Torel Vorne has a "well-run" and efficient organ donation scheme,which impoverished traderssometimesfind wisetodonateto.TorelVorne is willing to forgive bad debts for a small contribution.The rates for organs range from perhaps a few hundred credits (for seemingly unimportant organs) to several thousand credits for vital and non-duplicated organs, like hearts and stomachs.

A contribution to this scheme can yield a credit up to 25,000 credits.When the character undergoes the surgery,roll damage to see how careful Vorne's surgeon was.
