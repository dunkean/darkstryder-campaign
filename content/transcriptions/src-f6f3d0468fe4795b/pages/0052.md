## SCALE CHARTS

Die Caps: If a die roll is above the number, count it as if it was the number on the chart.

Example: if the die cap is 3 and the die roll is 5, it is considered a 3.

A "\_" means the item cannot shoot at the target in question.

When rolling wild dice and character points on the scale charts, if a 6 is rolled, count it as the die cap, but also roll the die again.
