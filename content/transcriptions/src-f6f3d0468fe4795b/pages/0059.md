## INJURY EFFECTS

Stunned:-1D for that round and next round (per stun)

Wounded: -1D. Incapacitated: Fall unconscious for 10D minutes. Mortally Wounded: Roll 2D after each round; if roll is less than number of rounds since mortally wounded, character dies.
