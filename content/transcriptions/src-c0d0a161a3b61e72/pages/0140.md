Yoda, the Jedi Master, is a mysterious individual whose origin is lost in the distant past.For over 800 years, this small, wizened, green being has trained young Jedi in the use of the Force, that power which binds all things together.Since the fall of the Jedi, he lives on a foggy, swampy planet in the Dagobah system, hidden from all but a few.
