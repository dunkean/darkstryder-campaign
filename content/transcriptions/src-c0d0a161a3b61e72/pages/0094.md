## Tauntauns

Hoth is the sixth planet in the system of the same name.Though it is covered by snow and ice.it serves as home to several forms of animal life.One such is the Tauntaun. Tauntauns are large,bipedal, oxygenbreathing herbivores.Thick.gray fur insulates them from conditions of their frigid,wind-whipped world. Friendly and easily domesticated, tauntauns have muscular hind legs that terminate in tridactyl feet with hooked claws. Large curved horns protrude from the sides of their llama-like heads.
