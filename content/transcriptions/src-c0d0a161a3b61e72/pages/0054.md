## Protocol Droids

Protocol droids are usually humanoid in shape, constructed to blend as smoothly as possible into Human society.Their primary function is in the areas of etiquette and translation,making them extremely useful aids for diplomats,traders,and heads of state. Protocol droids are well versed in forms of ceremony and socialcustoms observed byvarious cultures and speciesthroughoutthegalaxy.Theyareprogrammed to deal with and advise their masters in the decorum of a wide variety of cultures.

Cybot Galactica's 3PO protocol droids commonly serve as aides to diplomats, traders, and heads of state.
