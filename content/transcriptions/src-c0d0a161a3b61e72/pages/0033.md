Design flaws plague every high-tech construction, and the Corvette is no exception. Its main flaw appears when the ship is used for military applications: the principle solar collector and stabilizer fin, which is located dorsally amidships, is especially vulnerable to damage from attack.This small, curved panel is relatively non-vital, controlling only ship maneuverability within planetary atmospheres and collecting solar power for secondary systems and backup power cells.However,initialshock waves from direct heavy-weapons hits to the fin can sometimes cause severe vibrations and heat build-up in the main reactor and engine housings, located directly below the stabilizer.Once this occurs,all systems must be shut down or the vessel risks reactor explosion.There are many recorded accounts of smaller vessels crippling pursuing pirate Corvettes by repeatedly hitting the stabilizer fin.

To correct this flaw,some Corvettes have ben retrofitted with heavier fins and have upgraded the shieldingto the dorsal area.Ifa captain cannot afford such upgrading, he usually refocuses his existing shields to protect the fin.This isn't as good as a total upgrade, but it can keep the Corvette in a battle long enough for its speed and maneuverability to come into play.

Corellian Corvettes have served well in many interstellarfleets.Whileslowly beingreplaced bynewer, fancier ships, vessels of this class have a well-de served reputation for reliability and utility throughout the Empire, whether serving military,consular, piracy, or smuggling duty.
