- 12 TIE interceptors

12 TIE bombers (replacing TIE/gt models)

- 72 Starfighters*
- *TIEs only does not include shuttles, launches, and other craft.

## One Wing on Each Star Destroyer

The wing includes by definition starfighters, pilots all support personnel and equipment.

All wing personnel report to the wing commander through a chain of command separate from the rest of the Star Destroyer's crew.

The wing commander reports to the ship's captain.
