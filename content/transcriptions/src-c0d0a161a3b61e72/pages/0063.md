## Cloud Cars

Cloud cars are twin-pod atmospheric flying vehicles which have both repulsorlifts and ion engines. The unique double-pod design provides pilot and passenger separate compartments,connected by a boom-mounted ion power plant.Maximum cruising speed can exceed 1,500 kilometers per hour;maximum altitude can extend to low orbit.

The major manufacturer of cloud car vehicles is Bespin Motors.They make a complete line of plea sure craft.personal transports, air taxis,and patrol

Cloud cars are employed to patrol airspace, guide incoming craft, and spot-check landing transports.They are unsuitable for military applications.

## Sail Barges

Sail barges are huge antigravity vessels used to cross large,relatively flat surfaces.Using repulsorlift engines,some sail barges can travel up to 100 kilometers per hour and cruise at a maximum ceiling of 10 meters.Sail barges are also equipped with massive sails (hence the name) that can propel the giant craft by wind power,greatlyreducingthe energy drawn by the repulsorlift generators.In this mode they can reach speeds of 30 kilometers per hour.

Sail barges serve as luxury vessels and touring craft over seas of sand,water,or ice.
