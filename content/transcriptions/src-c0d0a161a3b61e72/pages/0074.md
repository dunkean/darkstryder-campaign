## Gamorreans

Gamorreans are green-skinned,porcine creatures noted for great strength and savage brutality.A mature male stands approximately 1.8 meters tall and can weigh in excess of 100 kilos;Gamorreans have pig-like snoutsjowlssmall horns,and tusks.Their raw strength and cultural backwardness make them perfect mercenaries and menial laborers.

The Gamorreans understand most alien tongues, but the structure of their vocal apparatus prevents them from speaking clearly in any but their native language. To any species unfamiliar with this language, Gamorrese sounds like a string of grunts, oinks,and squeals.But it is, in fact, a complex and diverse form of communication well-suited to its porcine creators.

Gamorr is a pleasant planet with varied terrain ranging from frozen plains to deciduous forests.But, in spite of the pleasant ecology,Gamorreanhistoryis singularly marked by constant war.Simply, Gamorreans love to pound and hack and slash.
