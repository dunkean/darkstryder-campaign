Gorak's Mission Logs

+++Mission Log

+++New Republic Starship *FarStar*

+++Dateline 43:4:5

+++Personal Log Security Chief Gorak Khzam

 

*

++Week 1 Day 1

*

 

What a job! Captain Keleman Ciro is fresh to the big chair, about two of the crew have even seen a ship this size before and the crew have negligible security value at all, I have never seen such a varied bunch of no-hopers, smugglers, arms traders, ex-imperials, not-so-ex-imperials and the odd new republicer in my life and Ciro has hired me the duty of maintaining the ships security!

 

I accepted the post because any route off of Kal’Shebbol was a good one but I may just have bitten off more than I can chew.

 

I hope Ciro isn’t going to be pussy-footed about his security, if he wants me to do the job he had better expect to allow me to get on with it. I quite like the guy but he is going to need more of an air of command and soon if he hopes to keep this bunch together!

 

**

Initial Security Assessments

**

 

**

- Jessa Dajus

** - awarded the new republic rank of Lieutenant on account of her ‘betrayed’ sob story. She was betrayed all right but she’s fallen from much higher than Ciro realises - Sarne’s right hand woman no less, a Colonel by official rank but somewhat more highly rated by other ranks. She must be after personal revenge on Sarne, which will make her reckless and dangerous. Her own goals will be of greatest importance to her and this could compromise the crew’s security. Special monitoring required.
**

- Akanseh

** - a very interesting addition to the crew, he must be as desperate as me to leave this hole of a planet. Last I heard of him he was Sarne’s chief torturer. Could have been against his will of course but I would hate to assume anything about him, it takes a certain dangerous mind set to be able to do that sort of thing. His record lists him as having been on ‘a sabbatical tour’ but is more than a little hazy on the exact details about it, something about the Bothan Navy is all. He went direct form that to Sarne’s services, interesting... I don’t trust Bothans. His exact loyalties can not be guaranteed at this time. Monitoring required.
**

- Loh’khar

** - procurement officer. I’ve tangled with this one before. He is a swindler of the first order and will only be on board if he can personally profit from it which I am sure he can. If he is profiting then someone else must be losing out - cause and effect - I’d better keep close tabs in case our security is compromised by any of his scams. I’m sure he’d happily sell us out if the price was right. Special Monitoring Required.
**

- Ranna Gorjaye

** - Now here is a truly interesting mystery. Her record is spotless but brief and the items in her earlier days are clouded somewhat. It seems that she interned at the Imperial Flight School but left in disgust due to the high casualties amongst pilots due to the imperials finding it cheaper to have lots of useless ships rather than a few good ones. Lucky she got out of there alive. It could be a very clever ruse, but she has been loyal to the New Republic for years now, sufficiently long to be totally trusted. She may be a deep sleeper agent but her kill roster certainly shows no remaining love for imperials. Further background checks required. Monitoring required.
**

- Kaiya Adrimetrum

** - Stock New Republic Commando, from an Alliance world and an Alliance family, in the Alliance then New Republic from day one and in Ciro’s squad for most of that career. Security rating A1 - probably trustworthy. No monitoring required.
**

- Darryn Thyte

** - Stock New Republic Navy Officer, seems to have had an accident putting paid to his earlier career as an X-wing pilot, its left more than his arm twisted, he has some serious sour grapes directed at no one in particular. He is undoubtedly loyal to the New Republic and can safely be given an A2 - mostly trustworthy rating but his temper could lead to rash decisions and security breaches. Monitoring required.
**

- Kl’aal

** - This is one old friend I didn’t expect to see turn up but I rather fancy he is running from his latest career move, just like myself or Akanseh I suppose, but the nature of his career doubtless makes him all the more useful to Ciro but also all the more dangerous. He is a deadly fighting machine but the combined bounties on his head could buy out the remaining stocks of Alderaanian Whisky. There is even a New Republic warrant out on him although it is for the "person or persons unknown" involved in that particular incident. If the crew were to find out these details it could certainly be damaging, Kl’aal’s past makes him a danger to himself. He is an efficient killer but not a good keeper of secrets, promises or orders. Security rating B - should be excluded from higher level command meetings. Special monitoring required. Must make enquiries and check how many other crew members know of his past or the bounty on his head.
**

- Lofryyhn

** - This is rather unpleasant. He does not seem to have recognised me and he certainly wont know my name from the last time we met but I’m sure the slaver’s mark burned into his hide feels just as sore to his Wookiee sense of honour as the brand did when I applied it. Dajus, of course, knows of this but then she would, she signed a couple of the cheques for payment of goods supplied. New Republic man through and through, standard hatred of Imperials due to slave trade and a record of good deeds stretching back to the clone wars, seems to have come down in rank since then though, Security Rating B. Must remain out of his way just in case he remembers me. No security monitoring required.
**

- Scimitar

** - A smuggler turned Alliance Officer turned smuggler turned Alliance Officer turned smuggler turned New Republic Officer turned smugger turned free lance pilot for FarStar. I think its clear where his loyalties lie, with him and him alone. The FarStar must be useful to his glory seeking needs at the moment. Security Rating D - no information above confidential. Monitoring required.

 

That concludes the major contenders but there are several others I don’t like the look of and I haven’t had time to read even a tenth of the crew files. I’m going to have to take most of the New Republic ones on trust and work through the others as quickly as I can, starting with those who filled in the ‘background / experience’ section of the application form a little lightly.

 

The *FarStar* itself is certainly one huge security threat. Sarne is a twisted individual that loved intrigue and play at war as if it were a joke or game. This ship is booby trapped. No two ways about and short of stripping it to the bone and re-building it it’s going to take a miracle to find them all. We don’t have the time to strip and re-build and I don’t believe in miracles so I’ve taken a few early precautions:

 

- Ran all the major navigation programs through simulations in a Virtual Machine - ie convinced the computer that it was actually flying and ran it through its paces as if it was actually flying. No obvious bugs found there.

- Ran all the major security programs through simulations in the same way. No obvious bugs there but there is layers and layers of encrypted data that will take an age to wade through. If I know Sarne most of it will be useless waffle designed to be useless but time consuming to decode and the rest have auto-wipe routines if accessed. Must tread carefully hear.

- Ran all Damage Control and Life Support programs through simulations in the same way, again no obvious bugs.

- Investigated as far into the engine core as I could before we powered up, found and disarmed two devices but there are certainly more. Now the engines are under way further investigation will be extremely hazardous, just need to hope that I have covered all the bases for what might set them off by testing the computer core so thoroughly.

- Ripped as many panels off of the bridge as I could to check for devices, detonation or monitoring, found three which I removed and disarmed. Wiped the brains of the K4 class droids on the bridge and had Dann Drugah reprogram them to my explicit instructions.

- Wiped and reprogrammed from scratch the Security console on the bridge, allowing me to monitor all other bridge stations and lock out almost any terminal on the ship or deny any access code throughout the ship should someone be compromised.

- Ran exercises and drills with the security team. My initial conclusion here is that there is not nearly enough of us, hardly sufficient to cover the bridge at all times let alone anywhere else on the ship.

 

**

Conclusions

** about initial security survey:

 

There is a lot to watch and monitor and plenty more that I may not have taken account of although I have made provisions at the Security console for dealing with almost any set of circumstances, I could virtually run the ship from there.

 

This is going to be hell, I need to maintain a long arm relationship with all of the crew to avoid being compromised myself. Note - consider getting Scoryn or Shimmer to set up a monitoring device of me.

 

*

++Week 1

*

 

Blasted out of Kal’Shebbol with the ship working, managed to disarm a device that was activated as we left orbit without anyone even noticing the incident occurred. I’ll mention it to the Captain later but the crew are nervous enough without worrying about the ship sabotaging itself.

 

As expected there are *frictions* between certain members of the command crew. Good. As long as they don’t wholly trust each other, they can’t work together and will monitor each other to some degree or other making my job a little easier.

 

Incoming distress call from Kolatill, this will be the crew’s first test under pressure. Close monitoring required. Suspend Log.

 

Continue Log. That *fool* Ciro, our first damn mission and he explicitly ignored my security instructions and measures taking only one member of my staff on an idiotically conceived trip to an unsecured planets surface in the middle of massive upheaval. His own actions placed him in the belly of the *Retribution*, I rather hope Kaiya Adrimetrum will listen to me a bit more.

 

*

Retribution’s

* em-readings and hyperspace spike have been recorded and the computer will automatically notify if it is detected although I suspect recovering the captain will prove more tricky than merely finding that ship again. If I were Sarne I’d use him as bait, recovering the Captain is therefore not in the immediate interests of this novice crew.

 

++Week 2 Day 1

 

An interesting time has been had by one and all recently and my suspicions about certain individuals have been not only heightened but clarified to some degree. While under the influence of that artefact [see log 1432.9] some truly stunning behaviour became apparent. I was not, of course, immune to it but I note that my behaviour became - more like me - a sort of intense version of my personality that would manifest if I had no moral fibre at all. Which makes the changes in other peoples personalities a point of interest:

 

Gorjaye was like *a totally different person*, her mode of speech changed, virtually imperceptibly - more proper almost and her accent as well. Exposure to the artefact seems to have triggered something with in her, in the few days since I notice it in her eyes, a change for a fleeting moment then gone. I do not believe that Gorjaye is intentionally a threat to the crew but closer monitoring of the situation is required.

 

I have a lot of files still to wade through in the computer in this investigation but I’m certain I can find more details if I try hard.

 

Meanwhile Mr. Loh’khar, under the influence of the artefact, became even more possessive than usual. However I doubt he was still under it’s influence when he recorded the co-ordinates of the resting place of the artefact, he believes of course that no one knows that he did this but a simple alteration to the file he keeps the data in should scupper any notions he has of retrieving it. I will have to locate and analyse this data, I have noted on several occasions him taking co-ordinate notes now, I wonder what he is up to. Further monitoring required.

 

++Week 2 Day 2

 

Another unfortunate coincidence, my old friend Chillo Sanpona has shown up, Kiaya has started a mutual back scratching with him and his current boss Drednar. Neither is trustworthy further than I could comfortably throw a Bantha but hopefully his own blaster will take care of the problem directly. The ship’s security must come first, besides I had a few scores I wanted to settle anyway.

 

A curious incident this morning, an obvious thorn in Adrimetrum’s side showed up in the form of one Captain Travis Isquavel in the frigate *White Wolf*. He seems ready to put aside differences and start a mutual back-scratch with regard to hunting Sarne. Ironic, isn’t it that I find Kaiya’s association with Drednar and Chillo so distasteful when I think feel that this could be an asset! For security reasons I had better find out Isquavel’s side of his history with Kaiya anyway so I can anticipate her actions in tense situations where he is involved and a little access to some Imperial files relating to Gorjaye could be extremely useful. To that end I have made brief communication with the first officer of the *White Wolf* I await his reply.

 

++Week 2 Day 4

 

Gandle Ott, what a depressing place. If I ever see another mushroom again I swear I will kill someone with it.

 

++Week 2 Day 5

 

The *White Wolf* has bushwhacked us [see log 1563.6] and have captured Lt. Gorjaye. Casualties from the crew of the *FarStar* were low apart from her, she seemed easy to catch, and one thing I have learned about Gorjaye is that she is a good enough pilot to *not get captured*. I’m not sure if my suspicions should lie with her or the *White Wolf* so they are lying with both but I neither consider her lost nor dead, merely *not here*. I await developments on this one with great interest.

 

++Week 2 Day 7

 

I don’t often dream or dream so vividly, nor do I ever recall dreams which makes last night peculiar. I dreamt of two figures fighting, the woman I could see was Jessa and the person she was fighting (with a lightsaber no less!) was robed with his back to me. The robe flowed over his head in an odd manner, I am certain he was not entirely human, too short for a Mon-Cal and not big enough for a Gamorrean. They’re lightsabers clashed and as I saw them clash suddenly my hands were gripping the saber that was locked with Jessa’s. I saw naked fear in her eyes as they locked with mine.

 

The dream ended there. Most peculiar, I must ensure that my dislike for Jessa is not overriding my duty to ensure her security in future.

 

++Week 3 Day 2

 

An ex-imperial, Palme, has joined the crew. I like a man who is up front about his allegiances and everyone knows he was an Imperial so everyone will be watching him so I won’t have to. Of course he can not be trusted but at least he is more honest than Jessa about his position! Monitoring required.

 

++Week 3 Day 3

 

On route to Tanquilla Beach a booby trap was detonated [see log 1632.6]. I have been worried about this sort of problem since day one but have been unable to get Adrimetrum to take my advice and keep the ship still long enough to check it out thoroughly, she is anxious about Ciro and little else, she must be coaxed into being a little less reckless if she is to be expected to keep the ship secure.

 

We will now *have* to carry out this work on the *FarStar* on Tanquilla Beach, I should be careful what I wish for, the worst place we could possibly have it! This is not a pleasant place to be and not the sort of place that the New Republic is going to be particularly welcome. It is also a black hole for pulling some of the most undesirable people in the galaxy together, I met some of my closest friends here, which means bounty hunters and Kl’aal could thus present a problem. Careful monitoring of the situation is required.

 

++Week 4 Day 3

 

Repairs and searches are underway and so far we have only had one run in with bounty hunters which Kl’aal came out of alive and Loh’khar came out with a profit - one intact but erased droid in the form of 4-LOM.

 

I had a peculiar experience in the depths of the *FarStar*. Kaiya and I were exploring and discovered a box containing a lightsaber. It was an ancient looking weapon and must have been there for centuries but I seemed to recognise it and the sight of it stirred such a loathing in me for no apparent reason. Touching it, even through my space suit, felt like a mixture of ice, fire and blades I passed it to Kaiya but managed not to cry out. With it were some logs in a language we do not know but the signature name at the bottom of it was recognisable ‘Dajus’. I am deeply suspicious and do not like coincidences like these.

 

++Week 4 Day 4

 

I had another dream last night. A robed figure holding a lightsaber was facing me, his face shadowed and his hand extended. As I walked towards him I felt that I was walking towards myself. Then Jessa was coming up behind me, I mean him, and sliced with her lightsaber towards him catching his shoulder. He fell and I flew into a rage as I also felt pain in my shoulder. I snapped on a lightsaber that I did not know I had and caught her blade as it came down towards me again. Again our eyes locked and I saw her fear. I felt hatred rise with in me and I brought the saber round towards her as it caught her I awoke.

 

It took some time for me to calm down enough or for my heart to slow enough for me to record this log. I fear that I am not all right but I do not trust the Doctor sufficiently to seek treatment. I must simply monitor the situation.

 

Again I must monitor my dislike of Jessa if I am to maintain her security.

 

++Week 4 Day 5

 

Our rescue attempt on Gorjaye went too well. They wanted us to have her back. And I have again contacted the First Officer of the *White Wolf*, he was kind enough to admit that they had no further use for her, I shall meet with him again later to discuss the Gorjaye situation. As I suspected she is not just one person, she has been triggered by the *White Wolf* into resorting to another personality, Phaena Del’Gorjax, who’s disorientation is apparent but at least partly put on as she attempts to gain more information about her situation - clearly this sleeper personality has been triggered prematurely by something... perhaps that artefact or the treatment at the hands of the *White Wolf*.

 

++Week 5 Day 3

 

My meeting with the first officer of the *White Wolf* went well, I have the drugs required to restore - or at least stabilise - Gorjaye. He has answered some questions but raised more, Gorjaye’s implant is not any standard imperial technique but clearly the Del’Gorjax personality is Imperial loyal, also he has no record of her in the Imperial Navy so she must be from very high or be very secret but for *no record at all* to exist suggests that even the Del’Gorjax personality is not original either, erasing everything about a person is problematic but to simply not enter it in the first place is relatively easy. More investigation required.

 

++Week 6 Day 1

 

Repairs are concluded and we were straight in at the deep end with Sarne’s forces showing up. The crew performed well under this pressure and Adrimetrum overcame her *White Wolf* phobia sufficiently to make a joint retaliation feasible and successful. Perhaps she is finally putting the crew before personal grievance, her security value is therefore tightened.

 

++Week 6 Day 4

 

Pembric II has turned up a bunch of interesting people and two of our new acquisitions seem useful but I am not entirely satisfied with the third. Gaelin has, at least, proved useful in disarming several more booby traps but his methods are maniacal and unpredictable so he must be watched.

 

Drake’s record speaks for itself but as an intelligence officer it is his job not to reveal everything and he certainly has his own agenda with the Wraith. Security Rating : Under Investigation, to be assigned.

 

His bouncer, Drenn, seems to be a meatloaf and nothing more but I trust no one who has been ‘vouched for’ until such times as I can ‘vouch for’ them myself. Security Rating : Under Investigation, to be assigned.

 

++Week 6 Day 8

 

The Captain has been poisoned and we are on route to Sebiris to locate an antidote. I am in command of the ship, but after last night I am not certain I am in command of my faculties. I dreamt vividly of things flashing past me, faces, shapes, colours and that cloaked figure again. I don’t recall it all but I awoke not in my own quarters but in the mess hall having ordered a Coruscant Style Coffee. I can not stand Coruscant Style Coffee.

 

I am considering giving up command to Mr. Thyte as I am now critically aware that my own security value is under question.

 

My lightsaber is no where to be found and Halbret is on board, or at least a descendant of her.

 

The security of the ship is compromised, who poisoned Kaiya...?

 

The bouncer. He is the only person it could be by logic, he could not have been on board if Drake had tracked him to Pembric, the wraith must have come on board then... it has to be Drenn. I must warn the crew.

 

The Captain is disabled and I am in a position of power, he will forget this wraith for now and proceed as if nothing were wrong.

 

My shift on the bridge is due. End log.

 

++Week Eleven

 

It’s not me anymore, not always anyway. I drink a lot to try to suppress *him* but even in that state I am not wholly myself, I can move and act almost freely but not truly, I can not speak of it, *he* can prevent me and I can not act out with *his* knowledge. I am re-miss in all my duties, I must be relieved as my security value is nil. So much knowledge I can not impart.

 

Gorjaye may be restored but Phaena uses her body at night but I seem unable to speak of my fears to the Captain, prevented. I use my body as if I were a puppet but I am neither the puppet nor the puppet master but I hang in between. *He* sleeps occasionally and I awake but he has left me weak, too week to move. Pheana, the tracker reported her in the armoury. And she is not the original, I did a visual cross reference of her through the new republic files, one Tamara Barinn is either her twin sister or a clone - missing after the battle of Hoth.

 

He wishes violence and to leave the ship, it has no further purpose for him. He *needs* violence, it feeds him like a drug. The Doctor, he wants to confront the doctor and Kl’aal too. It’s easier for him to direct me at them because I feel anger towards them anyway. Why not Dajus though? He fears her I am certain.

 

He’s waking and I can hold no more liquor. I pray the crew may come out of what I will do now intact.
