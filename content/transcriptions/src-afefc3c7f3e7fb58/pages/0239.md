## Y-wing

The  Y-wing was  adopted  at  the  inception  of  the  Rebel Alliance, and due to its versatility remains popular despite its old, almost outdated, design. The ships are appreciated because parts for them are much more readily available than limited run ships, such as the A- and B-wing starfighters.
