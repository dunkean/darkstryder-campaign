## Rodians

Rodians are a humanoid species with multifaceted eyes, a tapered snout and deep green skin. They have a prominent ridge of spines running along the back of their skulls. Their fingers are  long, flexible  and  end in  suction cups. The  suction cups are not vestigial; they can pick up small objects in them and manipulate them quite well - though the suction is not very strong.

The Rodians greatly prize the "art" of bounty hunting, and many have found great success in this field throughout the galaxy.

Average Rodian. Dexterity  3D, Knowledge 1D+2, Mechanical 1D+2, Perception 2D, Strength 2D+2, Technical ID. Move: 10.
