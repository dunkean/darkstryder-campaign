## Skiffs

Skiffs  are  used  for  any  large  cargo  and  relatively  lowspeed and low-altitude transportation. They tend to be slow and clumsy, and can be easily operated, even by low intelligence labor droids.

![Image](../images/image_000001_09c6d3f85dc0fb8289731e0aeb47b4432e150be7b1c6f39151cbbb6b27a5e2ca.png)

Sail barges are massive vehicles that are long on luxury and short on practicality. They feature massive sails, as a throwback to the legendary days of early planetary explorers,  and  are covered with gaudy ornamentation. They are normally used as pleasure craft, vacation vessels and touring vehicles, able to cross any smooth, rolling, or flat surface. They are slow (they seldom travel faster than 100 kilometers per hour, and most have to struggle to reach that speed), and have a low flight ceiling (normally less than 10 meters).
