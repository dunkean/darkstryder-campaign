## Wyrn Otro, Galentro Heavy Works

The Galentro family of companies began many years ago as a hastily organized alliance of commercial exporters.They initially banded together to protect their trade routes from other competition and piracy. Since then, the terribly quiet Galentro Board has slowly increased the corporation's sphere of influence to include asteroidandgasmining,manufacturing,andotherheavy industries. Their corporate crest and slogan, "Galentro Works For All,"can readily be seen on sites across the Velcar Free Commerce Zone.

Wyrn Otro is the chief representative of Galentro Heavy Works in the Chamber of Order.
