## Rendezvous With Abya

Making contact with Sybegh Abya requires the Rebels to meet him at a tavern heis known to frequent,a place calledthe Trail's End.The baris located near the stockyardsin Besia Osurne,and is popular with breedtashherders andmeat packers.Theidentity signal consists of drumming out a particular pattern on the tabletop with one's fingers.When the characters make the signal, Abya leaves the bar and leads them to his home on the outskirts of town, where they can speak freely.
