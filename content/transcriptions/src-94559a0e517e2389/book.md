# Kl'aal

## Forward Scout, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Played by [Mark Boyle](credits.html#mark) in Seasons 1 and 2

 

![Klaal](images/a7d67d2dee119623fee2657c07ec70bb82cb94ff561f874c60e2395ec424a741.jpg)

"I gave my word. I'll keep it."

 

When the *FarStar *needs a good picture of what's on a planet's surface and a subtle way to gather information on its inhabitants without notice, the crew sends a scout team led by the Defel Kl'aal. The *FarStar*'s stoic forward scout is a mysterious individual. He literally emerged from the shadows on Kal'Shebbol to join the efforts of Lieutenant Page's commandos in the assault on Moff Kentor Sarne's headquarters, and later agreed to join the *FarStar* mission. He believes that the New Republic has plenty of work to be done in the Kathol sector, and he wants to help make a difference - or so Kl'aal claims. The *FarStar *crew couldn't pass up a Defel for a forward scout; his natural ability to blend into shadow is perfect for examining worlds and peoples without being seen. Few know of Kl'aal's true origins or his reasons for joining the New Republic expedition - and he wants to keep it that way.

 

Kl'aal mostly keeps to himself (when he allows others to see him), and excels at planetary scouting sorties where he can roam on his own, collecting information and pursuing his own personal agenda. Often, he'll disappear from a scouting party, returning when he feels he has completed his mission objectives. Kl'aal considers most of what he does on these personal sorties to be a private matter, not for discussion among the crew. He does not speak much, and he does not waste words on petty pleasantries. Kl'aal is very literal in his mission reports and does not embellish the truth - if he says something, he can be trusted not to exaggarate. Some crew members, however, do note a savage sense of satisfaction whenever Kl'aal returns from a sortie.

 

Kl'aal tends to avoid involving himself in shipboard matters on the *FarStar* as long as he can get down to a planet once every week or so. The petty politics and power plays of other beings do not interest him, and he is a staunch supporter of Captain Keleman Ciro and First officer Kaiya Adrimetrum - after all, they were part of the commando team which sent Moff Sarne on the run. Occasionally, the Rodian Gorak Khzam accompanies Kl'aal down to a planet once it's been thoroughly scouted - the two met a few months ago on a hunting expedition, and they enjoy sharing the thrill of the hunt. However, Kl'aal has noticed the Rodian's nervous disposition and assumes he has much to hide - if Kl'aal ever becomes party to the Rodian's secret he may rethink his attitude, but for now he does not really care about Gorak's past, and even considers him a friend to some extent. Unlike the Rodian, Kl'aal believes the hunt to be an honourable contest between two beings, predator and prey - he avoids ranged combat, and rarely carries a blaster.

 

Kl'aal has no true place of his own aboard the ship, but the crew generally assumes he hides wherever he can when not needed. He has an uncanny ability to appear at the appropriate time - or when others are speaking of him. Nobody among the *FarStar* crew has ever seen Kl'aal eat.

 

Kl'aal fled his homeworld of Af'El many years ago. He was accused of savagely murdering another Defel, his brother, in a crime of passion, and swore on his honour that he was innocent. But the evidence was not in his favour, and he was convicted of the crime, and further vilified among Defel for lying. He fled for his life, and has been on the run from his homeworld's reach ever since.

 

Since then, Kl'aal has seen himself as cursed. He fled from one planet to the next, initially pursued by Defel hunters, and later chased by local law agencies, the Empire, and numerous bounty hunters (including the infamous Zuckuss and 4-LOM) for various 'crimes' he committed during his flight. He found temporary sanctuary when his starship crashed on a remote planet; but, in the few months he spent fighting to survive in the wilds, he developed a taste for freshly killed meat.

 

Eventually. Kl'aal found himself on Kal'Shebbol. Moff Sarne's forces somehow tracked and captured him. But, rather than turning the Defel over to his pursuers, Sarne struck a deal - the Defel would serve as the Moff's personal assassin in exchange for feeding the Defel's unnatural eating habits and protecting him from his enemies. Kl'aal's survival instincts made the choice for him, although he felt his conscience would be forever stained. Moff Sarne set him to work eliminating potential threats to his power, and killing known members of the small resistance groups on Kal'Shebbol.

 

Eventually, Kl'aal defected to the New Republic and aided Page's commandos against Sarne. Now that Sarne has escaped, Kl'aal believes he must kill the Moff to restore his own personal honour and cleanse his conscience. The Defel is aware that several *FarStar* crew members served his former master, but he does not feel it would be honourable to speak against them. Jessa Dajus and Doctor Akanseh are unaware of Kl'aal's role in the Moff's rule of Kal'Shebbol - this was known only to Sarne's closest advisors.

Season Two Update: 

 

***

Kl’aal faced off against not only the likes of Zuckuss and 4-LOM in seasons one and two, but in season two he was confronted by his father, Vith’naal, who had decided to ensure that sentence was carried out on his own son. After a formal hearing in which Kl’aal’s shipmates acted as character witnesses for the Defel, Vith’naal decided to call off the bounty on his son, and let Kl’aal fulfil his usefulness to the FarStar. Kl’aal was later confronted with Zuckuss one last time, but this time Zuckuss was attempting to negotiate for peace with the FarStar crew. Kl’aal’s fellow crewmen attempted to stun him as he rushed headlong at Zuckuss, claws bared – but to no avail. Kl’aal died when Zuckuss’ gun went off in the struggle.

Feeding Time

* 

 

*

Moff Sarne stared down at Kl'aal from his transparisteel window high above the observation cell. The Defel hunched in the corner while the young woman cowered opposite, staring in wide-eyed fear at the brooding shadow. "Go ahead, wraith," Sarne taunted. "Eat your fill."

 

Kl'aal watched the young woman, backed against the far wall like a cornered animal. This one was too shocked to fight back - no doubt she had been subjected to Sarne's other 'entertainments'. Her grimy hair partially hid her face, hid tear-filled eyes and a quivering lip. Kl'aal found himself oddly attracted by something in her. Was it her helplessness? No, it was some warped camaraderie shared by two victims.

 

"Why are you doing this to me?" the woman screamed, asking Kl'aal's question for him.

 

"Why, to test my wraith, of course," Sarne replied. "You have served me faithfully up to now," he told Kl'aal, "but I believe in testing my servants every now and then - to ensure their loyalty."

 

"What are you talking about?" the woman cried.

 

"Silence, prisoner! I'm not speaking to you, but to the shadows."

 

The shadow stared back at Sarne, lip curling around a snarl. Kl'aal growled to himself, and the woman retreated to the far corner with a whimpering sob.

 

"Eat! Or you shall starve, wraith!"

 

This was not honourable. Kl'aal was a hunter, not a savage. This woman was no prey. This cell was no hunt. She was innocent, one who was not capable of defending herself. No, he would sit in the corner and deprive himself rather than give in to Sarne's twisted games. Sarne continued to bait Kl'aal into the night, but the Defel ignored the voices and the figure of Sarne, watching instead the figure of the woman cowering in the far corner...
