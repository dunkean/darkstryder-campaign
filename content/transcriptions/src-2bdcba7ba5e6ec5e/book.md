The FarStar crew in season 2, taken by one of the K4 Security droids on the FarStar's bridge. As you will note, there are some fresh faces in the command crew, filling the gaps left by betrayal and capture in season 1. Captain Ciro is back to lead them, Dajus now wears New Republic uniform and carries her grandfather's lightsaber, Vallens takes over as Security Chief and Defano as Starfighter Commander. Also joining the command crew is Drake, a former NRI Major specialising in counterintelligence.
