## Mos Eisley Inn 8.

This run-down building has the bare necessities and little else.For 10 credits a day(officially; the clerks try to haggle for more and pocket the excess), a customer gets a place to sleep,a shower, and access to public communicators. Those who arewillingto paythe extrafew credits get the subterranean rooms, which are dark and cramped,but cool;those who are tough bargainers often get stuck with the rooms on the upper floor, which are almost unbearably warm.
