It was impractical to execute or exile every criminal. When crime began affecting profits, the Authority realized it had to take action.

The Authority did what came naturally --- it added another layer of bureaucracy. It created the Detention Taskforce. After several years of study,the Taskforce concluded that the Authority needed a permanent penal facility for those who were "unreformable" -- those who could not adapt to a labor colony world or who would not change their ways..

The Detention Taskforce set about designing the "perfect" incarceration facility. Out on the edge of the galactic rim, construction began on a facility that would become known as Stars' End. Within that facility, the Authority would prove once and for all that it had absolute authority over the Corporate Sector.
