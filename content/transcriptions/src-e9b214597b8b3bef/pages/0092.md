## Capital Ships

Even in a section as controlled as the Corporate Sector, large capital combat starships are required to enforce the peace and control smugglers and pirates. However, when the Corporate Sector was first chartered, there wasn't much serious thought about providing any substantial sector navy. It was thought that pirate and Rebel activity in the region would be minimal. The Authority centered its naval plans on patrol ships with advanced scanners; almost as an afterthought, the Authority built a few carriers for IRD fighters.

What the Authority didn't take into account was the fabulous lure its wealth would have. Without the comforting power of Imperial cruisers and destroyers, the Corporate Sector had its work cut out for it. The Authority has slowly realigned its priorities, and is allocating more funding for the design and purchase of adequate capital ships. For thetime being, however, roughly two-thirds of the Picket Fleet has been assigned to patrolling major trade routes, leaving most of Corporate Sector space poorly defended.
