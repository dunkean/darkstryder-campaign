# Lieutenant Ranna Gorjaye

## Starfighter Squadron Commander, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Played by [Phil Rodger](credits.html#phil) in Season one.

 

![Gorjaye](images/63431574fb9b592ea55d69493e268de4870dc2c525d479508c3592ce1ac1d8c2.jpg)

"There's two ways to do this, pilot - my way, and the wrong way!"

 

The fiery-tempered red-haired beauty named Ranna Gorjaye is the flight commander for the *FarStar's* complement of starfighters - although at times one could mistake her for the *FarStar's* captain. She knows how to get the job done without wasting pilots or starfighters. While she might be abrasive and pushy at times, Ranna is a valued member of the *FarStar's *command team.

 

Ranna takes no guff from anyone - on the flight deck, in formation, and in combat, her word is law. Anyone who disagrees with her can sit out the next mission. She has supreme trust in her abilities and her pilots. Ranna is a hard taskmaster, however, and insists that her pilots pay close attention during briefings, hang on every instruction she hands out, and execute her orders perfectly and without question. This discipline keeps the *FarStar's* pilots alive - when it is flouted, people die in combat and starships are lost.

 

Ranna is the scourge of the docking bay. She's often found yelling out orders, bossing the techs around, and chewing out anyone who gets in the way. To her, having all craft in 'ready' status is the optimal state of operation - unfortunately, this is rarely the case. The docking hangar is her domain, and anyone trying to usurp her authority there, or even questioning it, is quickly and ruthlessly corrected.

 

Ranna Gorjaye was originally a squadron leader in the New Republic task force which drove Moff Kentor Sarne from Kal'Shebbol. She didn't choose to serve on the *FarStar*; she was assigned to the duty by Admiral Haagen Kolaador, in charge of the task force. Ranna is consequently very upset about being dumped on this "losers' mission". She doesn't trust the "hot-shot commandos" or the *FarStar* command team - she thinks they're going to get everyone killed and that they don't plan before they act. She firmly believes that the *FarStar's* situation would be better if she were in charge. Occasionally, Ranna visits the bridge to dole out her free advice and tactical know-how, and to get some retaliatory remarks in against Lieutenant Darryn Thyte, the disabled ex-pilot who hates her merely because she's on active duty and he isn't.

 

Ranna doesn't get along well with Captain Ciro or First officer Adrimetrum, although Captain Ciro's younger brother, Noell, has a good deal of faith in her, causing Captain Ciro to modify his views. Adrimetrum in particular has a hard time agreeing with Ranna - they often concur on the outcome, but argue over the finer points of achieving it. When barking out her free advice on the bridge, Ranna is often asked to leave - in more critical times, the captain often has her escorted away.

 

Unlike most of the *FarStar's* crew, Ranna makes sure her peers and underlings know about her colourful past. She often boasts of her years of starfighter service with the Rebel Alliance and the New Republic, quoting battles and actions nonchalantly to add authenticity to her authority. She flew as Grey 7 in the Battle of Endor, with Dasha Defano on her wing. Her greatest achievement - the one she mentions most often - is her training and graduation top of the class from the Raithal Academy's flight corps programme.

 

While Ranna brags about her past, a good deal of it remains in shadow. Ranna would never admit this, but she grew up on Salliche, in the Core Worlds, where she joined the Salliche Student Militia, a military training programme for young people. Here, she learned to fly Z-95 Headhunters in training and eventually won her scholarship to the Raithal Academy's flight school. After her graduation, only she knows what happened, but rumours abound among the crew that she was an elite TIE fighter pilot. Some stories have already circulated that she gained a good deal of her combat and survival training after being shot down - whether this happened when she was a 'TIE pilot' or a cadet at the academy is much debated. Most of her peers get the impression that she joined the Rebellion soon after she graduated, but her reasons for defecting remain unstated.

Season Two Update:

 

***

At the end of season one, Gorjaye was revealed as a sleeper personality, implanted in Sarne’s agent Phaena Del’Gorjax. Commander Del’Gorjax’s personality was then re-activated and she rejoined Sarne throughout season two, as an aggressor for the valiant FarStar crew. Fortunately in a later encounter, Dr. Akanseh was able to modify a subspace carrier wave to trigger the Gorjaye persona’s return, and she rescued the captive command crew.

Season Three Update: 

 

***

During the course of the second season, the crew discovered that neither Gorjaye nor Del’Gorjax was the original personality of the body they inhabited. Using Darkstryder technology, Moff Sarne mindwiped a young pilot named Tamara Barinn whom he captured at the Battle of Hoth. He implanted the personality of his dead lover, Phaena Del’Gorjax, into Tamara’s desirable body. Then, when Phaena’s talents became clear, he decided to use her as a sleeper spy in the Rebel Alliance – hence, he created the Gorjaye identity, persona and history. **See *Tamara Barinn* for more history on this character.**

Survival Class

Gorjaye was furious.

 

It was a simple training patrol gone all to hell because of an overconfident and underskilled wingman; the kid crowded her formation and clipped her fighter's wing. Both fighters went down, right in the middle of the Wilds, an overgrown jungle filled with swampy lowlands, insect swarms and bloodthirsty predators.

 

After the fighter stopped skidding and thumped into a tree, Gorjaye forced open the bent canopy before the kid could even unstrap. Ripping her flight helmet off, Gorjaye manually opened the hatch on Calson's fighter and dragged the disorientated trainee out. When he found his footing, he pushed Gorjaye away. "I can take care of myself," Calson spat.

 

"Listen, bud," Gorjaye sneered, "I'm not the idiot who crowded formation. If you'd followed my orders then we wouldn't be down here. And, if we're going to get out of the Wilds without some pack of mangy Corvaj ripping our livers out, you'll do exactly as I say, got it?"

Calson stepped back and brushed himself off. "I'm not taking orders form a woman anymore."

 

"Damn straight you are," Gorjaye spat back. "You're part of my command. I'm responsible for your sorry hide."

 

Calson huffed, then rummaged through his cockpit for his survival gear. Gorjaye pulled the directional transponder from her own gear bag and took some readings, checking them on a datapad map of the planet.

 

"I've got a small settlement seventeen klicks northwest," she said. "I suggest we start out now while we still have a few hours of light." She stowed her map and pulled the service blade from her pack.

 

"No way I'm going with you," Calson said. "I saw something back there when we were going down, and I'm going to check it out."

 

"You're coming with me, and that's an order," Gorjaye said.

 

"I'm relieving myself of your command," Calson sneered. "You're just going to get me killed out here." The junior officer slung his gear bag and began shooting a path through the underbrush with his blaster.

 

Gorjaye gave up. She wasn't going to let some rowdy runt endanger her safety. Cutting a path with her service blade, she headed northwest towards the settlement.

 

It wasn't long before she heard a distant human scream above the snarling of the pack of Corvaj...
