# Ensign Tamara Barinn

## Helm Officer, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Played by [Phil Rodger](credits.html#phil)

 

[![Tamara](images/c2c920099da17948cc8814a227983209b3e5be12796b542a5770c5a2ef22b0c1.gif)](images/tamarabig.gif)

 

"Leave that to Tamara."

 

Tamara Barinn was born twenty-six years ago on Salliche, in the Core Worlds to Ensor and Lora. She had one younger sister, Tanya, five years younger than her.  Her early childhood was difficult, but mostly happy. Her father was employed as a minor bureaucrat in the local Imperial Bureau of Ships and Services (BoSS), regulating and licensing transponder codes for starships. It was a tedious and mind-numbing job which her rather straight-laced father seemed to enjoy too much. Even so, there was not much room for advancement in his career and the family was not wealthy. 

 

Ensor Barinn, however, loved his eldest daughter very much and wanted to give her every break in life. For years he scrimped and saved, unbeknownst to the rest of his family, working in a thankless job which he pretended to like for their benefit. Eventually, he raised enough money to put his bright, beautiful daughter through the best University on Salliche. The sheltered Tamara’s eyes were opened to a universe of possibilities and wonders the likes of which she had never seen. In her first year, she discovered where her sexual preferences lay when she got a little too friendly with her room-mate, Tabs. At Tabs’ insistence, she joined the Salliche Student Militia, a pseudo-political military training programme for young people. This included pilot training in a mixture of old training Z-95 starfighters and shuttles.

 

In her second year at University, news filtered in from the Galaxywide Newsnets about the destruction of Alderaan. The nets said that the peaceful planet had been building and testing a new superweapon to use in an act of Rebellion against the Empire. Tabs had contacts, and told Tamara the truth – the true story of the Battle of Yavin, and the heroism of the Rebel Alliance. All this sounded rather grand and exciting to the young, impressionable girl, and of course she trusted her lover more than any Newsfeed. 

 

With new taxation laws, cessation of student grants and curfews in the streets of Salliche in the wake of this tragedy, the students took to demonstrating – peacefully of course. Thus, anti-Imperial sentiment was stirred up on Salliche, and the Imperial presence became stronger. After the sixth rally, Tabs did not come home. The whispers on campus were that she had been arrested by the Stormtroopers following the peaceful dispersal of the demo. However the morning newscasts had a different story to tell. They showed scenes of violence, with students and Imperial troopers clashing in the streets, and several troopers being taken down and possibly killed. "But that’s not what happened," thought Tamara. "I was there." The ‘casts had obviously been doctored and the ringleaders of the anti-Imperial movement arrested. To this day, Tamara has never found out what happened to her first – and only – love.

 

After these events, Tamara returned home to her family to recover. She admitted to her father that she had been at the demonstration, but he would not listen to the truth as she told it. He was almost completely indoctrinated by the Imperial way of thinking, and only saw a riot on the streets in which loyal soldiers had been wounded and killed by brutal, undisciplined youths who had no idea of the realities of politics. Ensor forbade his daughter to return to University, saying that there was an opening in his section at the BoSS and that he would send her application in the morning. There followed a blazing row, in which the truth about Tamara and Tabs’ relationship came out. Her father, close-minded traditionalist, was horrified at this "deviancy", and instantly threw his daughter out of the family home. 

 

Returning to University, Tamara discovered that her father had ceased payment of fees, and she lost her place there, too. She had only one place left to turn to – a place where she could fight the Empire, avenge the memory of her lover, and put her piloting skills to work. That place was the Rebel Alliance.

 

Using one of Tabs’ contacts, she was smuggled away from Salliche (her first time off-world) to the Rebel base on Derra IV. With her extensive pilot training, the base’s Commander Naara assigned her to flight ops on one of his Bulk Transport ships, where she also filled in as gunner sometimes. The transport’s captain, an Ithorian named Bleer, was a strict and authoritarian being who liked to run a tight ship. All his crew had to be immaculately turned out at all times, which did not sit well with the increasingly bohemian Tamara. She frequently reported for duty without her regulation Blast Helmet, and was put on charge after charge by the safety-conscious Bleer. Mid-trip from Bespin to Derra IV, Tamara and Bleer had a blazing argument about this piece of trivia, in which Bleer took off his helmet to demonstrate to Tamara the protection offered. He set his helmet down on the deck, and fired his pistol at it to show her that it could stop even a blaster bolt. Unfortunately, at that moment the transport was hailed by an Imperial customs frigate and instructed to prepare for boarding. Bleer rushed to the bridge to order his crew to run for it, as they were carrying illegal arms. Tamara took up her helm position, and plotted the course. As the ship made the jump to lightspeed, cutting across the frigate’s bow, the bridge was raked with turbolaser fire and a beam fell from the ceiling, striking both Bleer and Tamara. Bleer died instantly, his cranium crushed from the impact. Tamara lived to regret that day. Now, she constantly has her blast helmet to hand. 

 

After two years’ service in the Rebel Alliance, Ensign Tamara Barinn ended up at Hoth base during the critical evacuation as Vader’s fleet bore down upon the Rebels. Tamara’s transport was hit by an Ion Cannon, and was dead in space. Aside from the base’s general, Carlist Rieekan (who was badly wounded), she was the sole ranking officer on board. She successfully organised a defence team against boarding actions, and waited as the Battle of Hoth raged. Eventually, the transport was boarded, but not by Imperials. Instead, it was boarded by the opportunistic bounty hunters Zuckuss and 4-LOM, in search of easy bounty. Tamara struck a deal with the bounty hunters, offering payment and medical aid in return for evacuation aboard their ship for the wounded crewmembers. The last tangible memories that Tamara Barinn has are of Zuckuss’ ship departing the transport, and of waiting in the cold darkness with a few others, waiting for the inevitable boarding and capture by Vader’s men… 

 

The rest is a tangled, messy, dreamlike whirl of confused thoughts and feelings. Defiance, pain, fear, hope, and resolve gelled into one emotion… and then were gone. Tamara now awakes (five years later) in an unfamiliar place, surrounded by strangers – although the presence of 2-1B droids is familiar, and at first makes her believe she’s at Hoth. She dons a surgical gown and wanders up to the bridge of this unfamiliar starship to greet her hosts…

Season Three update:

 

***

Tamara, ever without a sense of belonging, was talked out of jumping ship by Mr. Thyte on many occasions. She bounced around the chain of command, sometimes serving under Jerrell, sometimes under Ciro, until, in the third season two-parter spectacular entitled ‘Fallen Star’, she gave up her life. The FarStar was called upon to defend Corellia from the Mandalorians during the Clone Wars, knowing that historically it was doomed to fall. As the FarStar went down in flames above Corellia, Tamara remained at the helm to the last, alone save for her faithful, devoted droid R2-D7. Tamara managed to steer the burning wreck away from any landmasses or population centres, and splashdown the hulk in the deep ocean. As the wreck of the FarStar sank, the hull buckled, and water began to pour into the crippled bridge, Makezh projected his presence to be with Tamara in her last moments. She gradually lost consciousness as the underwater darkness and pressure claimed both her and the noble starship…

 

And I wept for her. – Baz. (GM)
