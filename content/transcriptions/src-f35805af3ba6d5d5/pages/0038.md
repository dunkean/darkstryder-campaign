## Weaponry

Charactersuseeither their capitalshipweapon repair or starship weapon repairskill (depending upon the scale of the weapon) to improve their ship's weapons.

Tramp freighter captains have a number of worries-pirates, hostile aliens, unwary customs vessels-all of which can call for enhancing a ship's existing weaponry.

The cost is based on the original cost of the weapon, not the starship.
