## Escape Equipment

Enough escape pods for each crewmember and passenger on board a ship is required by Imperial law (military vessels are, of course, exempt from this rule).A standard freightersized escape pod seats eight passengers and has food and oxygen for two weeks.

Cost:1,200 credits.Weight:5 tons.

## Environmental Converters
