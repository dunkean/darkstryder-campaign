## Finding a Customer/Seller

To find a customer/seller, a trader must make a bureaucracy skill roll. The difficulty of the roll is dependent upon the scarcity of the product he wishes to buy,or the demand for the product he wishes to sell.Examine the"Buying/ SellingDifficultyChart'inthe"Charts and Tables" section.This lists the difficulty of finding either a cargo or a purchaser of a cargo,according to the supply/demand for the cargo.For example,if the trader wishes to find a cargo with a high supply, he must pass an Easy bureaucracy roll. If he wishes to sell an item for which there is a low demand, he must pass a Difficult bureaucracy roll.

## Bad Deals

A trader can always refuse to buy or sell at the gamemaster character's final price,but, if he does so,he must find a different person to deal with, and the difficulty of doing so increases by one difficulty level.

## How Much Can I Carry?
