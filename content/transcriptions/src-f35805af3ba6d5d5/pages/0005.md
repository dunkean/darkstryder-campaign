## Time Frame

This supplement is set in the classic Star Wars time frame,before the deaths of Darth Vader and the Emperor, shortly after the Battle of Yavin. During this period,there is still a highly effective and well-organized military present in Imperial space, Jabba the Hutt is still alive and operating and Boba Fett is still among the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy.Player characters should be reminded that these are dark times, indeed ...
