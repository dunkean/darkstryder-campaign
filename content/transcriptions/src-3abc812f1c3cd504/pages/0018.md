## Slicers

The criminal "slicer" is the galaxy's quintes­ sential computercriminal.Slicers are individuals who break into "unbreakable" computer sys­ tems. Once inside, they use accessed informa­ tion to manipulate financial arrangements, ob­ tain trade secrets for sale to the highest bidder, and even use personal information to blackmail wealthy citizens. Not all slicers, however, are motivated solely by the lure of easy credits. Manyengage in a more nefarious form of activity involvinginformationsabotage. In suchinstances, haVing breached the host computer's security systems, the slicerwill often wipe out files vital to the target's continuedoperation. In extreme case, slicers may even plant computer viruses which eliminate vital information long after the slicer has withdrawnfrom the system completely.
