## The Crime Cartel: The Mandroxans

One of the most profitable sources of illegal revenues today is spice. Control of the spice trade is the target of one of the most insidious criminal conspiracies in the Outer Rim: the Mandroxan alliance.

The name Mandroxan is derived from the three principle criminal organizations that make upthis cartel: the Manliss Enterprise, the Droxian Traders League, and theAnavillSmuggler'sGuild. All lie within the boundaries of the Portmoak sector (in fact, Mandroxan does a great deal of business with Torel Vorne on Reuss Vlll; see pages 33-35 of GalaxyGuide 9: Fragments from the Rim).
