The black market, or "invisible market" as it is often called, is a familiar criminal institution found on practically every populated planet. While few respectable citizens are aware of its existence, few in the criminal underworld fail to interact with this shadow economy on a regular basis. The invisible market is more than simply a place where illegal sales are conducted. For many, the market is a loosely knit community: a net­ work of people helping people, and never mind the legality of it all.
