The local bosses can easily gloss over that one. At the same time, Lorn can use Hutt sponsored agents to help settle some old scores.

At an entirely different level, Lorn has set about to win young hearts and minds by institut­ ing a mandatory series of classroom instructions aimed at glorifying his role as planetary saviour. Children are routinely taught that Lorn has their best interests at heart, and that he alone can keep the planet safe. To this end, the continued safety of the Andasalan people justifies whatevermeans GovernorLornmayuse.Whilesome parents may question the validity of such arguments, none dare do so in public.

In turning his native world into one, gigantic, criminal underground, Lorn may have made a serious mistake. He has failed to consider how the planet's citizens are being changed as well. Even a casual look at the personal ads found in Andasalan daily newsfiles suggests a community wholeheartedly embracing the criminal way of life. Forced to adapt as best they can, many Andasalans are acquiring the same ruthless men­ tality that put Lorn himself in power.
