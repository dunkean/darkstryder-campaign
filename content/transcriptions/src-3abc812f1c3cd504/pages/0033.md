## The Sabrin Connection

In the few short years the Sabrin Ring has been in operation, Lady Sabrin and her gang have managed to solidify an efficient criminal enter­ prise, largely undisturbed by law enforcement agents. Thatthis is so is due, in nosmall measure, to an influx of Hutt credits and labor shipped into the Sabrixin system. This labor is particularly useful in mining radioactive elements from abun­ dant veins found throughout the system. These ores are transported through various smuggler connections to mineral poor worlds in other systems. The credits obtained from these sales bring in a steady income.
