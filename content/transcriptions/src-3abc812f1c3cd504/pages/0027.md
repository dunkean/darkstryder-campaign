## Criminal Empire

Largest of all known criminal organizations is the criminal empire. These massive criminal unions strive to combine all of the above ele- ments into one grand criminal conspiracy. The Hutt crime empire which existed prior to Jabba's death was among the most famous of these em­ pires, but many others exist.

In their heyday, the Hutt clans controlled im­ mense resources stretching across large por­ tions of the galaxy. With Jabba's death, the em­ pire has disintegrated into the more traditional model of various Hull clans engaging in active "competition." Just how widespread the direct and covert influence of this particular species extends will be examined in Chapter Three, "The Hull Crime Empire."
