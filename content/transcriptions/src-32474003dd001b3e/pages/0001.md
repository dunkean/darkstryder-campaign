# Loh'khar the Finder

## Procurement Specialist and Independent Trader, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Played by [Oliver Oswald](credits.html#oliver)

![Lohkhar](../images/a58ff1667c37337b922d594670680fc7941d573ab4d3a5405c2705704543ba99.jpg)

 

"It's a talent. A skill, as it were. Not easily acquired ... but perhaps useful?"

 

Loh'khar is the *Farstar's* procurement specialist - anything the team needs, he can often scrounge up in a matter of minutes to a few hours, depending on the size and rarity of the material. The tall Twi'lek is intensely secretive about his methods and sources in obtaining equipment, and often explains himself by simply saying, "If you have to ask where I got it, you won't really want it."

 

Many years ago, Loh'khar escaped from the Twi'lek homeworld of Ryloth, apparently fleeing from a clan conspiracy working against him or so he claims. Through unknown means he acquired a light transport, and journeyed throughout the galaxy as a sort of travelling salesman, rivalling the infamous Quist 'Screwy' Rawsker himself, bringing odd goods from world to world and matching them with the perfect buyers. At times he settled down and worked his procurement wonders from a fixed base, often near a starport or a busy urban centre where entrepreneurs, hustlers, smugglers and bureaucrats craved illegal, rare or expensive items they couldn't obtain themselves. In each location, he established a tight network of spies, street urchins, thieves and 'buying agents' who helped him obtain what his clients wanted. In some cases, especially when he was travelling, Loh'khar did the work himself.

 

During his travels, Loh'khar traded some valuable information for three unhatched Turazza eggs. At the time, others thought that Loh'khar got the short end of the deal, but the eggs soon hatched, and the three Turazza bonded with the Twi'lek. Now the three short female reptiles, Rizzal, Vizzal and Nizzal, work for Loh'khar, running errands and collecting information.

 

Loh'khar uses a combination of high perception, bribery and extortion when trying to obtain items. Sometimes what his clients need is within his grasp, either through payment or thievery. Other times, he bargains with the complex network of the black market - using bribery and intimidation where necessary, and often trading items or information he has for materials he needs.

 

Loh'khar continued his business, profiting from the greater conflict created by the galactic civil war. Granted, some of his best customers were representatives of the Rebel Alliance - always in need of materiel - or the Empire, seeking shady ways to accomplish their military objectives. The Twi'lek played off both of them, betraying them only when it was profitable to do so.

 

With the rise of the New Republic, business has been slower, but not unprofitable. There was still much wealth to be gained in the Outer Rim Territories and in other places where the New Republic's bureaucratic grasp could be easily avoided. Loh'khar was running a brisk trade out of his light freighter when he made the mistake of stopping on Kal'Shebbol, hoping to assist the renegade Imperial Moff Sarne. Instead, Sarne commandeered Loh'khar's ship, stranding the Twi'lek on Kal'Shebbol. Loh'khar was grateful he wasn't imprisoned by Moff Sarne, and attempted to make the best of the situation. He set up shop in Kal'Shebbol's main starport, catering to the needs of the few traders who flew in and the desires of the innumerable, miserable creatures living beneath Sarne's iron boot-heel.

 

When Lieutenant Page's commandos blasted through Moff Sarne's palace, sending Sarne fleeing with his sector fleet, Loh'khar didn't much care. Empire, New Republic, same thing - there was still money to be made, even if the police uniforms had changed. Loh'khar's search for profit led him to investigate the *FarStar* venture. It took him two tries to make it aboard with the *FarStar *crew. At first, Lieutenant Page felt that he didn't have any viable skills; but, when the *FarStar* needed some heavy-duty turbolaser power capacitors within twenty-eight hours, Loh'khar obtained them within twenty-four. Nobody knows how he did it, but he assured the command crew that his diplomatic talents and his finesse at resource management could get him almost anything.

 

Loh'khar keeps tabs on everybody and everything. Perhaps it is his Twi'lek origins, or his many years in the procurement business, but he has a knack for making arrangements behind the scenes, sowing rumours to get others to do as he pleases, collecting dirt on some to blackmail them, and bribing others. However, all the while he seems to be the noble Loh'khar, procurement specialist, certainly incapable of subterfuge and intrigue.

 

Loh'khar cuts a mysterious profile. He often covers his grey tunic with a black, voluminous hooded cloak. This cloak is filled with pockets stuffed with datacards, pouches of this and bits of that, useful trade items, or information with which to blackmail others. Loh'khar often keeps the cloak's hood covering his head, keeping his shifty eyes in shadow. He hides most of his emotions behind a stoic, businesslike face.

Season Two Update:

 

***

After the defeat of Moff Sarne, during which Nizzal was tragically killed, Loh’Khar hitched a ride back to known space aboard the defecting ISD *Darkstryder *with her captain, Lokan Brannidge. Once there, Loh’khar reined in enough of his schemes and money making enterprises to make his fortune. Through sale of alien artifacts, secrets, and information about Kathol sector, and even the rights to an exclusive holodrama based on an ‘entirely fictional starship lost at the edge of space’, he makes enough money to set himself up as owner-operator of his own shadowport.

Business Meeting

* 

 

*

Loh'khar the Finder sat solemnly in the corner booth where his regular customers knew they could find him. In his arms he cradled three fist-sized eggs, each mottled with grey-green spots. He quietly cooed to them, soothing whatever was inside and calming them amidst the usual chatter and bad music which assaulted the patrons in Galliwig's Cantina.

 

A swarthy man in an apron - Galliwig himself - swaggered up to the booth. "Looks like ye got some bad end of yer latest deal there, Lookar," he drawled.

 

Loh'khar looked up from his seat, his eyes still shaded in the darkness of his cloak hood, but staring nonetheless right through Galliwig. "There is no 'bad end of the deal' in my book," the Twi'lek said softly. "'Bad' is a relative term, and I ensure that any deal in which I engage has the maximum profit reserved for my end."

 

Galliwig gave Loh'khar a perplexed look. "Tell ye what," he drawled. "I'll trade ye those eggs for twenny-five credits. Take 'em off yer hands real easy, like."

 

"I'll pass, thank you."

 

One of the eggs stirred within Loh'khar's grasp. It began shifting - something on the inside 

was pecking its way out the top of the egg. The other two eggs began moving too, and soon the tops of each egg were cracked.

 

A fanged red snout poked out of the lead egg, pushing pieces of shell away so that a little red-scaled head could pop out. Dark eyes peered from under the reptilian brows, looking at Loh'khar. The little creature began to make meeping sounds.

 

Soon the little reptile was joined by its two other siblings, all meeping and extending their short necks towards Loh'khar. As Galliwig continued to stare at the Twi'lek with that same perplexed look as before, Loh'khar lifted the three baby Turazza to his face, and gently touched their fanged snouts to his lips. "Welcome, my little friends," he said. "Welcome into my humble service; welcome to Loh'khar the Finder's little business..."
