## Episode Two: Disaster

The Rebels travel to Premier Provisions, only to find the entire crew dead, and the processing station in ruins, with every droid and computer malfunctioning. Duringtheirexplorations they find one functioning messenger droid, holovid record­ ings detailing the destruction of the base, and a group of intruders with unknown goals. The Rebels are diverted from chasing them bythe threat of the platform's utter destruction.
