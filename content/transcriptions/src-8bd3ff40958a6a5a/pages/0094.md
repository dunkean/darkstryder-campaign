## Oh, I'm Sorry. Was That Your Troop Transport?

Within the hangar are two Imperial troop trans­ ports and a group of eight Imperial Army troopers (see stats above) about to go out on patrol. They are in the process of firing up one of the transports and taking positions on the ship. If the characters rush in, they will have to combat the troopers, but if they wait two rounds, they will be able to take the other transport with no trouble.

Unfortunately, the second transport is here be­ cause it is not operating correctly. The mounted heavy blaster is nonfunctional and the right side repulsor lifts are only operating at 70 percent. Whatthis means in gameterms isthatthe repulsorlift operation difficulty for maneuvers is increased by one level.
