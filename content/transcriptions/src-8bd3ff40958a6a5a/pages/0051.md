## The Chase

The characters have loaded the virus into Gryseium'smain computer, so there is nothing left to do but leave. The Rebels should realize that staying to fight a long battle is as useless as it is dangerous. When the characters reach the door they entered through, they find that it is now locked and needs a Moderate security roll to force open.

Once out in the open again, the landing bay is exactly as they left it, except that there are five corporate security guards there waiting for them (use the statistics for corporate security guards given in EpisodeThree). The guards haveorders to stop the characters, killing them if necessary. They have no energy weapons, so once the characters are on board ship, they are safe (for the moment).
