This battle marked Tarrus as a hero to the Bitthavrian people, almost equal with Colonel Tavier himself.Because of his tremendous courage and loyalty to Tavier, he was awarded the Ransar Rhal Insignia, virtually guaranteeing his place as the next leader of m'Yalfor'ac Order.
