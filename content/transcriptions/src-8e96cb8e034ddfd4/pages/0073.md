Mlatar Thon Gra is one of the most skilled bladesmiths to have left his world.For more information on Togoria and the Togorians, see pages 86-88 of Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races, Second Edition.)

Only with a thorough knowledge of the use of the sc'rath can a smith understand what the blade should be, many Togorian smiths reason.
