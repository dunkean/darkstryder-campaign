## Guiteica Militia

The planetary defense force which the m'Yalfor'ac Order officially belongs to is known simply as the Guiteica Militia.The 16 different continents each contributehardware and troops to the Militia for defense, but the m'Yalfor'ac Order is clearly recognized as the leader of the Militia, as the headquarters are based on the I'Quar'ta continent of the Order,and most equipment and funding comes from m'Yalfor'ac accounts.In terms of beingpower,the m'Yalfor'ac Order represents over 80% oftheGuiteica Militia.

The equipment discussed below is indicative ofthetechnologyleveloftheentire Militia,though only the Order has all of these items. Each continent is responsible for the purchase or"acquisition' of its own equipment.
