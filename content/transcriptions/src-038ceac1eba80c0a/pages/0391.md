## ANTITHEFT COMLINK LOCATOR

Availability:

1

Type:

Traceable distress signaler

Cost:

100

Game Notes: A droid equipped with an antitheft comlink locator can send a distress signal to its master's comlink with no apparent activity by the droid. The distress signal broadcasts no information, but it can it be tracked. The signal continues to broadcast if the droid is deactivated, but it shuts down if the droid is destroyed.

##  ASTROMECH VOICE BOX

Game Notes: Allows and R-series droid to speak a specific language (language programs run from 25 to 100 credits), with adjustable pitch, intonation and accent. Installation requires a Moderate droid repair rolls and takes 15 minutes. Removal requires an Easy droid repair roll and 15 minutes; failure causes 3D stun damage to the droid and randomly eliminates one skill.
