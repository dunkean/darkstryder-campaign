Since then, you've lived the life of a fugitive. At times, you are   convinced   the   Empire,   and   Darth   Vader   in   particular,   is hunting   you   fiercely.   At   other   times,   you're   convinced   the Empire's decided you aren't worth the trouble. Still, you have the fondest memories of your master. And there's still a chance, no matter how slim, that the Rebellion can overthrow the Emperor and his minion Vader. Connection   With   Characters: You're   happy   to   serve   the Rebellion in any capacity. You'd gladly accept a brash pilot or another character as a student. You'd be eager to learn from the failed Jedi. You could easily have become friends with any of the player characters.
