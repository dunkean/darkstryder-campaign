Kathol Resurection Comunique

++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

TO: New Republic Intelligence team Omega;

      (Colonel Andrephan Stormcaller commanding).

FROM: General Airen Cracken, New Republic Intelligence.

REGARDING: The Kathol mission.

 

++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

 

Team:

 

The objectives for your mission to the Kathol system are outlined below. You will be allocated the modified Lambda-class shuttle *Inquisitor* for the duration of the mission, and this will be well-provisioned enough to see you safely to the Kathol system and back.

 

**

- Ascertain the fate of the NRS *FarStar.*

**

- The black box we retrieved from the Bothan Frigate *Boash’ua, *found drifting just outside the Kathol Rift, shows reports of a large, many-factioned space battle involving the Bothan Navy, the forces of Moff Sarne, and the *FarStar’s *fighter wing, Outrider Squadron. Other forces were also present. The *FarStar *herself, however, was not present, and many of the Outrider Squadron pilots’ names were not on the original roster. Find the survivors, if any, of our forces – and, if possible, the *FarStar *itself. Its C.O., Keleman Ciro, must be extensively debriefed. We at Intel don’t hold out much hope of the ship’s survival, I’m afraid.

 

**

- Ascertain the fate of Moff Kentor Sarne.

**

- Although we got much from the *Boash’ua’s *black box, it turned tail and ran just before the battle ended. We need to know if the *FarStar’s *original mission succeeded – we need to know that Moff Sarne is either in custody, or dead. If he is still at large, you have the authority to terminate.

 

**

- First Contact any unknown alien races still present.

**

- The black box also gave us several names of new alien races – the Sanhedrim, the Yvarans, and the Qektoth Confederation being three of the most prominent. We will be sending the protocol droid V-6PO with your team to handle any first contact that may take place with any members of these races. The New Republic is still finding its feet and we need as many planets on side as we can get.

 

**

- Recon and report on the area known as the Kathol Rift.

**

- A recent chart remote-downloaded by an anonymous trader (for an exorbitant fee) shows us much information on Kathol Sector, but the Kathol Rift is still an unknown quantity. Our scout ships have already reached the rift’s edge, and we have gained some information on the phenomenon. However, no New Republic vessel (save the *FarStar*) has yet tried to navigate through the rift. Contact with the planet Danoor, at the rift’s edge, suggests that only an exceptional navigator can pilot through the rift – to this end, we are adding the independent Zod Duer to your team’s roster for the mission. This Mon Cal will serve as pilot and navigator.

 

We have a lot riding on this mission, for such a small team to undertake. I am sure, however, that you are up to it. Good luck, and good hunting, people. I never had much time for superstitions, but may the Force, or whatever you believe in, be with you.

 

General Airen Cracken.
