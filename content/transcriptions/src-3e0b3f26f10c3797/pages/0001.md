Captain Jerrell is a career military officer; however, his career has been somewhat hampered by his political sensitivities over the last few years. V’halla was born to well-off parents on the Bothan homeworld, Bothawui, 62 years ago. His father was a wealthy business magnate, dealing mainly in security systems and military materiel. His life was mapped out for him – as the third son, it was expected of him to join the military officer corps as soon as he came of age. His marriage to Bhaimma was arranged by his parents when he was three years old, to cement a business deal between the heads of their two families. The marriage actually took place when both children were sixteen, and V’Halla has been with Bhaimma ever since. Their marriage was at first rocky and loveless, and they spent some years apart – but when both partners matured emotionally and physically, the marriage began to work. In fact, Bhaimma became the rock upon which V’Halla stood firm through all those bitter years of war…

 

When the Clone Wars ravaged the galaxy, the Bothan navy was forcibly drafted into the main Republic fleet, and so the twenty-five year old V’Halla Jerrell, one of the most promising rising stars of the Bothan navy, was given command of his own gunship, the *Prodigal.* Captain Jerrell was one of the youngest captains in the fleet, and he was fully expected to make full Admiral by the time he was thirty-five.

 

During the Clone Wars, he had his ship stolen and returned a few days later, inexplicably; also a lot of money was removed from his account. Further, he encountered a very strange Bothan indeed. This old man claimed to be a relative of his, the ‘black Bantha’ of the family. He instructed the young Jerrell to follow the career of a man named Kel Ciro, from Dolomar, who would be born in a year’s time. He also asked Jerrell to be in the Minos cluster on a certain date, almost forty years hence, for the ‘sake of moral rectitude’. Believing the Bothan to be a Jedi, Jerrell took his words on board, but was still faintly disbelieving… Imagine his surprise when Kel Ciro was born on Dolomar the following year!

 

By this time, Baimma bore three children of V’Halla – two boys and a girl. V’Halla was not a wealthy Bothan – as a young captain on sufferance, he had to spend a great deal of money to move in the circles which he was expected to. Hence, his children became very independent. The two boys, V’ytor and Salethan, joined the military and their careers almost mirrored each other. They are both currently Lieutenants in the New Republic Navy. The girl Alita also joined the military, in the Bothan Espionage Division, becoming one of the Rebellion’s most valuable agents – until she was killed during the theft of the plans and information on the second Death Star at Endor. 

 

After the Clone Wars, V’Halla was one of the foremost Captains in the Bothan Fleet. He captained the *Prodigal *for a few more years, and then peacetime began to tell on him. He returned to Bothawui to spend some time with his wife and children, and entered semi-retirement to his modest country home. His children grew up and moved away, and he spent much time reading, and thinking. He took various jobs as negotiator for his government in treaties and trade deals, and found he had a talent. And as he and Bhaimma whiled away the years, the Empire rose, with Palpatine at its head…

 

Four years before the Battle of Yavin (eleven years before the Darkstryder Campaign begins), V’Halla got himself elected as a speaker in the Bothawui ruling council. He was vociferous and passionate, and soon made political enemies – one of whom was the infamous Borsk Fey’lya. There was no love lost between the two, but as was always the case in Bothan politics, idealism lost to deviousness every time, and Fey’lya’s star rose faster than V’Halla’s. Fey’lya soon became very powerful, but V’Halla knew secrets about the councilor – some large enough to topple him, and certainly dark enough to merit his toppling. As the Rebellion erupted throughout the galaxy, V’Halla once again signed on with the Bothan Fleet, this time captaining the Nebulon-B Frigate *Rectitude. *His sons joined the Alliance fleet proper, and his daughter barely ever got to see him, so busy was she. Bhaimma remained on homeworld for a few years, after which she was moved to an Alliance safehouse to protect her from reprisals. He looked in on the career of this man Ciro from time to time – he was now a very capable commando.

 

Captain V’Halla Jerrell was back in business, and with a capable crew and good ship, he scored many victories for the Rebels. He captained the *Rectitude *at Endor, Bakura, Tanaab and other famous battles. He was decorated by the Rebel Alliance and New Republic many times, but he never was promoted. You see, Fey’lya had much influence with the New Republic, and the method of his gaining this influence was known to V’halla. Fey’lya had performed exterminations on his political enemies and their families in the guise of sending them medical aid. The person at the heart of the mystery was Fey’lya’s personal physician, a Mon Calamari named Akanseh, an elusive being indeed. Fey’lya could not kill V’Halla because of his prominence, but he could stunt the captain’s prospects and prevent him from becoming more powerful. V’halla could not reveal the conspiracy for fear that his family would be harmed. In fact, V’Halla believes that Fey’lya had something to do with the death of his daughter, but has no hard proof.

 

Back in the arena, V’Halla was happy to serve the New Republic in the Bothan Navy ; there was much work to be done fighting back the Empire, there was a new conundrum regarding Fey’lya and Akanseh, and he had time to kill before the ‘black Bantha’s prophesy became true.

 

A few months ago, Fleet Captain Velst Nas’yro, a close friend of Fey’lya’s, requisitioned the *Rectitude *and a large number of other craft for an extended mission into the Minos Cluster. The dates corresponded. V’Halla was wary of what Fey’lya was up to, and went cautiously. The mission took the Fleet into Kathol Sector, with sealed orders that V’Halla was eventually able to crack.

 

Suddenly, everything began to fall into place…

Season Three Update:

 

***

V’halla Jerrell’s feud with Fey’lya is close to being explained. When the FarStar was in the past, in the Clone Wars, Jerrell contacted his father and told him all. He discovered the nature of Fey’lya (that he was a shapeshifter named Derris), and threatened him. Jerrell from the future at one point even stole Jerrell from the past’s ship, the Gunship *Prodigal*, and later returned it as he’d found it. He found out that the ‘black Bantha’ was his sidekick, Sgt. Major Forin Deevo, sent by Jerrell to pass on the message. He also stole lots of money from his own bank account. These mysteries, which had puzzled him for years, were at least solved – as Jerrell said at the time, "I’d hate to think that I’d been outwitted by anyone but myself."

 

Returning to the present, Jerrell resigns his commission and promotes his sidekick Deevo to Captain of the shuttle *Alita.* Having captured and interrogated his brother Jovunn (a longtime aide of Fey’lya’s and technically an enemy of V’halla’s) Jerrell learns of his people’s involvement with an Imperial warlord and of Derris/Borsk Fey’lya’s plans to take over the galaxy…
