"First,  we had  to chop  a landing bay out  of the Ghtroc's superstructure, replacing bulkheads with temporary retaining  walls. We  hooked up  some  separator  charges  on  the  removable  'floor'  and  side panels,  so when  Skywalker activated  the  'shroud' sequence, the panels would blow out and release the X-wing. Not exactly conventional maneuvers, eh?
