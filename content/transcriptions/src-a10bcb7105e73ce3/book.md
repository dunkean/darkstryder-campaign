# STAR LINES

## The Unofficial Onboard Magazine of the N.R.S. *FarStar*

 

Volume 2 Issue 1 Special Morrtmas Edition, Dateline 432-09

 

Well, once again the Gamorrean festival of Morrtmas is upon us, and the mess hall rings with the joyous sound of crewmen everywhere wishing their colleagues peace and love in the times to come. Or so the ideal story goes.

 

The reality is different. Every day this ship becomes a home for rogues, vagabonds and scoundrels of every order, not to mention mad droids, spies, traitors, ex-Imps, "shuttle pilots" - we seem to take on more crewmen at every port, every one of which is a potential saboteur. The editorial team here at Star Lines feels that it’s about time this ship became a stable home for all of us regulars, or we may as well turn around and go home. Hell, the next time we use the bathroom we may set off a booby-trap and blow up half the ship!

 

And what of our command crew? Our new Captain, a clone with a vendetta. The second officer - an Imp Colonel with delusions of grandeur. At least our first officer’s well-balanced - he’s got a chip on both shoulders. Add to this a droid with a bantha-hair wig, a Defel who attracts bounty hunters like flies, a bar owner-cum-secret agent and a Twi’lek con-man and oh yeah, you got yourself the perfect team for this mission!

 

But, enough moaning. We have something to celebrate! It’s Morrtmas once more, and Bongo, our esteemed Gamorrean Chef, is making a special effort to make his meals edible as his religious festival approaches. So while we sit back and sample his traditional Mynock Pies and Roast TaunTaun, let us sing together some of these festive tunes (adapted from the original Gamorrean, with words doctored by the editorial team), and for the moment forget our cares…
