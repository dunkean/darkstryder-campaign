## Basic Education

Where one goes to school plays a significant role in the development of life goals, network of friends,and, of course,education.Species,social level,and the character's culture and technological level will all impact educational opportunities and experiences.In this section, we are concerned with basic education, the childhood education which consists of the sort of knowledge and information a person needs to get by in Imperial society.University education will be covered in "Past Occupations."
