The bottom line is that Imperials are not a homogeneous lot. There are distinct sub-divisions among Imperial supporters,which may be broken down into four categories: elite, traditional, citizen, and renegade.

## Imperial Elites

Imperial "elites" are people who owe their power to the Emperor and serve as his power base;as powerful as the Emperor is,he could not easily rule without the support of certain key aristocrats and military leaders. In either case, the elites know that they need Palpatine where he is if they are to continue thriving -their fortunes are tied with those of the Emperor,like itornot.Consequently,their loyaltyistoPalpatine first, the greater good of the Empire second.
