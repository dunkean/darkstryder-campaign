## Rebel Alliance Templates

The following templates represent characters allied with the Rebel Alliance or the New Repub lic government, depending on the era.Such support may be either overt or covert.

Some characters presented here serve in the Alliance military. Others are civilians who lend their support indirectly to the Rebel cause.Each is capable of supporting other Rebel characters already committed to an ongoing campaign.

Please note that the characters are not locked into a particular time period.Obviously,a classic-era Rebel will work well in the New-Republic era as well.These characters should beolder and wiser; an experienced fighter can think a way out of a tight spot instead of shooting their way to a solution.

Also,many Rebels can retire from military life in the New Republic era.(Why not have a resistance leader-turned-independent shipper?)

In short, these characters are restricted only by.player imagination and the storytelling needs of the gamemaster.
