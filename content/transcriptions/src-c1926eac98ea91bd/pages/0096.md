Background: You once had a home, friends, family and career on a colony world.Politics held no interest for you,until the Empire appeared in orbit over your world, and bludgeoned it into submission. When the smoke cleared,your home was destroyed,your friends were in irons, and your family was dead.Now politics interest you a great deal.

The Imperial militaryforces moved on after pacifying the local populace and settingup a puppet government, leaving you and your fellow citizens to pick up the spirit out of your people, but you are determined to prove them wrong.

You've dedicated your considerable organizational skills to planning and executing mass demonstrations, peaceful sabotage, and so on.You refuse to commit violent acts, however, and have resisted attempts to develop your organization into a Rebel cell.
