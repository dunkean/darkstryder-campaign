## Berchest

Berchest is one of the largest trade worlds of

the Anthos sector, and benefits from a healthy volume of Imperial traffic.One of the great wonders of the Old Republic,Berchest has long been a major tourist attraction, though the tourist industry has been in slow decline since the days of the Clone Wars and the rise of the Empire. Fortunately, the Imperial trade has helped it maintain its high standards.

Since the early days of the Old Republic, Berchest has been hailed as a planet of extraor-

dinary beauty.Its saline-rich seas have left huge crystallinedeposits alongthe coasts,and formed theminto amazing crystalstructures.These crystals,some of them.several kilometers in diameter, have been painstakingly sculpted into actual cities by the Berchestian artists and architects.

The colonies mine Tibanna gas from the atmosphere.
