Like most of his species, Marakoloon is physically imposing; he stands over two and a half meters tall and has a furry body dominated by a huge, tusked head.His tusks have been engraved and inlaid with swirling, lattice-like silver designs.

Marakoloon was born on Toola. His father's holdings on the Great Ice Floes allowed his family to make a good income selling the ice to traders. Since he was not in line to inherit the holdings, his family paid passage on an ice freighter so that Marakoloon couldjoinhisuncleasa bountyhunter.

## Chambamakk

Chambamakk began his bounty hunting career long before he ever received an official license.On his home world of Kashyyyk, he was a member of a select group of hunters and warriors called an "Honor Clan."

His Honor Clan considered all Wookiees killed or enslaved by the Empire to be extensions of their"honor family."Therefore, members of Chambamakk's clan were held by their honor to effect the release of the enslaved and to bring their slavers to justice-Wookiee justice, which is none too gentle. The Honor Clans wreaked havoc among the slaver groups gathering Wookiees to ship offplanet, at least until the slavers began to receive heavily armed escorts from the Empire.

During the entire reign of the Emperor, the Wookiee resistance kept detailed records of all the Wookiees enslaved or killed outright by Imperial Forces. Just as importantly, exacting files were kept on the slavers themselves, their ships and their Imperial colleagues.
