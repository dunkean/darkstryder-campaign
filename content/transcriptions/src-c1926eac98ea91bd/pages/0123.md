## Toirboggle

Toirboggle is a Snivvian artist working on his crowning life's work, Honor Amongst Thieves,an epic poem about piracy. To this end, he reads texts and works dating all the way back to the Expansionist Period.

Ever one to take research just a bit too far Toirboggle decided to seek membership on a pirate ship.The Snivvian wentto a spacer bar on Nar Shaddaa, and simply asked the first ruffian he saw where the pirates were.Amazingly,this figure actually was a pirate of some note, Olian Okamie.

Rather than kill him outright, Okamie, amused by this bold Snivvian, decided that Toirboggle could serve as her "court jester."To her amazement, Toirboggle actually came in handy aboard her vessel.A capable accountant, he kept accurate records of the loot they scored, and soon revealed to Okamie that one of her pirates was stealing from the group coffers.

Now Toirboggle travels with Okamie's pirates on the rim of Trax sector. He doesn't personally participate in acts of violence,but he does try to get into the spirit of things,with displays of flamboyant threats and frequent loud shouting. After all,what better wayto understand the mind of a pirate than to become one?
