## Tatooine

Tatooine is a small desert world far from the center of activity of the galaxy. Twin suns beat down on this sand-covered world, burning the great expanses of desert and all those who dwell there.

![Image](../images/image_000000_f0401cc5dd227566b4fd2cdcf14f22b1951aae57c6e860ad3e1f29b9104170ed.png)

The planet is home to Jawas, Sand People, banthas,dewbacks,humansettlers,and assorted aliens who populate Mos Eisley's spaceport district.The human settlers,most of whom make a living as moisture farmers, live in small communities and homesteads. Many members of the. galaxy's fringe society,such as smugglers, mercenaries, and bounty hunters,use Tatooine as a base because of its distance from the watchful eyes of the Empire and other galactic governments.
