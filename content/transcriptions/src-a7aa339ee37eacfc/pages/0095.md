Those Weequays who are not employed by Hults are often found serving in some military or pseudo-military capacity: many find work as mercenaries, bounty hunters and hired muscle. When Weequay leave their homeworld and seek employment in the galaxy, they most often go in small groups varying from two to 10 members, often from the same clan.

Suggested Skills: Weequays tend to prefer melee weapons, particularly force pikes, over ranged weapons such as blasters and slugthrowers. Most Weequays have training in melee combat, melee parry and survival: desert.
