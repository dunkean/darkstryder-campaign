## Anomids

Appearance and Biology: Anomids are wil­ lowy humanoids native to the Yablari system. They typically dress in oversized dendrite robes that brush the ground around their six-toed feet. The sleeves hang below the tips of their slender hands, which are often gloved, and their faces are shadowed byvoluminous hoods the entire outfit keeps the sun, rain, and wind at bay.

Most Anomids have pale, virtuallytranslucent skin that is delicate because of a lack of exposure to the elements. Some groups of Anomids wear sleeveless tunics and no covering on their heads, and tend to have slightly ruddy complexions; they are considered upstarts and dissidents by the Anomid majority. These Anomid radicals are nonconformists who delight in their differences and are quick to scoff at their peers' weighty garments and pale compiexions.
