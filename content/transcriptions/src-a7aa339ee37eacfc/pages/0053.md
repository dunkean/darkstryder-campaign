TheKlatooinan representative was Barada M'Beg, who came to be one of the most revered scholars and leaders in Klatooinan history. Many a Klatooinan child has been named Baradaor M'Beg since.

TheHutts have used the Klatooinans, the Nikto, and the Vodrans as their enforcers, bodyguards, and all-around lackeys. The three Hutt charges (as specified in the contract, theTreaty ofVontor) helped the Hutts repel Xim the Despot at the Third Battle of Vontor. The Klatooinans have served the Hutts loyally for over 25,000 Standard Years.

However, the Klatooinans believethatallthings eventually pass perhaps even their servitude to the Hutts. Word of Rebel Alliance victories at Toprawa, Begeren, and Yavin fanned the flame of rebellion in the hearts of Klatooinan young. Many Klatooinans have turned their backs on the tradi­ tions that have ruled their people for a thousand generations. Some have escaped on cargo ships, becoming members of tramp freighter crews. Others have fled into the Klatooinan wilderness to escape their elders.

The Rebel Alliance has taken notice of the Klatooinan youths, but because Klatooine is deep in Hutt Space, it is almost impossible to provide assistance or aid to the Klatooinans. It should be noted, however, the Rebel Alliance seems to have a knack for accomplishing the impossible.

The council elects new members only after a sitting council mem­ ber has died or has proven him or herself unable to complete council duties. Can­ didates are judged on age, work experience, and a debate process based on the inter­ pretation of ancient Klatooinan myths and traditions. The Klatooinans are left by the Hutts to rule themselves, but the Hutts control all off­ world concerns.

TechnologyLevel:The Klatooinans have been introduced to hyperdrive-Ievel technology, but prefer a simpler existence centered around in­ dustrial-level technology. While the main cities of Klatooine have advanced computer systems and repulsorlift transportation, the area around the Fountain is kept as it has been for thousands of years. No advanced technology is allowed within a kilometer of this sacred site.

Trade and Technology: The Klatooinans have never established a trade relationship without the approval of the Hutts. Klatooinan corpora­ tions and organizations, such as the famed Klatooinan Trade Guild, are simply vehicles to entend the will of their Hutt masters.

Klatooinans in the Galaxy: The Kiatooinans are known throughout the galaxy as Hutt hench­ men, along with the Nikto and the Vodrans. They are often erroneously referred to as Baradas because so many of their members have that as their name. Younger Klatooinans are forsaking tradition and refusing to enter servitude; some of them have managed to join competing crime families or the Rebel Alliance.

Gamemaster Notes: Klatooinans can range from cannon-fodder gamemaster characters for those players in trouble with the Hutts to heroic player characters aspiring to be Rebels. The Klatooinans who are loyal to their traditions usually cannot be swayed, while those who were delinquent youths tend to be hardened and cyni­ cal. If given free reign by their masters or employ­ ers, they can be a very dangerous lot indeed.

PersonalityNotes: Klatooinans displaya fierce dedication, be they traditionalists or rebellious youths. There is a sense of disenfranchisement among the younger Klatooinans, who see little value in ancient traditions that keep them en­ slaved.

SuggestedSkills: As toughs, Klatooinans have combat skills, but individual Klatooinans can have virtually any skills and can be found in any occupation.
