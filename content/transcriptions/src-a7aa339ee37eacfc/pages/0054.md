## I&lt;rytollal(s

TheKrytollaks are a species that virtually worships Emperor Palpatine. These aliens come from Thandruss, a world with a rigid social structure and a staunchly pro-Imperial government. The Krytollaks havea reputation among other species for "having skulls as thick as their shells."

Appearance and Biology: The Krytollaks are a warm-blooded spe­ cies that have both a hard internal skeleton and a tough outer shell. The shells have several different plates, while the muscles workwith the skeleton and plates to provide movement. Most Krytollak shells are dull brown or black, but a few have green shells. The Krytollaks see green shells as an outward sign of greatness.

Some Krytollaks paint designs on their shells. In particular, Krytollak nobles usually have their personal crest painted on their body plates. A Krytollak's shell keeps growing throughout his or her lifetime and the aliens cannot survive the removal ofthese plates.

Krytollaks have visible mouths and eyes in the breaks between facial plates. Krytollaks have eyes with irises and pupils, although they tend to be black or brown in color. Krytollaks can hear through a series of membranes ringing the facial plates. They have no sense of smell. The skin beneath the shell plates is usually reddish brown.

The Krytollak diet consists mostly of vegetables. They can also devour any sort of carbon-based rock, from which they draw trace elements to grow their shells. Krytollaks consider rocks and plants from other worlds to

be delicacies.

Female Krytollaks average 10-20 centimeters taller than the males and their shells tend to be darker. Krytollaks grow throughout their lives and older specimens reach heights of almost 3 meters. Krytollaks can live for nearly a century, and suffer few signs of deterioration from aging.

Temperament: If left alone, most Krytollaks ignore the world around them. To others, the species tends to come off as apathetic and self­ involved, but this is because Krytollaks want to understand a situation before becominga part of it. Krytollaks are difficult to anger, but once a Krytollak gets offended, forgiveness is a long time in coming, if ever.

Krytollaks on Thandruss dress in robes, imi­ tating the clothing of Imperial officers and nobil­ ity. Thecolor gray is reserved for Krytollak nobil­ ity.
