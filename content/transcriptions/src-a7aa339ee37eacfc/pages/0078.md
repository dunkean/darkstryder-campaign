## Siudir

Appearance and Biology: Sludir are large beasts with six stocky legs and two arms. Their arms end in two fingers and an opposable digit. A Sludir's head consists mostly of a thick snout with four nostrils and a large, toothy sneer. Two eyes are often framed beneath a mound of hair which Siudir are fond of tying in several different styles. The six legs are short yet stocky, and end in flat, round feet with little proto-toes toward the front.
