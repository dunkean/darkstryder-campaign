## Then a huge mouth filled with blade-sharp teeth, lunges out of the water and snaps at you.

The monsail attacks the lead character. The monsail's victim may use his or her swimming. skill as a combat or full dodge. If it hits, the monsail drags the character under the water. Characters may attack the monsail by making Easy swimming rolls and attack rolls.

If it takes stun or greater damage, the monsail spits out its lunch and darts away. See "Animal Encounters" for its statistics. If killed, the monsail provides enough food for four meals..
