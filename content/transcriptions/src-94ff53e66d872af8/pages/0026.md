## Asteroid 7785

The Rebels should feel a fair amount of skepticism about the possible survival of the Royal Palace on Alderaan. After all, how could anything have withstood the devastating power of the Death Star's superlaser?

In fact, the portion of the palace that survived was little more than a fragment, and a predominantly underground portion besides.
