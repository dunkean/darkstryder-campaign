## Level One - High Orbit

Keep in mind that the player characters' ship is in a screaming dive toward the surface, and they have no time to think, only act. Keep the pressure on by giving each player five seconds to announce an action. If he hesitates, skip him and go to the next player..

Pilot: A Moderate space transports roll is required to pull the ship out of its dive. A failed roll results in a stomach-wrenching lurch as the ship spins out of control and drops to level three. The range to the Scent of Fear increases to medium.

Modified to be better shielded, more maneuverable, and faster than the average Taloron Prey Hunter, Crutag has sacrificed weapon power.
