1. The Jungle Cantina. This large building houses one of the best cantinas on Bazaar. Three bartenders work the massive central bar, and up to six waitresses wander among the tables. A. four-man band plays from a secluded alcove, mixing Ithorian classics with the most popular tunes on the galactic charts. Everyone who visits Isttu comes here eventually, and the Rebels can. hear an assortment of stories and rumors if they spend some time (and some credits) among the clientele. These rumors include:
