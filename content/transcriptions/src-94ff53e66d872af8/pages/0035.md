## Asteroid Run

To get to Asteroid 7785, the Rebels must navigate a path through a very dense portion of the asteroid field. It is not an easy trip, and there are a number of dangers along the way -- both. expected and unexpected. If the Rebels used the. asteroid tracking station at the outpost to map a path, then they get to add +2 to all piloting and copiloting rolls made during the run (see "The Rules" below).

If the Rebels don't have their own ship, we provide statistics for a ship that can be assigned to them for the completion of this mission. Also, note the salvage ship stats as well, for it will. appear during the run to harass the Rebels.

The salvage ship, piloted by Belinda Vog, arrives just as the Rebels have cleared the outpost dock and are getting ready to begin their run through the aster-. oid field. Naturally, it follows them..

If you would prefer to use the space combat rules from Second Edition, base difficulty for passing through the field is Moderate.

Passage through the field takes six rounds, with difficulty increasing one level every three rounds. However, gamemasters are encouraged to increase or reduce the difficulty number based on the character's actions.
