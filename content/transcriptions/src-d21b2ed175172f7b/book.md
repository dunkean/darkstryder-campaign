# Captain Keleman Ciro

## Commanding Officer, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Played by [Will ‘Pix’ Pickering](credits.html#pix)

 

![Ciro](images/b8e16e8aa67037779b7798daef8b0e8a73d7dcc404592541f1dff779a252fd32.jpg)

"We’re a New Republic Special Operations force, and we’re here to save your planet."

 

Keleman Ciro was born on the ice planet Dolomar, a prosperous but remote mining world plagued by blizzards, ice storms and extreme cold. The Dolomar colonists make their homes underground in immense tunnels and caverns, and are an extremely stubborn and resilient group of people. After the Battle of Endor, the world allied itself with the New Republic, and the Empire found out just how stubborn the inhabitants could be - the initial invasion fleet was ambushed and wiped out. Even now, the remote Dolomar is besieged by Imperial troops, but the siege is deadlocked with the planet’s gunners shooting down any landing troop transports from deep within the surface.

 

Ciro grew up in the city-complex of Difru, under the L’tiri Peaks. He became a computer programming student and planned a quiet career in front of flickering computer screens. However, as the Empire’s repressive grip on Dolomar tightened, Kel found himself drawn into a local resistance group. After participating in a  series of guerrilla assaults that toppled the planet’s Imperial Governor, Kel joined up with the New Republic proper. His brother Noell, also seeing the opportunity to leave his isolated homeworld, decided to accompany him.

 

Noell was assigned to the New Republic pilot corps, and quickly made the rank of Flight Officer. Keleman, on the other hand, began an intensive training course for commando operatives, putting to use all the skills that he had developed during his stint as a resistance fighter on Dolomar. After exceptional performances during his initial assignments in Brak Sector, Kel was transferred to the New Republic’s most elite special ops unit, Lieutenant Page’s "Katarn Commandos". In the two years he has served under Page, Kel has proved his worth time and again, as well as learning a few tricks from his colleagues and developing his command skills. He swiftly worked his way up the chain of command, attaining the rank of Sergeant-Major. Kel does not make friends easily, and pretty much all the friends he has in the galaxy were made during his commando service.

 

When the New Republic invaded Kal’Shebbol, the capital world of Kathol Sector, Ciro’s life changed forever. He was part of the initial team which landed on the planet, broke into Moff Sarne’s citadel, and dropped the defence shields so that the bombardment could begin. The mission was accomplished, but the team tangled with Sarne himself on the surface before his escape. Sarne used alien technology against the commandos, technology which Ciro later found out was referred to as "Darkstryder". Ciro was the only member of the team to put up any resistance to the Darkstryder mindwarping modules, and this more than anything else was the deciding factor in Lieutenant Page’s decision to put Ciro in charge of the operation to hunt down Sarne.

 

Ciro used the time he had to outfit his ship, the *FarStar*, very well indeed. He was, however, forced to recruit heavily from the civilian population, and full background checks were impossible. Ciro decided it was worth running the risk of having the crew infiltrated by unsavouries in order to leave immediately with a full complement. With the mission came yet more promotion - a lightyear’s jump in rank to Captain.

 

Ciro felt he rapidly made the transition from elite operative to starship captain rather well, given the exceptional circumstances. This demonstrates one of his most admirable qualities - confidence in his own abilities. He is particularly confident in his own computer skills (yet recognises that his knowledge is broader and less specific than others’), but he lacks the bluster and bravado of many of his fellow commandos. Ciro prefers to let his actions speak for themselves.

 

In his role as commander of the *FarStar*, Ciro showed both reserve and insistence. He gave a speech to the entire crew in the hangar bay before the mission launched, a speech which outlined his goals and his rules: Ciro has no use for shows of temper or swagger. He expects his orders to be followed immediately and without question. This does not mean that he doesn’t listen to advice; in fact, it’s often his highest priority - it’s just that he then tends to issue his immutable opinion with the minimum of explanation. Those who want to follow his orders will be rewarded accordingly, while those who want to do things their own way are a danger to the mission, and can be dropped off at the nearest planet, inhabited or otherwise. Ciro knows that survival and success depend on building a focused team, and he doesn’t have time to coddle anyone’s personal preferences.

 

Ciro was ill at ease commanding such a large crew, but his own confidence, and that which Page has shown him, made him sure that he would succeed in his duties - even if there were to be a few rough spots in the early going.

 

And rough spots there were. At Kolatill, on the *FarStar’s* first mission, Ciro ordered his crew to assist in a relief effort to help the sick and injured on the surface after an Imperial bombing. During the operation, Ciro and Corporal Peters of security accompanied the Kolatill council members in a shuttle down to the planet’s surface. On the way, they were ambushed by one of Sarne’s cruisers and tractored aboard, despite the best efforts of the *FarStar* and its fighters.

 

Thus began Ciro’s ordeal at the hands of Sarne. For days at a time, he was tortured and deprived of sleep. Ciro was no stranger to torture - once, he fell into the hands of the Imperial governor of Brak sector for two days - but as well as normal implements, Sarne used strange alien technology, Darkstryder modules. He took blood tests, DNA samples, neural matter, and continually tested Ciro’s resistance to the Darkstryder modules’ effects. For some time, Ciro endured all this aboard what he took to be one of Sarne’s ships. His memories of that time seem blurred and hazy - he was far too weak to try to effect an escape or learn any secrets. He developed his mental resistance further, and even turned to the Force in his hour of need; while not an overtly religious man, Ciro always entertained the possibility of the Force’s existence. Now, he knows he felt something...

 

One other thing helped him through his ordeal. Kaiya Adrimetrum. Kel’s first officer on board the     *FarStar,* she had served with him in Page’s commandos for two years. She was smart, pretty, fierce, honourable, and true. She had faced so much suffering, with a husband and daughter dead. And Keleman was in love with her. He had been since he met her, and he never told her. Oh, he knew that she considered him a friend, a comrade-in-arms, trusted him with her life, but did she feel the same way? They’d seen each other go weeks without a bath, they’d huddled together for warmth on night missions, they had no secrets. And Page always preached that personal attachments within the group were a sure-fire way for a commando to check out early, risking all for an individual and costing the team the mission. And when thousands of troops are depending on you, that’s not acceptable.

 

So, Ciro bided his time. And he thought that, once aboard the *FarStar*, he’d be free to finally tell her. He never got the time. But her face carried him through his ordeal. Once, near the end, he even dreamt he talked to her, and it was so real he could swear it really happened. When she arrived to rescue him, to exchange herself for him, he knew there was no stopping her, so he let it happen, even though he considered her to be the best candidate for the leader of the mission. It was then that he told her how he felt, for he feared he’d never see her again.

 

Ciro also found out the reason for Sarne’s tests - the Moff was cloning him to create an army of Darkstryder-resistant stormtroopers! He now believes this is Kaiya’s fate too, and has sworn to get her back, whatever the cost, and make Sarne pay...

Season Two Update: 

***

 

Ciro is back with his crew now, and will shortly take command from Acting Captain Thyte, the communications officer. He has a prior acquaintance with some of the crew - he has served with the Wookiee Lofryyhn in Page’s commandos, and knows him to be a staunch friend of Kaiya’s. Kaiya told Lofryyhn to look after Kel before she left. He also knows Sergeant Randall Vallens, who served with him during his time in the Brak Sector operations. Vallens was in charge of Brak Garrison’s security, and the leader of the squad that rescued Ciro from the Imperial Governor’s torture rooms. The two men fought side-by-side for much of the remaining engagements. They owe each other their lives many times over, and have remained in correspondence ever since Brak Sector. Although Vallens is in the front line less and less these days due to his old injuries, the moment Ciro heard he was in the Kal’Shebbol task force he immediately requested that his old friend be transferred to the *FarStar* to head up the ground assault crews.

 

Keleman Ciro is a fairly non-descript human male of medium height, with a shaven head, and on occasion a goatee beard. He is in his early thirties, but is in much better shape than most men a decade younger.
