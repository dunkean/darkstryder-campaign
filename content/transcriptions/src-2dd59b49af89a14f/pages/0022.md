With therise of the Empire,House Melantha saw its opportunity.It began to forge ties with the Imperial Court, and sent many of its best leaders to Imperial Center.Moreso than any other house,Melantha has contributed to the Imperial effort.Several dozen moffs and governors hail from Melantha,and three Melanthan Dark Adepts serve the Emperor himself. Hundreds of lessor officials serve in the government and military as bureau chiefs, ministers, Navy captains,and so on.
