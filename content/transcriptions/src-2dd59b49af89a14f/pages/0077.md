## Grrtok's Band

Grrtok's Band is a mercenary unit based outside of the Tapani sector. However, over the past few decades, it has been hired by numerous factions in the sector, such as the Jalk Syndicate,House Barnaba,and even the Empire. Currently it is under contract with House Barnaba.

The unit consists of 400 Gamorrean heavy infantry,armed with two-handed vibro-axes.
