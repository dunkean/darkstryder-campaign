From below,the common people have their system of guilds,juries,boards,and local community governments which allows them to be heard and take part in the governing of the sector. While most of the sector's planets are claimed by the nobles, most day-to-day decisions affecting these planets are made by civil servants,most of whom are commoners (others are knights).

Civic festivals such as Tapani Day,Expansion Week, and numerous lesser holidays observed by various houses are occasions of communal celebration which help unite the classes.Such government-sponsored holidays are heavy on pageantry and lavish displays of house might (including popular sporting events), as well as elaborate public feasts. Such activities generate goodwill toward the sponsoring houses,and,by extension,the entire system of government.
