About a hundred years ago,however,House Mecetti drew it into the thick of politics by passing laws targeting Barnaba's trade with theFreeworlds.Its economythreatened,House Barnaba moved quickly to seal a treaty with House Melantha, one of Mecetti's chief rivals.
