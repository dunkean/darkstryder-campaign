Enemies:Historically,Melantha has played middleman in the Pelagia-Mecetti feud,playing one side against the other to best advantage With Pelagia gone, Mecetti and Melantha have butted heads with increasing frequency,and the contest for Tapani is developing into a bitter rivalry.Calipsa and Reena,as loyal allies of Mecetti, join in the greater house's rivalry against Melantha.

House Melantha has not yet moved directly against Mecetti except in the Great Council. However,should an opportunityariseto strike outatMecettithroughanotherhouse,Melantha will almost certainly take it.

Assets:Financially,House Melantha has a secure foundation, both within and without the sector. Through its expatriates on Coruscant and other Imperial worlds,it holds considerable investments in foreign megacorps, and funnels these outside credits backinto the sector.It alsoowns or at least has a hand in many sector-based businesses.By diversifying its holdings,it hopes to ensure its survival even should its domestic assets be seized or destroyed.
