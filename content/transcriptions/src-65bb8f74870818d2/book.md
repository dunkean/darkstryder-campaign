Early in season 3 of the Darkstryder Campaign, Captain Trey'Vhalla Jerrell of the Bothan Navy came aboard the FarStar after the loss of his Nebulon-B frigate, the Rectitude. Jerrell's charismatic leadership united the Bothan members of the FarStar's crew into a small, loyal faction, which also provided a haven for Tamara Barinn, the woman out of time who had no wish to be associated with the New Republic. This faction caused some friction between the Bothans and Captain Ciro early in season 3. With Jerrell's resignation of his commission and Tamara and Lu'ria's deaths, this faction disbanded.
