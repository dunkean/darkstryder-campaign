## Special Abilities:

Life-witchLife witches can sustain the infirm, the elderly and the dying through their peculiar gifts. While under a life-witch's "Support,"characters can be considered healthy,and are immuneto the effects of aging. Any wounds a Supported character incurs can be healed by the touch of the life-witch.However, after a relatively short period of time, the life-witch withdraws the Support, and the affected character dies instantly.The Support lasts for up to five years. Life-witches are not subject to aging,provided they remain bonded to a husband.An unbonded life-witch ages normally.
