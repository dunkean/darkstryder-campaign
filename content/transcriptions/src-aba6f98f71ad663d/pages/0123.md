## NRI Special Threats File 9888:89:98: Trellar Yagen

After Bovo Yagen was settled by humans,severalbeings on theplanet decided to explore the other members of the system. They found Trellar Yagen,named after one of Captain Yagen's sons. It is a sweltering planet filled with many carnivorous fauna and flora; one of the most famous is the Trell, a small but vicious jungle predator.The Bovorians took great delight in hunting this creatureits incredible speed in darting through the trees made it difficult to catch, even for Bovorian eyes and ears.The leather made from Trell skin is incredibly soft and supple, and is highly prized by well-to-do store owners.

The humans set up a processing plant on this planet, in agreement with the Bovorians after mining colonies were founded throughout the asteroid field and on Heloan Yagen's many moons. The Bovorians agreed that the mining operations were critical,but refused to allow the loud machinery on their homeworld; the rumbleofindustrialmachinerywould damage the Bovorian's sensitive hearing. The humans gladly accepted Trellar Yagen as the base of processing operations. This further added to the challenge for Bovorians huntingTrell-theyhadto wear specialized ear protection to save their hearing, hence increasing the difficulty.
