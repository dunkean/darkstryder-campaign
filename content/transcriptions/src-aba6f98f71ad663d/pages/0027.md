Augwynne's lifehas been filledwithtragedy.During her tenure as Clan Mother of Singing Mountain, Augwynne has experienced the loss of all three ofher daughters. Kara'Teel-heryoungestchild-died while protecting her. The eldest, Baruka, was seduced by the dark spells and had to be exiled from the Clan.But her greatest tragedy came with the loss of Gethzerion, her favoritedaughter.ThoughAugwynneloved the child dearly, Gethzerion wanted nothingmorethan total controlofSingingMoun tain Clan.To do that,she endeavoredto kill Augwynne herself-a plan that was thwarted by Kara'Teel's sacrifice.

Augwynne is a shrewd, calculating politician of the highest order.Despite her "barbaric"appearance,or perhaps because of it,Augwynne was able to maneuver Han Solo into a tenuous bargain.Singing Mountain Clan would protect Solo and his companions, helping them to recover and repair the damaged Millennium Falcon. In return, Han Solo would give up all rights to Dathomir to the Witch clans.

Augwynne is a thoughtful some say tired-old woman who has finally found some cause for joy in her life.With the death of Gethzerion and her Nightsister Clan, Augwynne and the rest of her clan sisters can live the remainders of their lives in peace. The return of Baruka has also been a blessing to her mother.With her granddaughter Teneniel departing for Hapan space, more than ever Augwynne needs the reassurance of family.
