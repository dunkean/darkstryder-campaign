General Melvar is just one example of the many psychopaths the Empire called "patriotic soldiers."Originally a member of an Imperial Intelligence Destab team, Melvar proved to be too vicious for even that group.Although sadism is often times considered aDestabvirtue,Melvar eventually had to be removed from the field. (Personnel records captured after the Battle of Selaggis indicate that Melvar was considered a "tactical liability"by his fellow Destab agents.) Melvar's psychological profileindicates that hewas a grasping, evil man whose only pleasures came from inflicting pain.

Nevertheless,Melvar'sloyaltywas never in question.Imperial Intelligence had him transferred to IntCon branch and eventually,Melvar was stationed aboard the Star Destroyer Iron Fist.

Melvar-along with his commanding officer, Zsinj-were promoted after a successful attack on the Nightsisters of Dathomir.With the financial bonus he received from that campaign, Melvar purchased ten cybernetic weapons: cuticle implants that gave his hands the appearance of claws.

During his years of service to Zsinj, Melvar attained the rank of General, serving as the Warlord's personal secretary and second-in-command.He was architect of the Raptor program (NRI Security file

119.541.901/RP) and he occasionally accompanied these fearsome soldiers on missions.

Melvar's dark reign came to an abrupt end on the planet Dathomir,having come face to face with another facet of evil-the Nightsister Gethzerion whoexecutedtheGeneral.
