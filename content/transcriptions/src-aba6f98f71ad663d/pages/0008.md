## Section 3.2: The Dathomir Incident

SoloOrgana,Chewbacca, and SeeThreepio discovered not only a portion of Zsinj's fleet encircling Dathomir,but also the Iron Fist, the Super Star Destroyer thoughtto be destroyed at Selaggis,undergoing repairs at the enormous spacedock erected by Zsinj himself.After crash-landing on the planet, the group was found by locals who called themselves the Singing Mountain clan.

The Singing Mountain clan quickly understoodthegroup'spredicament-as did Gethzerion (NRISecurity File 5528192.261) and her "Nightsisters."While the Singing Mountain clan wished to assist the offworlders,Gethzerion wantednothingmore than to find the Millennium Falcon and escape Dathomir, the world on which the

witches hadbeen trapped for their entire lives.

Skywalker andIsolder foundsimilarrefugewith the witch, Teneniel Djo (NRI Security File 4412567.91.9:03),who managed to capture both men and considered them slaves.Once convincedthatthemale "witch,'LukeSkywalker, was not slave material, she promised to let him go,though both men would have to keep up appearances when she brought them to the Singing Mountain tribe.

Once reunited, the Heroes of Yavin,accompanied by Prince Isolder) decided their best course of action would be to repair the Falcon andleave Dathomir.However, parts were scarce, and the only supply depot of sorts was the former Imperial prison on the planet-now home to the dreaded Nightsister clan.

The group brokeinto and out of the prison, accomplishing their mission to retrieve the necessary components with which to repair the Falcon. Gethzerion and her Nightsisters, gave pursuit. A battle ensued-now known as the Battle of Singing Mountain. The Nightsisters were pushed back though Gethzerion survived the engagement. The battle took its toll on the SingingMountain clan as well.Heavylosses were incurred, including Luke Skywalker (who went missing only moments after the battle began).

WarlordZsinj-awareofSolo'spresence on Dathomir and still smarting from the battle at Selaggis)-deployed an elaborate orbital nightcloak (NRI Security File 71518491.3:01), covering the entire planet withdarkness.In threedaystimetheplanet would cool to such a degree that Dathomir could no longer support life.
