Yevethan culture is unusual in that even the greatest Yevethan thinkers donot seem to have seriously considered the idea that there could be other intelligent species in the universe. Intelligent and expansionistic, the Yevethans began to probe out into space shortly after the development of a world-widehierarchical governingsystem. Although lacking hyperdrive technology, the Yevethans settled11 worldsusingtheir long-range realspace thrustships.None of these worlds were occupied by the few sentients of the Cluster, and until contact between the Empire and Yevethans, Yevethan culture saw its own intelligence as a unique feature of existence.
