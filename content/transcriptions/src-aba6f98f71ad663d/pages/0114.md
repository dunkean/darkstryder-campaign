## Addendum/Personal:Airen Cracken

I must admit that for all I thought the man was brilliant, I thoroughly disliked him.I once had the pleasure of being dressed down by the Admiral about ten years ago.I had to report to Bakura to surveysomeNewRepublicwreckagefound in their system. When I met Admiral Ossilege in the docking bay of his cruiser, I was informed by him that the "New Republicwasinefficientandclumsy"in losing one of our ships.He expressed his doubtin the competence of the New Republic,then remarked his opinion was confirmed when they sent me to fix the problem.

Never had my temper been so sorely tried aswhenImet Admiral HortelOssilege. Now, after finding out he sacrificed his life to help win the battle against the Sacorrian fleet, I am bemused to find myself nearly praising him when I talk of his character to other people.It is not the end I would have wished on anybody,yet it seems to fit Admiral Ossilege's personality-a proper passage for him to leave this galaxy to whatever lies beyond.

Unlike most astromech droids, the Q9 series was designed with a fairly complex personality matrix to facilitate rapid learning.

IA mantains that the Q9 series was a complete disaster; the personality matrix programming was faulty in most production models, and only a few hundred were made and sold. Q9-X2, one of the last Q9series prototypes constructed by IA, is an exception to this.

Q9 is owned by Ebrihim who has, at times, regretted his decision to buy the droid. The droid has a tendency to drive even a patient Drall mad with aggravation. Still, Q9 has proven to be invaluable in times of disaster.Q9 has modified himself far beyond original design specs; unlike most Q-series droids-which cease selfmodification after a time Q9 constantly improves his own design.
