## NRI Special Threats File 4109.7891.09:CorellianAssault Boat

The Corellian Mynock-class assault boat is little more than an upgunned system patrol craft.The vessel has a higher crew capacity (to accommodate boarding actions) and slightly upgraded weaponry.

The assault boat's primary mission profile is ground assault; the craft has sufficient weaponry to cover ground troopers during a landing action, and can carry a sufficient ground strike force to handle most small skirmishes.
