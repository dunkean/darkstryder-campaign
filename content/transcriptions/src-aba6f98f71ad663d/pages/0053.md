The rancors of Dathomir have divided themselves into several tribe like groups known as"herds.Each herd is led by a Herd Mother,whose leadership is passed along matriarchal lines. Those herds that have allied themselves with the Witch clans have grown strong and wise (at least when compared to other rancors).

The Witch clans have also taught their rancors how to make nets and spears, how to wear battle armor, and to a very special few-how to read and write in a simple pictographic language.In addition-perhaps due to their proximity to the Witchesthese rancor "mutants" havebecomeincreasinglyintelligent.

Modern Dathomirian rancors possess a rudimentary form of "racial memory."These rancors arenow able to understand and relate to one another several forms of abstract thought.A talented few, such as the rancornamed Tosh,can even communicate these ideas to other sentients.
