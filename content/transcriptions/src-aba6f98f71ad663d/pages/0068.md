The Koornacht Cluster is an isolated collection of some 2,000 suns just inside the borders of Farlax sector along the New Republic-Imperial Core border. Among these worlds are about 100 worlds with life, six of which have developed sentient species. Only one has reached its space age: the Yevethans of the N'zoth system.
