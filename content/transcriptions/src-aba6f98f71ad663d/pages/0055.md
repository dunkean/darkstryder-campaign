The Hapan Gun of Command is one of the most innovative technological accomplishments developed in the Hapes Cluster.Few beings know of its existence,and even fewer have ever seen one-that they know of. A Gun of Command looks the same as any other blaster, save for a large dial on the weapon's side.When struck with a blast from a Gun of Command,the target becomes susceptible to any command issued by the attacker.

The Gun of Command was designed by the technologically advanced society of Charubah in the Hapes Cluster as an alternative to violent policing methods. The idea behind the Gun of Command was to disarmacriminalbyorderingthemtodrop their weapons, rather than shooting them with a destructive energy weapon.

In the end however, the Gun of Command was prohibitively expensive, and outfitting all law-enforcement teams with one would have easily broken many local governments.
