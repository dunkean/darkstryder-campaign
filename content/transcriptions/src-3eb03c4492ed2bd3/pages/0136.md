Developed worlds can be worth just a few thousand credits if there is a primitive civilization with no trade,or a world with a depressed economy-remember that entire planets may suffer fromstaggeringdebt).Somewhereinthemiddle aretheplanets with populations in the millions and an annual value of perhaps a few billion credits.On the other hand,a bustling trade world with several billion residents and plenty of valuable resources could be worth several quadrillion credits.
