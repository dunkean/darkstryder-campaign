## Gamemastering Boarding Actions

Boarding actions are the most overused but misplayed obstacle inStar Warsadventures.Most gamemasters andplayershavenoreal idea of what actually would go on during a customs boarding and often resort to lackluster techniques to achieve their goals.Most times characters resort to stowing the contraband in secret compartments that just happen to be on the ship,reducing the boarding action to a series of die rolls and little or no roleplaying. Gamemasters who prepare for and are well-versed in boarding activities will find that players often rise to new levels of creativity in an attempt to outwit or outplay the gamemaster.

The most crucial element of any boarding action is the objective. As the gamemaster, you must have a clear purpose in mind.Is a dramatic plot twist about to occur? Is a comical break from the rigors of gaming needed? Perhaps you need to impart critical information or a few false rumors.Sometimes your boarding events should serve no other purpose than to scare,mislead, or deceive players as to the true purpose of the adventure.Once the objective is chosenyou can create the appropriate boarding officer and resources.
