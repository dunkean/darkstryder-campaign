West End Games has published numerous materials which provide even a novice gamemaster all the basics he or she needs to jump into a boarding scene.

Cracken's RebelField Guide includes information on altering your transponder codes,and howtogetaway withit.Therearealsosome interesting gadgets and modifications to normal equipment which might come in handy in distracting customs inspectors.
