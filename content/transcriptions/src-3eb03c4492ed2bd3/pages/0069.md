## Imperial Seals

Model:Imperial Customs Bureau Seals

Cost:Not available for sale

Type: Coded shipping container seals

Availability:X (Imperial use only)

Game Notes: Imperial seals are virtually impossible to counterfeit,and success should be beyondthe scope ofmostcharacters.Any breakage.electronic disruption or rough handling will cause the seals to rupture and dissolve, rendering them useless.

Capsule: Imperials seals are small plastic strips and stickers used to seal the latches,locks and openings of shipping containers.Embedded with tiny microchips and thin reservoirs of dissolving compounds, Imperial seals can be individually identified with standardized code scanner units.The seals are coated with a bonding agent that activates when the protective wrapper is removed.The bonding agent dries in approximately 30 seconds,hardening the seal.At this point any code tampering,rough handling or breakage activates the seal's small reservoirs of dissolving compounds,destroying the seal. Containers bearing Imperial seals that match Imperial inspection reports written to Write-Once-Read-Many(WORM) chips are allowed to bypass normal custom inspections.
