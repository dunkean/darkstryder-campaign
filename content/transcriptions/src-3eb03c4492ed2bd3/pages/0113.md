- 1.Alliance Combat Assault Vehicles are to be used in a mobile fashion,striking against the flanks and rear ofthe enemy to disrupt their advance,
- 2.When possible, CAVs should support and cover infantry operations, and should seek to overpower enemy infantry.
- 3.Combat Assault Vehicles should not be set against Imperial armored forces unless they outnumber the enemy by a factor of at least three to one.

The Alliance has enough sense not to burden commanders with lists of tactical expoundation.These rules are subject to the exception that all other Alliance doctrines follow:except in emergencies. Some sector commands develop more specific general local orders, but many simply leave the general orders alone.Unlike Imperial doctrine,failure to follow theserules rarely leads to a court-martial. If a commander believes the situation requires an override of standarddoctrine,it's overridden.Successisitsown reward,failure is almost always its own punishment.
