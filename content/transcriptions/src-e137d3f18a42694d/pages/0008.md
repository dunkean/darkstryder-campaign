The Alliance is constantly seeking to expand its spy network to all sectors o f   the galaxy. The Core World sectors are the biggest intelligence prizes: they are the richest, the most  strategically important and heavily-controlled regions in the  Empire,  granting  a  successfully-infiltrated spy network unparalleled access to  the  Empire's corridors  of  power. Networks in the  Core  Worlds stand to make the  most spectacular  gains. Unfortunately, they are also more likely to be compromised  and  eliminated. The Outer Rim Territories, on the other  hand, are relatively poor and of  little strategic importance, and thus receive little Imperial attention.
