## lime Frame

This supplement  is set  in the classic  Star Wars time frame, before thedeaths of  Darth Vaderand the Emperor, shortly after the Battle o f   Yavin. During this period. there is still  a highly effective and well-organized military present in Imperial space, Jabba the Hutt is still alive and operating and  Boba  Fett  is  still among the most  feared bounty hunters in the galaxy. Player characters should be reminded that these are dark times, indeed . . .
