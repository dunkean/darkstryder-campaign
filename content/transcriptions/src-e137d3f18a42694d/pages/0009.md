To survive,  the player characters  will have to change  their  style. They  are not  going to the Minos Cluster to save the galaxy, they are going to the Minos Cluster to hide out. If they can make a little money, and aid the  Rebellion in whatever way they can, whenever they can,  then so much the better. They are ordered to stay out of  any Rebel operations in or near the Minos Cluster, nor  are they to make contact  with  any Rebel personnel.
