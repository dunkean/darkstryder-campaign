## Escape Equipment

Enough escape pods for each crewmember and passenger on board a ship is required  by Imperial  law  (military  vessels  are,  o f course, exempt  from  this  rule). A standard  freightersized escape pod seats  eight passengers and has food and oxygen for two weeks.

Cost:  1,200  credits. Weight: 5 tons.

## Environmental Converters

With this device installed, the captain is able to provide  comfortable  environs  for many different species throughout the ship,  or in one cabin or the cargo hold.
