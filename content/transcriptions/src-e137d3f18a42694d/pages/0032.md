The cost for restocking is  based  on what  is called  a 'base  fee.' Most starports in fairly well traveled  routes charge around 10 credits as a base fee,  while isolated starports (such as  some in the Outer Rim Territories) may charge a base fee  as high as 35 credits (since food  and supplies are expensive to ship to such out  o f   the way locales). ## Repairs

Repairing starships can be a lengthy and expensive process.  The  following  rules  are  repeated from Star Wan-, Second  Edition. Note  that  the repair  costs listed  are for characters who are doing the repairs thernseloes. In general, characters will be using their space transpotis repair or starship weapon repair skills.

These costs are for  new  replacement  parts only and are based on the original retail cost o f the vehicle (as one might suspect, replacement parts are expensioe -it's part  o f the price  of doing business).

In general,  characters can make repair rolls at the end o f 15 minutes o f   work, then one hour, then two hours, then one  day and then two days (this is  for  each repair, not for  fixing the entire ship).As always,  the costs,  repair difficulties and time taken should be customized to fit the situation.

Characters can save on costs by buying used parts (in general, the cost for  used parts will be half that o f   new parts). However, used parts may not be as reliable as new parts -if these parts were abused, they may give out very quickly, causing catastrophic  starship malfunctions at the most inopportune times.

If  characters need to hire someone to do the starship repairs for them, figure that the labor costs  run  about  equal  to the cost o f   the new parts.
