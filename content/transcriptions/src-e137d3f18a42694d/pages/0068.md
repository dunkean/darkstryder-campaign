## Spice Smuggling

A major problem for  the twin planets are different  varieties of narco-spice, which are quite popular  on  Travnin  and  Adarlon  as well  on Shesharileitself.  Though produced by the  underworld, they have still become a leading export, and the player characters are bound to run into the spicerunners after spending  any time on the planet.

## The Spaceport

The spaceport has an extremely tough customs area,  and it is run entirely by local  officials. Atrader  must bribe  theseofficials to  belet  through quickly, otherwise it could take weeks or even months. Most traders also bribe the laborers to unload  quickly, and  the truckers to carry the loads quickly, and so  forth.

This  planet  is  corrupt.  and  it  can get very expensive if you are in any kind of  hurry. However, even  with  a  failing economy,  there  are credits  to  be made:  a  trader  merely needs  to know where to look.
