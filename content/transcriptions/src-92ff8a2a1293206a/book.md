***The Cuthbert
    AI and The Torpedo Sphere***
 
Not enough is known about this to make a

    been modified with Darkstryder tech.

Tanquilla Beach, other Rim of the Kathol Core

***Bombaasa
    Cartel***
 
Before it's collapse, it was the most powerful

***Veild-Danol
    and the Danol Trade Syndicate***
Throughout the Kathol Core Region
An apparent strong trade group on the edges of
    the Kathol Core Region, and in the Outback.  Talks are underway for the Danol group
    to join the Free Traders Alliance.  The disappearance of Danol himself has slowed the
    talks.

***The Jarell***
Homeworld: Oon-Tien

Spread throughout the
    Kathol Core Region

The Jarell is a former Imperial slave race that
    is very bitter.  This has spawned a strong hatred for outsiders.   Some
    still group in raiding parties and attack anyone that is not Jarell.  The New
    Republic is working on a isolation treaty with their leadership, but it is not going well.

***The Gree***
Gree Enclave
The Gree is a old mysterious species. They are
    said to be one of the oldest races in this galaxy.  They apparently had a war in the
    Kathol Sector thousands of  years ago, and some of their technology has been found
    here, including super-fast hyperspace gates, ship size and personal size.  They are
    very protective of that technology and recently sent a warship to destroy what Gree
    technology they could find.  This brought about the collapse of the Church of Kathol
    Prophets.

***The Kalvessan
    Starwolves***
Unknown
Not a lot is known about the Starwolves, because
    most of the records of their dealing with the Old Republic was destroyed by the Empire.
      However, it is apparent they are relatively friendly to the New Republic.

***The
    Mandalorians***
Unknown
The Mandalorians are remembered as the Jedi
    killers, and renegade mercenaries; servants of the early Empire and of the Sith.  If
    it is true, and there still exists factions of Mandalorians, they are to be considered
    enemies of the New Republic until proven otherwise.

***Clans of
    Timbra Ott***
Timbra Ott
The Clans of Timbra Ott were fragmented, and
    warring before the Farstar arrived.  they now are working to make Timbra Ott a
    central gateway world into the Kathol Outback.  Each clan has proven to be very
    radical and different.  Timbra Ott was a former penal colony of the Noble Houses, who
    have agreed to recognize Timbra Ott as an Independent Planet.

***Pimbrellan
    League***
Kathol Outback
7 world coalition of cultist who believe
    fanatically that the meek will inherit the universe; if the wait through and passively let
    major events happen, they will be the ones to pick up the pieces. They are very xenophobic
    and isolationist.  they have a small fleet of Old Republic corvettes.

***Kathol
    Republic***
 
A republic of 10 worlds, based on the Old
    Republic ways of governing.  They have a fleet of several Marauder class corvettes.
      They are very factionalized and politically torn.  There are some factions
    that would agree more to a government like the Empire.

Records show some of the original
    colonists of the Kathol Republic were scientists that were involved with the Starwolf
    project during the Old Republic troubles with the Union.  Some where actually Union
    scientists.

***Qektoth
    Confederation***
 
Another small government formed by Old Republic
    dissidents several generations ago, they too are suspected to be realted to another Union
    faction during the Old Republic.  They are apparently expert bio-engineers and
    promote bio-technology, genetic manipulation and genetic molecular grafting over
    cybernetics and modern technology.

***Lance of
    Endor, *Corellian Corvette**
 
A renegade ship terrorizing the Outback, in the
    New Republic's name.  This is suspected to be  a Sarne plot.

**Updated Status**: Destroyed at the Battle of Danoor.

**Aing Tii Monks**
 
Alien race long thought to have contributed to
    the "ghost ship" legend of the Kathol Outback, they are extremely xenophobic and
    extremely anti-slavery.

**Uukaablians**
 
Alien race from the planet Uukaablis, their
    planet lies very close to Qektoth Confederation space.

**Planetary
    Government of Danoor**
 
Once an Imperial ally, it is now collapsed and
    has given way to New Republic representation.  The planet was used as a Way station
    to Rift for Sarne. It is rumored that he shipped many political prisoner to this point and
    beyond, into the Rift.

**The Rift
    "ferryboat"**
 

Another ship that has contibute
    to the "ghost ship" legend, even the Aing Tii monks call it their "ghost
    ship".  According the Xenoarcheologist Fasha Dansun, it is an alien construct
    that can travel through the Rift without problems.  It stops at specific points with
    int he Rift when summoned. It is understood that the bulk of Sarne's Imperial Forces will
    be using this ship to get across.
