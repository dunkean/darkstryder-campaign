DRALASITES

**DRALASITES** 

Attribute Dice: 12D

Dexterity
    1D/4D 

Knowledge 2D/4D 

Mechanical 1D/3D 

Perception 2D/4D 

Strength 2D/4D 

Technical 2D/6D 

Move 10 

[Image distante non téléchargée : http://users.vnet.net:80/seawolf/sw/images/DRALAST1.jpg]

| **Physical Appearance and Structure ** |
| --- |
| average size: 1.3 m tall, 1 m wide  |
| average mass: 65 kg  |
| average lifespan: 250 years  |
| reproduction: hermaphroditic, budding  |
| body temperature: 30C  |
| Dralasites are short, rubbery aliens that have no bones or hard<br>        body parts. Their skin is a flexible membrane that is very tough and scratchy. It<br>        generally is dull gray and lined with dark veins that meet at the Dralasite's two<br>        eyespots.  |
| The internal structure of a Dralasite is very different from the<br>        other races. The Dralasite's central nerve bundle (brain), numerous small hearts and other<br>        internal organs float in a pudding-like mixture of protein and organic fluids. Dralasites<br>        breathe by absorbing oxygen directly through their skin, so they have no lungs. They are<br>        omnivores, but eat by surrounding their food and absorbing it, so they also have no<br>        digestive tract or intestines.  |
| All Dralasites go through male, female and neutral stages during<br>        their lives (these phases can be controlled with medicines). Males release spores intothe<br>        air, which drift until they become attached to a female. A young Dralasite then<br>        "sprouts" from its mother, eventually maturing and dropping off.  |
| **Senses** |
|  |
| The most important sense for a Dralasite is smell. They breathe<br>        directly through their skin, and the entire membrane is sensitive to odors. Their sense of<br>        smell is so keen they can identify persons by smell alone and can recognize familiar<br>        smells on objects or persons. The membrane also is sensitive to touch and to vibrations,<br>        allowing Dralasites to hear and feel. |
|  |
| Dralasites see only in black and white. They see shapes and light<br>        and darkness very clearly, and can see a difference between distinct colors (blue and<br>        green, for example) because one is darker than the other, but they do not see actual<br>        colors.  |
| **Speech** |
|  |
| Dralasites have a voicebox, but it works like a bellows because<br>        they have no lungs. A Dralasite's voice can vary from a soft whisper to a thundering roar<br>        and from a bass rumble to a piercing screech. Among themselves, they also use shapes,<br>        odors and touch to communicate.  |
| **Society and Customs** |
|  |
| Dralasites are philosophical and thoughtful. Their communities are<br>        small, and many Dralasites prefer to live alone. Dralasites do not care about wealth,<br>        power or status symbols. They judge themselves by the quality of their ideas and their<br>        ability to discuss important ideas wisely. Dralasites often hold large public meetings to<br>        discuss new ideas. Discussions and debates are among their favorite ways to relax. |
|  |
| Steam baths are another favorite Dralasite recreation. They mix<br>        perfumes and intoxicants with the steam. These effect Dralasites the same way alcohol does<br>        a Human. |
|  |
| Dralasites are widely known for their strange sense of humor. They<br>        love old jokes and puns that make Humans groan. Many Human comedians who could not find<br>        work in Human cities have become rich performing on Dralasite worlds. |
|  |
| Dralasites do not normally wear clothing, because it makes<br>        breathing difficult and interferes with their sense of smell. They usually carry their<br>        equipment on web belts. When they must wear clothing to protect themselves, they use<br>        special materials that let air reach their skin.  |
| **Special Abilities** |
|  |
| **Elasticity**. A Dralasite's skin is stretchable and<br>        supported by a complex muscle structure. This allows them to change the shape of their<br>        bodies, within limits. They can "grow" arms and legs to use for walking and<br>        handling tools and weapons, and reabsorb limbs when they are not needed. |
|  |
| A Dralasite can have a number of limbs equal to its number of<br>        Dexterity dice (drop the pips). The player must decide whether a limb is an arm or a leg<br>        when it is grown. For example, a Dralasite with a Dexterity score of 3D can control up to<br>        three limbs. It could have Two legs and one arm, one legs and two arm, no legs and three<br>        arms, or any other combination adding up to three or less. |
|  |
| Growing or absorbing a limb takes five minutes. Only one limb ran<br>        be grown at a time. A limb can be up to 1 meter long, and no less than 10 cm thick.<br>        "Fingers" for handling items can be up to 10 cm long and no less than 1 cm<br>        thick. |
|  |
| Even though a Dralasite can have many arms, it can not fire more<br>        than two weapons at once. When a Dralasite player creates limbs, he must specify one as<br>        the dominant limb, the same way a Human must choose to be either right or left handed.<br>        Also, despite a Dralasite's stretching and shrinking, the pattern of veins and ridges on<br>        its skin does not change, so they have a permanent "fingerprint" for<br>        identification. |
|  |
| **Lie Detection.** All Dralasite characters have a<br>        chance to realize when someone is lying to them (+1D to Perception to detect lies). The<br>        Dralasite must be communicating face to face with the character, and the Dralasite player<br>        must tell the GM he is trying to detect a lie.  |
