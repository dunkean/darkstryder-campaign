## Charis

The history of Charis has been relatively unaltered except that the revolt that Sarne crushed was started by House Shador, which lead to the Shattering. The Shattering was a period in which Sarne usurped Shador's power, and subjugated and suppressed Shador activity, causing the House to fragment more than the others had.
