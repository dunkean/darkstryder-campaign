# STAR WARS : THE DARKSTRYDER CAMPAIGN

# RESURRECTION

### The first Darkstryder Spin-off series!

### PROLOGUE: "CASTAWAYS"

The Battle of Kathol System rages, as the *FarStar* and her Bothan allies struggle to destroy the evil Moff Sarne. Following the battle, several survivors of the various forces band together in small groups to survive the hostile conditions of their new home, the unstable planet Kathol. 

 

 

***

### EPISODE 1 : "EIGHT AGAINST THE GALAXY"

***

A New Republic expedition is sent from Coruscant to discern the fate of the *FarStar*, but is attacked by the Sanhedrim ships in orbital blockade around Kathol. Corporal Palme of the *FarStar* and Speaker of the Yapi save Lieutenant Shara,  Zod Duer and the droid V-6PO from the wreck of their crashed shuttle. Flight Officer Brelon Mattius of the *White Wolf* and Bruuk the Yvaran rescue Stormtrooper Sergeant Rojam Mot and the Arcona geologist Vegath Tist from the Qektoth Confederation’s soldiers. These unlikely allies form a party and vow to find a ship, so they can escape Kathol before the violent earth tremors tear it apart.

 

 

***

### EPISODE 2 : "DUPLICITY"

***

The group comes upon a woman appearing to be a clone of the former Captain of the *FarStar*, Kaiya Adrimetrum. The woman has been wandering alone in the wilderness, and seems to believe that she actually is Kaiya. Meanwhile, strange green lights are seen in the skies over the site of Darkstryder’s ruined fortress as V-6PO reveals that the planet’s core is hollow.

When the party is attacked by a Sanhedrim Warship, V-6PO is destroyed and the others are forced underground into an abandoned Imperial base. Therein, Kaiya’s clone uses the facilities to control a huge spacefaring Katholhu to destroy the Sanhedrim craft… but gives her life in return.

 

 

***

### EPISODE 3 : "FORTRESS"

***

Palme uses the base’s computer to locate the closest vehicle bay, and finds a vehicle hangar eight miles away across the mountains. The party travels there, losing Bruuk when he falls down a chasm into a fast-moving stream. On arrival, Mot sights his old enemy, the psychotic ex-Commander Morwen Glave, in command of the mountain hangar, a large force of walkers, and some Qektoth Confederation troops. The party is attacked by a large patrol of Snowtroopers, driven off, and Speaker is captured.

Regrouping and deciding to assault the base, the party is led by Tist into a connecting tunnel underground which will lead them right into the sublevel of the base. Tist, however, forges ahead and assaults the base himself with Darkstryder artifacts he has been concealing from the party…

 

***

### EPISODE 4 : "ROOT OF ALL EVIL"

***

With all the base personnel dead at Tist’s hands, Commander Glave shows up in an AT-ST and attacks. The AT-ST is destroyed, but Mot is severely injured and placed in Bacta stasis, and Glave escapes into the snow. Speaker is rescued, along with the missing FarStar crewman Corporal Brak Peters. Palme uses the base’s auto-defenses to drive off the rest of the base’s returning walker patrols, led by the Qektoth Commander Varko. Mattius and Duer get to grips with the controls of a walker as Tist researches Darkstryder further. Palme and Tist have a confrontation about Tist’s secrecy in which Palme is severely wounded and Tist teleported away by one of his mysterious Darkstryder modules, to who knows where… 

 

***

### EPISODE 5 : "ALPHA MALE"

***

The Sanhedri Monk Qrygg appears in the base, and joins with the group, explaining that the FarStar has disappeared into the past, and that the task of destroying Darkstryder has not yet been completed. Heading north out of the mountains in an AT-AT and an AT-ST, with a cargo of speeder bikes and an antique AT-PT, the group is ambushed on a high pass by two AT-AT walkers under the command of Glave. Mattius and Duer manage to damage one and send it tumbling into the half-mile deep river valley below. The other, carrying Glave himself, comes alongside, and thanks to some heroics on the part of Speaker, Palme and Qrygg, Glave is defeated, his walker plummeting after him over the edge. 

 

***

### EPISODE 6: "ONE HOPE"

***

Descending from the mountain range in walkers, the group halts at a large mountain lake. In his ancient AT-PT walker, Mot detects a distress signal coming from under the lake. Homing on the signal, the group makes contact with R1-BJE, an astromech droid hardwired into a starship hidden under the surface. Upon commanding R1 to pilot the craft out of the lake, the group is astonished to see that it is the *One Hope, *a Jedi warship brought to Kathol a thousand years ago by General Halbret Dajus to combat the Dark Jedi Vee’khar. Using the logs, they discover that following the defeat of Vee’khar,it was abandoned and hidden when Halbret and her crew went to the aid of the Precursors ( the original inhabitants of Kathol) as their stargate overloaded and detonated in the catastrophe that created the Kathol Rift. Boarding the craft, the group gets it operational with the help of R1 and the droid crew and flees to the bottom of the ammonia ocean to make plans…  

 

***

### EPISODE SEVEN: "PRISONERS OF THE PAST"

***

Picking up Bruuk the Yvaran near the mountain lake, the *One Hope *heads into the hollow centre of the planet, hoping to discover the cause of the planetwide earthquakes. Once inside, Halbret Dajus’ ghost manifests aboard the ship, and takes the crew to the Lifewell, the mystic crystal that holds the souls of the Precursors captive once more. The first step to destroying Darkstryder for good is to strip away its power source – to release the captive souls. Halbret takes the crew inside the Lifewell, where they return to an alternate version of the past and stop the stargate overload which devastates Kathol. Rojam Mot gives his life for the Precursors’ souls.  

 

***

### EPISODE EIGHT : "A SHIFT IN PERCEPTION"

***

Returning from the Lifewell, the crew attempts to bring the ship online and leave the hollow core. The Lifewell crystal shatters, releasing the Precursors’ souls, just as propulsion fails on the *One Hope. *The crew discovers that there is a saboteur aboard, in the shape of DD-1, the ship’s security droid. Halbret’s ghost finds and deactivates the droid. Meanwhile, though, another traitor puts his plans into action: Brak Peters is actually a shapeshifter who attempts to destroy the ship and crew. He is foiled by Palme’s daring plan to blow up the armoury, and loses his life. As Halbret’ s spirit fades away,the ship flies up the hole in the crust left by the shattered Lifewell crystal, emerging in a hangar bay under Darkstryder’s new home, the immense Ziggurat once used by the Precursors as a starport…

 

***

### EPISODE NINE : "DELIVERANCE"

***

Under a withering hail of blaster fire from AT-AT walkers, AT-STs, and E-Web cannon, the *One Hope *crashes and comes to rest in the hangar bay. With the engine core on overload, due to blow in ten minutes, the crew abandons ship: Mattius and Duer take the ancient Z-95 fighters and blow up the doorway to the hangar so that none of the Imperial or Qektoth troops can escape, while Palme, Qrygg, Shara, Speaker and Bruuk climb higher into the Ziggurat. The group finds Darkstryder’s chamber, lair of the Kaiya Adrimetrum clone which now houses the creature’s mind. A fight ensues – Shara and Speaker take on Darkstryder’s Katholhu servant, a huge winged four-armed beast, while Palme shoots Darkstryder and Qrygg breaks her huge crystal. It is only then that the group realises that the breaking of the crystal has triggered the birth of the being at the planet’s core. Vegath Tist appears to protect his mistress, but Palme sends him tumbling into the huge hole which leads miles straight down to Kathol’s core. With scant seconds left before the *One Hope *detonates, destroying the Ziggurat, the small, weary band ascends to the roof, where there is no means of escape! Then, a Lambda-class shuttle accompanied by three X-Wings appears to airlift them to safety aboard the frigate *Quenfis*. Rogue Squadron has arrived on Kathol! And, as seismic events ripple across the planet’s surface in response to the birthing, the small group of survivors hatches a plan for one last, desperate rescue mission…   

 

***

### EPISODE TEN : "APOCALYPSE SOON"

***

Returning from the *Quenfis *with shuttles piloted by the Rogues, Speaker manages to win over his people by defeating a rival warrior in combat. He convinces some of the Yapi tribes to evacuate their crumbling home, and is reunited with his betrothed, Yipa. Meanwhile, Palme and Wedge rescue what is left of the *FarStar’s *Security Chief, Randall Vallens – his soul is preserved in a Darkstryder crystal housed in the body of a Ciro clone. Vallens almost kills himself to save Palme from an enraged Krakai. As Kathol shakes itself apart, and the Great Katholhu is born from the world, the *Quenfis *leaves orbit, on course for Demonsgate and then on to Yvara, where Bruuk’s people are being enslaved by the Qektoth. En route, Palme is made Security Chief of the *Quenfis, *and Mattius and Bruuk are invited to join Rogue Squadron. Shara fills in as Nav officer, and Zod pilots the ship through the Rift. And Speaker and the Yapi set about choosing their new homeworld from the ship’s holo-logs as Qrygg hatches an ambitious plan to retake Yvara and settle some old scores…   

 

***

### EPISODE ELEVEN : "EXODUS"

***

Arriving at Yvara with the Sanhedrim vessels from Demonsgate in hot pursuit, the *Quenfis *finds Yvara in the grip of the Qektoth Confederation, the Yvaran people in chains. Qrygg, risking everything, manages to convince the Sanhedrim to ally with him, and takes their test of leadership by commanding their fleet in battle. On the ground, Palme and Speaker lead the *Quenfis’ *marines and forty Yapi in an assault against the grounded Qektoth slave cruiser. Joined by Gunthar and Qesya Vth’naar, they defeat Commander Varko and his men, and get clear of the cruiser before it is destroyed. In space, Rogue squadron and the Sanhedrim vessels make short work of the Qektoth cruisers in orbit. With the Qektoth defeated and the Yvaran hive destroyed, the *Quenfis *takes on Yvaran refugees and sets sail for known space. Qrygg and the Sanhedrim go their own way, in search of their long-lost homeland. 

And, at the celebration in the *Quenfis’ * hangar bay, a Bothan Lieutenant draws Palme aside with talk of unfinished business – the rescue of an important prisoner of war on Kothlis.

 

But that is another story….
