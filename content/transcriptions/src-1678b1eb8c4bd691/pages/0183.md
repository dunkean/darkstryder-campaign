Unbeknownst to Drake, the Wraith has  been under his  nose for months.  The Wraith was  as­ signed  to  infiltrate  the  Bombaasa Cartel  by the Moff's office several months ago. He managed to get hired on at Bombaasa's ThrusterBurn Tapcafe,
