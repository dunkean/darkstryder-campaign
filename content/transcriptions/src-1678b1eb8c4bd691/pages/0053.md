Something was  calling  her  deeper  into the labyrinth, a gravely voice, in a language not her own. Or maybe it was her own. only distorted by the walls of the maze.

Then she saw it. A shadowy form. A large, dark spider, it's legs wriggling like tentacles, reaching out for her.  Her first instinct was to  flee,  but  she  hesitated.
