Hearing that the New Republic has conquered Kal'Shebbol,  and  may  be  moving  toward  the Pembric  system,  the  Bombaasa  Cartel  has  in­ stalled a  puppet government that will represent its  interests  while  claiming  that  the  people  of Pembric  II  are  pro-imperial.
