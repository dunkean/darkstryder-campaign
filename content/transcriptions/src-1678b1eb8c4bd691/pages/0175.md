As you  enter the  bar,  you  are  greeted  by a distinguished  looking  older  near-Human dressed in the formal uniform of most waiters. 'Greetings," he says, in thickly-accented Basic, 'allow me to show you to a table.'

The characters may attempt to mingle, though most  attempts  at  socializing  will  be  ignored  or politely rebuffed. Very few of the denizens of the bar are openly carrying weapons, and if the char­ acters  are visibly armed,  they  may be asked  by the bouncers to remove them and check them in with  Drake.

Drake is easily found, since he is the manager of the club. Any bouncer and most of the patrons can direct the characters to Drake, who is behind the bar with the bartenders.
