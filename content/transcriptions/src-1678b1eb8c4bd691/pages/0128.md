Initially this mission seems like a simple tactical mission: go in and rescue the survi­ vors  of  a  small  Imperial  research  team pinned down on Aaris III by unknown forces Characters with any tactical skills could be involved,  probably  led  by  Gorak  Khzam (who claims to have been here before). The rescue  team  might  also  include  Kraal  the Defel  scout  and  Lieutenant  Dajus  - with Brophar Tofarain flying his beloved shuttle In the interest of getting a better look at the planet.

However,  as  the  adventure  progresses and the characters choose to explore Aaris III  further, other characters with  more sci­ entific  skills  might  be  useful.
