## R2 ASTROMECH DROIDS

The FarStar has 10 R2-series astromech droids assigned  to  it,  primarily  for  starfighter  mainte­ nance  and  computer  programming  and  repair. Eight of these droids are permanently assigned

to the FarStar' s X-wings, while  two  others  are assigned on roving maintenance  duty  to support the FarStar' s R3 astromech units. Any of these droids can be as­ signed  to  astrogation detail  in  the  command center should too many of  the  R3  units  be  off­ line  for  maintenance. Since the R2s suffer less wear and tear, the tem­ porary reassignment is a  fairly  common  prac­ tice.
