# Lieutenant Commander Dasha Defano

## Starfighter Command, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Played by [Sinclair Manson](credits.html#sinclair)

 

![Defano](images/34d023bb0cd942bd333f80a0ba4cb2ff61606990cf84da068196c9e3a1c82280.jpg)

"A low-tech world? Let’s go make some crop circles!"

 

Dasha Defano is, like all Wroonians, quite tall, blue-skinned and dark-haired; she's also graceful and pretty, and is at home in her flight suit. Dasha was born in the tiny island town of St. Lucius on Wroona, to Loren and Recca. Loren was a blacksmith who specialised in making and selling trinkets to the many tourists who flocked to the tranquility of Wroona’s summers. He was a quiet, unassuming, good-natured man, well loved by all in the community. Recca was a trader, captain of the merchantman *Wroonian Rover*, which sailed the ocean-seas of Wroona bearing rich trade goods. Although a bit of a hellraiser in her youth, Recca matured into a good captain with a firm, if unorthodox command style – and an honest woman, who had no truck with contraband, piracy or slavery. Both Recca and Loren have never left Wroona. The young Dasha spent time with both her parents, as Recca’s voyages took her away from home for months at a time. The marriage was happy for the two or three months a year that her parents spent together – probably for that very reason. Dasha has a younger sister, Masra (‘Mazzy’), who ran away from home aged sixteen and was never heard from again. That was three years ago.

 

Dasha is a typical pilot - confident in her abilities, and not the least bit shy about telling everyone. She has reason to boast: she's very good. Dasha joined the Rebel Alliance several months before the Battle of Endor (in which she flew Grey Nine in point defence of the cruiser *Independence*). She also flew with the New Republic task force which captured Moff Sarne's sector capital, Kal'Shebbol - she acted as wingman for her squadron leader, Lieutenant Ranna 'Wing-Ripper' Gorjaye, who also came aboard the *FarStar *as its starfighter commander.

 

However, while she is all too ready to tell tall tales of her exploits under the banner of the New Republic, Dasha is less anxious to speak of her initial flight training and early battle experience - as part of a band of Wroonian privateers raiding both Imperial and Rebel shipping. Dasha left the privateers after she was forced to destroy a civilian transport to keep it from calling for help. Dasha later found out that one of her best friends from Wroona was aboard the transport. It's not something she's terribly proud of, so she keeps it to herself, preferring to boast more about her actions with the Rebels.

 

When she isn't flying, Dasha is fairly easy-going, but she’s becoming a worrier. She can be found relating her starfighter exploits to other crew members who probably have other duties they should be attending to. Dasha also enjoys lounging around the docking hangar, talking with the techs and the other pilots.

 

Although she seems at least to get on with just about every being aboard the *FarStar*, Dasha especially distrusts Chief Krudar, the Duros gunner. There was a Duros aboard the vessel on which she served in the privateer fleet, and she's afraid any Duros she meets will know something of her shady past. It's racist, and slightly irrational, she knows, but she can’t shake the feeling.  Dasha simply avoids Krudar whenever possible.

 

Dasha always carries her gear bag slung over her shoulder wherever she goes - and she even stows it in the cargo compartment of any fighter she flies. The bag is fairly light and doesn't take up much space, but it includes several personal and survival items Dasha is never without. Inside the bag she keeps a hold-out blaster, a medpac, one small rations pack, a glow rod, an emergency flare, a knife, and a holo-image of the best friend she lost on that civilian transport. She owns little else.

Season Two Update:

 

***

Dasha Defano was promoted from Flight Officer to Lieutenant after the departure and betrayal of Lieutenant Gorjaye. She was placed in command of Outrider Squadron on a permanent basis, and while her command style was a little shaky at first, she evolved into a similar C.O. to Gorjaye, stress giving her a similar hot temper. Dasha was deeply hurt by the revelation that Gorjaye was an Imperial sleeper spy – she had told Ranna secrets that she had told no one else, and now that trust, too was betrayed.
