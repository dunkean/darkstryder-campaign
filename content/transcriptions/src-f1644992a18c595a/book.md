FANTASY FARSTAR LEAGUE

Pick your own landing party… and watch ‘em burn!

This revolutionary new method of entertainment will change your shipboard lives forever. The idea is simple – just pick one crewmember from each category to make up a fully rounded FarStar away team, and score points each time they are actually picked for a landing mission by the Captain. Score:

*
- 1 point if the team member is shot or injured.

- 2 points if the crew member gets a kill.

- 3 points if the kill is a fellow crew member.

- 4 points if the crew member suffers a fatal wound, and expires painfully.

- 5 points if the crew member betrays the team.

- Bonus points for any special occurrences.
