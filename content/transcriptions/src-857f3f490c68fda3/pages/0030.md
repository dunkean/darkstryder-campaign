## Target Permits

Once a suitable bounty is selected, a corresponding Target Permit for the selected criminal must be issued. The issuance of such a permit acts as a legal form of extradition that effectively grants the hunter the right to transport the captured individual and return said person to the proper Imperial authorities.

It is important that hunters approach each hunt on a case-by-case basis.To begin with, individual Target Permits must be issued for every wanted individual the hunter has elected to pursue.

In some portions of the galaxy,Target Permits may have specific restrictions that are associated with local custom and convenience.Some, for example,may betime-sensitive(valid only for a limited time) orvalid only during aspecific time (prohibiting hunting"out of season").The price of a Target Permit varies,normally ranging from 10 to 1,000 credits per month.The exact cost is dependent on the value of the target's bounty, but averages out at 100 credits per target.
