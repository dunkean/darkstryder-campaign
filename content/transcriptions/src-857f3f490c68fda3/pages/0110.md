## Target Threat Analysis

Any characters checking through the Imperial Enforcement DataCore who make Easy investigation rolls will discover a new bounty on Jondrell originating from a private corporation in the Juris sector and, subsequently, that this corporation is a front for Mantis Syndicate operations.

The gamemaster can decide what rolls are appropriate to disclose further information on this company,though no direct link between the Syndicate and the Prefect will be established by these means. Those characters wishing to uncoverwherethefunds forthemostrecent bounty came from can find out by a Difficult computer programming roll that half of the funds came directly from Mantis and the other half came from Kallistan gems transported to Mantis by a smuggler.
