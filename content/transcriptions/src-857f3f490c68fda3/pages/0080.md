## Janildakara

Following a pirate attack on her family's transport, Jalindakara was sold to Zygerian slavers. After many years of suffering, she finally escaped.She was unable to locate her parents (their whereabouts are still undetermined), and she joined House Renliss,believing that the guild could help her find her family.Jalindakara has a developed a keen interest in poisoning, and has shown a marked aptitude for creating imaginative toxins.

Playing on her deep-seated hatred and resentments,her superiors have turned her emotional vulnerability to their personal ends and have manufactured an excellent tool for their own ends.

Janildakara is a young Human female, barely out of adolescence.She wears an armored halfvest and stun cloak and relies on poison-coated throwing knives as her weapons of choice.She has a prominent brand on her neck: the mark of her Zygerian slave masters.
