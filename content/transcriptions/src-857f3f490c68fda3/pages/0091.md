## Man Trap

The man trap is a miniaturized reversed repulsorlift field.A small, flat one-meter-square piece of metal is normally hidden; it is powered by a small repulsorlift generator, with a cord 10 meters long (additional length cords can be purchased).The man trap also has a remote activator.Once activated, the man trap creates a high intensity gravity field (repulsorlift repels against thelocal gravity,this reversed field greatlyintensifies the gravity).The resultinggravity fieldis so strong that a target stepping on or over the field ispulled totheplate,immobilized.Movement,let alone escape, is virtually impossible. Once the intended target is so immobilized, the acquisition can be quickly tranquilized and secured.
