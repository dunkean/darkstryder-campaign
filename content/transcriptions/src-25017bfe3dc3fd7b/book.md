# Commander Jessa Dajus

## Ground Forces Commander, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Played by [Mario Solis](credits.html#mario) in season 1. And [Kenneth Thom](credits.html#kenneth) in seasons 2 & 3

![Jessa](images/1c2bdc6836de4c8fcdbdb24e6e94c7fe90245d0ee6270f8263b454f323592131.jpg)

"You’re looking at a long-term hunt here, not just a quick search- and-strike. You need a capital ship. I can take you to one..."

Perhaps the most mysterious member of the *FarStar's *crew, Lieutenant Jessa Dajus is a former Imperial officer who served Moff Sarne. Although the sharp-looking young woman claims she's an Imperial turncoat, not everyone believes her or her motives.

 

Dajus claims she's after Moff Sarne for revenge. She says she was an Imperial shuttle pilot serving in his forces and was imprisoned when Moff Sarne purged his military and administrative personnel of "traitors". She says she was working secretly to undermine the Moff's power on Kal'Shebbol to rid the planet of his injustices. If she weren't such an important prisoner in the Moff's dungeons, Dajus wouldn't have been tortured - and she has several scars along her left arm, apparently burned in, to prove it.

 

The *FarStar* command crew initially thought that bringing Dajus aboard as a crew member would be dangerous - but they wanted to run the risks to have her along as an Imperial tactical advisor. Nobody else from Kal'Shebbol seemed to know too much about Moff Sarne's operations in the Kathol sector. Although Dajus doesn't know everything, she seems to know much, especially about military strategies and how Sarne operates.

 

In truth, Jessa Dajus was a Colonel, a top tactical officer under Sarne. She learned that some entity known as 'Darkstryder' was the source of his advanced weapons. She was determined to find out more - possibly for her personal use or to sell to her underworld contacts- but Sarne eventually learned she was digging too deep into his secret Darkstryder affairs. He tried to interrogate Jessa, but her resolve held and she revealed nothing. Moff Sarne was devising several devious and more severe methods of interrogating Jessa when the New Republic task force began its attack on Kal'Shebbol. Sarne had no choice but to leave Dajus in his prison when he made his escape.

 

Dajus' initial reason for joining the *FarStar's *crew is to pursue her own vendetta against Sarne. However, she's also interested in discovering what Darkstryder is and what kind of power it will give its user. This motive grows stronger as the hunt for Moff Sarne brings the *FarStar *closer to the mysterious Darkstryder. Dajus is focused on finding Sarne and Darkstryder - anything else that gets in her way is an annoyance to be ignored, and, if necessary, eliminated. She overrules and bullies anyone to get her way, but often her subtle persuasive efforts and sheer force of will can convince people to go along with her.

 

Dajus is sure that Darkstryder is important to her - it is the key to something, be it knowledge, mysterious powers, or something greater. When she sees it, she'll know. Jessa often has these kinds of premonitions - she's often lucky, she sometimes 'knows' things before they happen, she has haunting recurring dreams and is capable of pushing herself far beyond what others expect.

Dajus has recently become aware of the Force, and sees it as a reasonable, if not entirely rational, justification for her bursts of intuition. While on the planet Gandle Ott, during the     *FarStar *mission, she meditated in a ruined Jedi Chapter house, and since then she has tried more and more to ‘feel the Force’. Jessa’s perception of this mystical energy field was in terms of the power it could give her - until recently. Lately, during a refit, Jessa found a sealed box hidden deep in the bowels of the engine core. This contained a lightsaber, and a journal written in a strange language - Loh’khar the Finder translated this as the journal of one General Ibrahim Dajus, Jedi Knight - whom Jessa suspects is an ancestor. She delved deeper into the *FarStar’s     *history and discovered that it was once the *Freedom’s Messenger*, a Jedi Warship of the Clone Wars era. Armed with this information, Jessa has done some serious soul-searching as regards her loyalties and beliefs. She wants to find something bigger than herself to believe in, something that might save her soul, and the way she has been treated by the New Republic personnel has really made her think where her political sympathies lie.

 

As a result of both her service with Moff Sarne and her unexpected and unreliable bursts of intuition, Dajus knows a lot more than she's willing to reveal. Like most information available to her, she saves it to use when it can help influence others to follow her in achieving her personal goals.

 

Dajus is often haunted by dreams of shadows and crawling black spiders, tempting her towards night or driving her away from daylight. These dreams seem to have some ties to her intuitive abilities and seem to be the source of her inner dichotomy : she constantly wavers between supporting the *FarStar* crew, or eventually betraying them to attain her own objectives and power. She often isn't sure whether to subtly convince those around her of her methods and desires, or to use blackmail or sheer force to get her way. More and more each day now, she listens to her conscience, although it can get in the way of her goals. Dajus believes turmoil is good for the spirit, and knows she must use both the good and the bad in her to inflict her revenge on Sarne.

 

Few beings aboard the *FarStar *trust Jessa, but Captain Kaiya Adrimetrum placed great faith in her. Adrimetrum took command of the ship when Captain Keleman Ciro was abducted by Sarne.

Season Two update:

***

 

When Gorak Khzam, the *FarStar’s *Rodian first officer recently betrayed the ship, Jessa’s secret- that she was no shuttle pilot, but a top tactical Colonel - came out. Captain Adrimetrum chose to ignore Jessa’s past in the light of her service to the ship, and start with a clean datapad, as it were. Gorak, her old enemy, was gone, and she no longer had the deadlock of mutual distrust and dislike hanging over her head.

Soon afterwards, Jessa and Adrimetrum shared a dream in which Ciro called out to them and told them his location (another Force manifestation?) When they went to rescue him, they confronted Sarne, and Adrimetrum traded herself for Ciro. Jessa lost a friend that day, and has sworn to find her, and make Sarne pay for what she knows he will surely do to Adrimetrum.

On top of all this, Lt. Ranna Gorjaye also defected to Sarne’s side, and it was discovered that she was a sleeper agent, and was in reality Commander Phaena Del’Gorjax, Sarne’s right hand, an aide so secret that even Jessa didn’t know about her! This woman occupied the position that Jessa thought she’d been in all these years! That hurt her, more than she cares to admit, and it marked Sarne’s final betrayal. As the new chapter in the saga begins, Jessa has examined her loyalties and decided that there is no way back. She is wholly committed to the mission, to her crew, and to revenge on Sarne. If she can get her hands on Darkstryder, she intends to first get back to civilized space and use it to set right the injustices of the universe. What she doesn’t know is that her single-mindedness of purpose and her newfound loyalty to a cause are dangerously counterpointed by her anger and bitterness. Perhaps this is a road to the Dark Side of the Force, but if it means victory for her and her newfound friends, Jessa is willing to take the risk...

 

Despite the crew's mistrust for her, Dajus is fair and tolerant of them. She knows her best chance of finding Sarne lies with the crew, at least for now. She can be witty, charming and thoroughly likeable at times. Jessa also knows about Doctor Akanseh's role as Sarne's torturer, but keeps it to herself, at least for now.

 

At this stage in the mission, Jessa has abandoned her Imperial uniform. She has also abandoned her civilian clothes - the grey jumpsuit and hooded cloak she took to wearing when she left aside her uniform. As the new chapter begins, she appears on the bridge resplendent in a fresh New Republic Naval Lieutenant’s uniform, to the surprise and interest of the crew. Wearing this as her badge of rank, she takes up her new position as Tactical officer (replacing her old foe Gorak), and her new role as acting Second officer.

 

***

Season Three Update: 

 

***

With Moff Sarne defeated, Jessa feels that a huge weight has been lifted from her mind. She continues to discover more about her heritage, having met Jedi Master Halbret Dajus (a thousand year old woman preserved on the Darkstryder homeworld) and fought beside her in her last battle. Jessa also travelled with the rest of the crew back in time to the Clone Wars, and met General Ibrahim Dajus aboard the *Freedom’s Messenger*, even staying aboard with him as his ship went down on Corellia under Mandalorian guns. Jessa still does not know how she survived that experience, but she did. Now she is more in touch with the Force than ever, but with the revelation that her father, Briskin Lotac Dajus, once an advisor to the Imperial Senate, has set himself up as an Imperial Warlord back in the present, she has choices to make once more. Jessa was promoted to Commander and is now in charge of the FarStar’s ground troops – she usually avoids the bridge as she and Commander Thyte have difficulty working together.
