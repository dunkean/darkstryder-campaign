## BDG  Series  Bodyguard Droid

Although quite expensive, bodyguard droids are a cost-effective way to receive around-theclock protection. Government officials, celebrities,  and  crime  lords  alike  find  the  design particularly  suitable  for  their  needs.  Since Lanthe Artifice is based in the Tapani Sector, this model is common in the sector.

Bodyguard  droids  are tall  and  stocky,  giving them an imposing appearance. They come in a variety  of  colors,  usually  a dark shade, and corporate or  house  logos  can  be added  to  personalize  the units.  Lanthe  Artifice  also produces  a  stunning  array of modules to customize  a  particular  droid. These include additional weapons  packages,  martial arts programming, or enhanced visual  or  auditory units. These range in price from 1,000 to 2,500 additional credits per unit.
