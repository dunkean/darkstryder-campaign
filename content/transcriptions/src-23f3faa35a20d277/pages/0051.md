## Mind

Effect in this discipline prey on the minds of their victims, altering thoughts without the individual's awareness. Such insidious powers allow Sith devotees to manipulate and outright control their targets, forcing them to undertake acts  they  would  not  normally  perform  under the given circumstances.

While the Jedi do use the affect mind power, they curtail their reliance on it, for it is far to easy  to  slip  toward  the  dark  side  when  you consistently  intrude  into  other  people's  mind to twist their thoughts to your will.
