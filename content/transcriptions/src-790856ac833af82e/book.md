Devotees of the Darkstryder campaign will note that there is a fighter wing assigned to the *FarStar* when it leaves KalShebbol to begin its epic mission. This fighter wing has been the focus of many a subplot in our campaign, and has changed shape significantly due to its extremely high casualty rate. You have to be the best of the best (or the spawniest of the spawny) to survive an Outrider Squadron tour of duty.

 

Original Squadron Leader was [Lt. Ranna Gorjaye](outr.html#gorjaye), played by Phil Rodger, and the squadron was christened by Phil. There follows an alphabetical list of the pilots of Outrider Squadron, past and present.

ACHILLEOS, Lt. Tal

***

Human Male from Alderaan

*

An easy going 36-year old human male, just under 6-feet tall with a light build, shoulder length black hair, electric blue eyes and a scar on the right side of his face. His right knee is bionic. Tal was a TIE fighter pilot who learned the truth about the Empire after the Battle of Endor and began a crusade against its remnants as a vigilante. Joined the *FarStar* mission after being shot down over Kal-Shebbol, on a private vendetta against Sarne during which all but two of his men were killed.

*

- Development:

*

- Started a relationship with F.O. Bel Tarravon. Caused discipline problems due to his abrasive attitude towards the Flight Leader (Dasha Defano) leading to her temporary resignation. Became very depressed following the death of Bel Tarravon. Became the guardian of Bel Tarravon`s younger brother Telli. During a mutiny, Achilleos shot and killed Oouban Khilaarn, one of his friends from his band of vigilantes who had threatened Telli Tarravon. Formed a friendship with Carne Lopak.

TARRENS, Doctor Drigor

***

Human Male from Qektoth Prime

*

Born in Kathol Sector, Dr. Tarrens was an engineer and scientist who used to work for the Qektoth Confederation, a nation obsessed with bio-technology. He aided Moff Sarne and Phaena DelGorjax in a technology-trading operation to design the ultimate starfighter  the *Nemesis-*class X-wing. Powered by renewable Darkstryder technology, it incorporated an X-wings chassis, Qektoth bio-weapons and a Darkstryder based cloaking device powered by the pilots life force. Tarrens resigned from the Qektoth after having a change of heart, five years ago. His blinding enthusiasm for the Nemesis project was diminished by actually completing a prototype, and he finally realized what evil people were using his talents for their own ends. He also discovered many unpleasant secrets about the Confederation, and was caught snooping, injured, rebuilt with cybernetic enhancements, and brought to trial by the Confederation for treason. He escaped, fled and took all his work with him and hid out as a hermit on Uukablis. In the five years that passed, without Tarrens and his research, Sarne was only able to build five working *Nemesis*-class starfighters. Tarrens joined the FarStar crew when they helped him find a cure for a disease to which hed been exposed. He brought his personal ship, a sleek Arakyd Helix Interceptor. He is 42, with a cybernetic eye and braided, grey hair.
