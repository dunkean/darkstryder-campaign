## Sector Calendar

When dealingwiththeoutsidegalaxy,Tapani sector uses the Imperial calendar.However, in domestic affairs,the Tapani calendar (established by Shey Tapani in 5412) is still in common use (largely as a symbol of the sector's autonomy).

Thelocal Tapani calendar has been tweaked slightly to conform to Galactic Standard Time, but the traditional names of the days,months, and holidays remain in place. There are 60 minutes to an hour,24 hours to a day,5 days to a week, 7weeks to a month,and ten months to a year.Toround out the 368 day year,there are three festival weeks and three one-day holidays.

After the disbanding of the Imperial Senate and the elevation of the Imperial governors, most worlds of the Empire passed directly into the control of Palpatine and his military and civil servants.However,Tapani sector managed to escape this fate, and is one of the few regions of Imperial space beyond the Core to retain a fair degree of autonomy.
