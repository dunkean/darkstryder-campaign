Traveling out into the Jundland Wastes, to the abandoned home of Obi-Wan Kenobi, Luke constructed alightsaber.It was light and easyin his hands, and the beam, his own personal signature, a stunning, vivid green. Now he was a Jedi-or so he thought. He handed his weapon into the tender care of his faithful astromech droid, Artoo-Detoo, and sent Artoo and SeeThreepio into Jabba's palace.

Luke expected the rescue of Han Solo to be a relatively simple matter. All he needed to do was get inside - everything else was already in place. Artoo and Threepio had been given to Jabba as "gifts," and Lando and Leia, both in disguise, were inside as well. All he had to do was wait for the right moment, make sure that his friends were free of danger, and get his Jedi weapon from Artoo. The rest would be simple.

Well, the actual event didn't turn out to be quite as simple as Luke expected. Luke didn't particularly enjoy taking on the Rancor; Han and Lando came within centimeters of becoming lunch for the Sarlaac;and Luke took a blaster hit to his artificial hand which came perilously closetoincapacitatingit at adangerously inconvenient moment.Still, with thehelp oftheForce, he and his friends succeeded.Han was rescued, and Jabba's organization was destroyed.

After the rescue, Luke didn't rendezvous with the Alliance fleet with his friends.Instead, he journeyed back to a certain bog planet, to "keep a promise to an old friend" and to ask him a few questions.

At heart, Luke was still unsure whether or not he was truly a Jedi. At times he felt that he was
