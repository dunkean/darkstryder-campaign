## Gamorrean Guards

Jabba employed nine Gamorreans as palace guards, in additionto several others who worked at his townhouse or were out "on assignment." Gamorreans are fierce and determined, if somewhat slow,warriors, completely loyal to their employer (as long as they are paid on time and they get to hurt things on a regular basis). Occasionally Jabba would send them out into Tatooine tohandle very simple assignments,

such as strongarming moisture farmers whowere reluctant to pay protection, but mostly they remainedwithin the walls of Jabba's Palace, at their bloated master's side.
