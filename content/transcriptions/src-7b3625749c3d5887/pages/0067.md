## Imperial Assault Vehicles

## Imperial AT-AT (All-Terrain Armored Transport)

Though usually the forward rank of ground-based battles, the AT-AT was de prived of its usual role by the massive trees of Endor's moon.The forest limited the mobility of the Empire's AT-ATs, confining the walkers to a limited area around the Imperial garrison.As aresult,the walkers could not fully participate in the battle at the bunker, leaving their smaller counterparts,the AT-STs scout walkers)to provide all covering fire for the stormtroopers protecting the shield generator.
