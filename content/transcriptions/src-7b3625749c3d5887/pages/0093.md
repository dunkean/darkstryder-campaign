## Further Adventures ..

While playing the events of the Star Wars movies can be great fun, playing through and around those events offers the players a chance to affect the balance of the Galaxy without forcing them to follow the scripted plots.

Below you'll find a few adventure ideas that link to the scenes of Return of the Jedi. Don't tell the players up front that the events they will become involved in relate to the. movie. Instead, allow them the fun of figuring it out for themselves.

## Hunters on Tatooine

The characters receive orders from Alliance High Command to rescue a captured Rebel (who could be one of the characters) in the Jundland Wastes on the remote planet. Tatooine. Apparently, Jabba the Hutt has placed a price on this Rebel crewer's head, and a pair of bounty hunters is bringing himto Jabba's Palace.
