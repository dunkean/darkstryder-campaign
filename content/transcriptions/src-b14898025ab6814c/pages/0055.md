## Chapter Nine:The Force

- Only Force-sensitive characters can learn Force skills and powers.A character must be taught a Force power to be able to use it.
- Force-sensitive characters receive Dark Side Points whenever they commit evil. A Jedi who allows evil to occur by inaction also receives a Dark Side Point.
