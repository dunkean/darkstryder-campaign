## COMDAT MODIFIERS

Drawing Weapons. Drawing a weapon is an action (-1D all other actions)

Setting Weapons on Stun. Can switch weapon's setting between stun or normal damage; counts as an action (-ID all other actions).

Prolection. Sturdy objects may provide protection. If the attack roll is high enough to beat base difficulty, but not well enough to beat added cover modifier, shot hit. whatever target was hiding behind.

Called Shots. Making a "called shot" against a small target:

+4D to difficulty for target 1-10 centimeters long.

+1D to difficulty tor target 10-50 centimeters long

+SD to difficulty for target less than a centimeter long.

## CHARACTER INJURY EFFECTS

(See Star Wars, Revised and Expanded. p. 97.)

Slunned: (per stun)-1D for that round and next round. Stun "in effect* for half hour: if suffering from as many stuns as Strengih dice. unconscious for 2D minutes. Characters remove stuns by resting one minute.

Wounded tuice A character wounded a second time is wounded twice. Falls prone. no actions for rest of round. -2D to all actions until healed. A wounded twice character who's wounded again is incapacitated.

Wounded: Falls prone, no actions for rest oi round. -1D to all actions until healed.

Incapacitased: Falls prone: unconsclous for 10D minutes. If character Is wounded or incapacitated again. he's mortally wounded.

Mortally wounded: Knocked unconscious. Roll 2D after each round: If roll less than number of rounds since mortally wounded, character dies.

Weapons set for stun: A *wounded" or worse result means unconscious for 2D minutes.
