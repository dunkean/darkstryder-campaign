## Force Powers

For complete descriptions of all known Force powers,see the Tales of the Jedi Companion.

The difficulties for powers requiring more than one Force skill are separated by slashes. For example, lightsaber combat's Moderate/Easy difficulty designation means that the Jedimust make a Moderate control and an Easy sense roll to activate the power.Difficulties thatchangedependingupon specialcircumstances arelisted as "Special."Seethe full text entryfor details.
