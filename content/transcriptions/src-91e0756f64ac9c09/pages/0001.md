**Report 10049: Alpha 

From: Kathol Sector Central Intel

Subject: A new Bounty on the *Farstar*

Date: DS 127  / NR Date: 43.7.27**

If Sarne's troubles weren't enough, now the underground has put a bounty on any *Farstar*
crew, posted by none other than Chuborro the Hutt.  We are not sure if this was
motivated by Sarne's influence or Chuborro is acting alone, but we do know he is paying
10,000 for any regular crewmember killed, and 25,000 for any officer.  With the
amount of crew you have, I am sure many will be willing to collect on that bounty.  
This, we expect, will also make it harder to recruit repacement crew.

---

**Report 10045: Delta 

From Sapella Delegacy

Subject: Sapella Report 

Date: DS 128 / NR Date: 43.7.28**

The scouts that were sent to find the missing ship have yet to report in.  The
search mission is expected to take long.  No sign of the ship that attacked them
either.

---

**Report 10048: Beta

From: Kathol Central Intelligence, Astrogation Division

Subject: Sarne is missing

Date: DS 129 / NR Date: 43.7.29**

The search for Sarne is expected to lead you into the Rift.  Between where you are
and the Rift are many rift fragments and nebulas he could be easily hiding in.
