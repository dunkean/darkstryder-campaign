# Ensign Hydan's Desk

Since the beginning of the mission, at various points, wildly sarcastic and viciously vitriolic, (but still intensely funny) material has been anonymously published under the blanket banner "Star Lines". As devotees of the Wing Commander series 
of games will know, every starship needs its unofficial onboard magazine, and Star Lines serves that purpose aboard the FarStar. It's witty, irreverent, disrespectful of the chain of command, potentially mutinous, and really very uncomplimentary about som
e of the female officers' (not naming any names) ass sizes.

The individual responsible for ringleading the publication of Star Lines is one Ensign Hydan, a crack Rodian computer slicer who works on the bridge, and is affectionately referred to by Captain Ciro as "The Rodian Kid". Hydan, probably unwisely
, decided to go public in issue three. The material that follows is what we found when he trawled through his files while he was languishing in sickbay, courtesy of Dasha "No Sense Of Humour" Defano...
