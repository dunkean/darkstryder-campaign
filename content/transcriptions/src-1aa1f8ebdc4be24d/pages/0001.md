**Timeline** 

**Introduction** 

I've developed an epic plot that is somewhat inspired by two
things: Babylon 5 and the Dragonlance first trilogy of books,
which I read back in high school. I don't read the Dragonlance
anymore, but the dragon's in AD&D have always fascinated me.
Putting space dragons in a sci-fi game was something I did in
Star Frontiers back in high school, and I decided to re-awaken
the idea in SW RPG. It's a lot more in-depth than just a bunch of
dragons flying in space.
