# Doctor Akanseh

## Chief Medical Officer, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Played by [Sinclair Manson](credits.html#sinclair) (Season 1) and [Baz McAlister](credits.html#bazatron) (Seasons 2 & 3)

![Akanseh](../images/44a8d063b737a591cd266136b99afc848f5f3a89eaf4e37eeb7ac10d132d6de6.jpg)

"What was I charged with? Sarne seldom needed anything so mundane as a charge."

This Mon Calamari surgeon was a great philanthropist who dreamed of bringing medical services to the underprivileged backwater worlds of the Outer Rim Territories. Although ageing, he was already an accomplished surgeon when he fled his homeworld with his younger brother, Heskan, to avoid slave service with the Empire. Akanseh and Heskan spent several years in hiding, working on a surgical suite almost fully automated by droids. This suite's components could fit into the hold of a light freighter and could be quickly assembled to form a field surgery unit. Akanseh planned to supervise the surgical suite as he travelled, with Heskan and his medical droids assisting him and performing many routine operations on their own.

Akanseh actually constructed his suite, and assembled a choice group of medical droids that he programmed himself. A few months prior to the time of the Empire's defeat at Endor, the brothers began bringing their medical services to the galactic backwaters, helping many beings who were oppressed by the Empire. Several free-traders helped them transport the surgical suite to the outer systems, usually in exchange for free medical services or a reduced travel fee.

And then disaster entered Akanseh’s life. The brothers travelled aboard the Carosite Hospital ship *Sudden Restoration* to the Bothan colony of Kothlis when they heard of the Imperial attacks on the world. TIE squadrons had made bombing runs of all the major population centers, and some said this was in retaliation for some great crime the Bothans had committed in the name of the Rebellion. Heskan’s political sympathies ran towards the Rebel Alliance, and while Akanseh himself was determined to stay out of politics, he was persuaded by his brother to bring the surgical suite to Kothlis. The two set up camp at a landing field, running the surgical suite out of the back of a small freighter on loan from the Carosites.

On the second day, the two were both tired from their work. Endless streams of injured and dying Bothans lined up at the surgery, and medical miracles were performed by the Mon Calamari brothers - but there was little time for sleep. As the droids laboured on, the brothers rested, but when Akanseh awoke, there was no sign of Heskan. A Bothan sat on the end of Akanseh’s bunk - a Bothan whom Akanseh would come to curse as his tormentor for years to come - Borsk Fey’lya. Fey’lya explained that he had kidnapped Heskan, and if Akanseh did not comply with his instructions, or went to the authorities, Heskan would die. What Fey’lya wanted was for Akanseh to start ‘losing patients’, administering fatal doses of drugs and even poisons to the refugees. In this way, many of the Bothans belonging to Fey’lya’s rival clan would die, and his clan, on the southern continent, would rule Kothlis. Under duress, Akanseh did this, fearing for his brother who was his only remaining family. He realises he did wrong, and considered suicide as penance, but Fey’lya claimed that he would kill Heskan if Akanseh died, thus wiping the family name. Akanseh has gone over this series of choices in his mind a thousand times, and has come to the decision that it was fated to be. This is how he has drowned out the screaming Bothan voices in his dreams for the last four years. He has learned to go without sleep for days at a time, to avoid dreaming, but he always keeps holos of the Kothlis medical camp and of his brother close by him.

Borsk Fey’lya, after the Rebel victory at Endor, claimed that the Bothan spies who stole the Death Star plans had been working on his orders. Bothan voices spoke out against him for this lie, but they were soon silenced - there were not enough of Fey’lya’s rivals remaining to stand against him. So, the ruler of Kothlis gained a seat on the High Council of the New Republic, and the price was the blood of hundreds of his Bothan enemies. This price was paid by Doctor Akanseh.

Fey’lya used Heskan as leverage against Akanseh for years to come. The Mon Cal was very useful to the Bothan - after all, who would suspect a kindly old sad-faced medic of being a spy? Don’t people tell their doctors everything? Fey’lya kept Akanseh close and in thrall, but in relative luxury. The Bothan recommended Akanseh’s medical expertise to his political opponents, and Akanseh performed mindscans, brainwashes and prescribed personality-altering drugs on the hapless patients according to Fey’lya’s orders. After over three years of this, Akanseh decided to run away. Fey’lya sent him to the Minos Cluster to ‘re-educate’ the Governor, one of his political opponents. Akanseh packed his surgical suite and droids, went to the Minos Cluster and kept running to Kathol Sector out on the Rim. There, he hoped to formulate a plan to outwit Fey’lya and rescue his brother.

But Akanseh's plans ran afoul of Moff Kentor Sarne. On this final trip, the freighter he was travelling in was captured by an Imperial Interdictor cruiser. Akanseh was taken to the Moff's sector capital, Kal'Shebbol, as a prisoner, and his surgical suite was pressed into the Moff's service. However, Sarne saw much more in the Mon Calamari surgeon; his medical and droid skills would come in handy in the Moff's dungeons - as an interrogator. Since Akanseh was a peaceful and gentle being, not used to active resistance, he decided to avoid making trouble and be a submissive prisoner - again - and to carry out his duties in his own manner, not Sarne's.

In his new role, Akanseh rarely acted forcefully against his subjects - rather, he tried to convince prisoners to trust him enough to disclose the information that Moff Sarne wished to glean. He knew that if he couldn't break his subjects, they would be turned over to the Imperial interrogators and their savage droids; worse yet, Akanseh himself might be forced to torture them. One of his most traumatic personal experiences occurred when one particularly stubborn subject resisted his persuasive efforts - just when Moff Sarne decided to drop in for a spot inspection to make sure Akanseh was doing his job. The surgeon was given the choice to chemically torture the man, or face the same torture himself.

Akanseh was freed when the New Republic forces and Lieutenant Page's commandos liberated Kal'Shebbol from Moff Sarne's iron grip. The commando squad who broke open his cell included a Bothan who handled Akanseh’s debriefing personally. Akanseh was interrogated and tortured by Bothan agents of Fey’lya for twelve hours straight, attempting to glean whether he had given any secrets to Sarne, and whether he could still be used for Fey’lya’s purposes.

Eventually, Akanseh was released and told to apply for the job of Chief Medical Officer aboard the *FarStar*. He got the post, and was briefed by the Bothans to beam back reports of the ship’s location as it headed out into unknown space after Moff Sarne and his Darkstryder technology - presumably so that Fey’lya could mobilise a fleet to follow on and take Sarne’s prize for himself. Akanseh protested, until he was shown a holotrans of his brother, haggard and anaemic, but alive. The two were allowed to speak, their first few sentences for years. This cowed Akanseh at last, and he agreed to Fey’lya’s demands. Perhaps out on the Rim he could find a way to get Heskan back and run to where neither Sarne nor the Bothans could find him. 

So, the Mon Calamari surgeon joined the *FarStar* crew. He maintains that he did not know Sarne's reasons for imprisoning him, and claims that his time in the dungeons was spent tinkering with droids and offering medical attention to the other prisoners (both of which duties he actually performed), but sometimes the guilt caused by his lying by omission almost tears him apart inside.

Akanseh's official duties aboard the *FarStar* include keeping track of all medical supplies and machinery, as well as maintaining a pool of operational droids of all kinds. He's interested in bringing medical advances and assistance to anyone the *FarStar *encounters, as Akanseh believes that withholding medical aid is almost as bad as actually inflicting injury, and maybe this way he can do penance for his evils. Akanseh is not a good combat field medic, as he often gets confused and panicky in stressful combat situations. However, his keenness to help others often ensures that he is the first into the shuttle when a planetary relief mission is announced. Akanseh excels in his shipboard duties and is also very effective when dealing with droids. His ultimate goal is to somehow replicate his portable surgical suite aboard the     *FarStar*, so he can leave the ship with some collateral to help him run away. However, since this isn't the mission objective, he secretly hoards medical equipment and supplies and works to his own end in the few hours when he is off-duty. Akanseh is also not above giving away much-needed medical supplies to the needy, crewmembers or otherwise, without the Captain's approval or knowledge - although this means some 'creative accounting' with the records at a later stage. He is also trying to modify the ship’s droids to continue his comm signals to the Bothans after he jumps ship, so that Fey’lya will not kill Heskan out of hand when the transmissions cease.
