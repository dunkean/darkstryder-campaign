As a young adult, you desperately wanted to go to Coruscant to continue your education,but were turned down because you were an alien.Being denied an educational opportunity because of your species went against everything your culture taught you.Your intense disappointment turned you against the Empire.

After your tour was up.you returned to Mrlsst to become a freelance tutor, hiring yourself out to teach small bands of students who wanted to supplement their official education, or who were too poor to afford it.Moving in these new circles,you met others who felt as you do about the Empire,and began to establish ties with them. You have resolved to seek out the Rebel Alliance and see if it can make use of your skills.

You served in the Freeworlds Common Navy for nearly a decade,first as ashuttle pilot,then as a piloting instructor.It was while in the military that you discovered your love for teaching.
