When the gases reach the main concourse level, the smaller molecules are carefully separated from the larger.The smaller gases are then frozen into blocks of carbonite in the many freezing chambers located throughout Cloud City.

These blocks are taken to panning chambers, where layer after layer is scraped off the blocks with high-powered, microporous energy fields. The spin-sealed Tibanna gas atoms, more com- pressed than the other gases,pass through the field and into a waiting processing chamber.

Utilizing nanopincers, the spin-sealed atoms are arranged against each other so that their unnatural shape will hold even in higher temperature.These strings of atoms are then sealed in canisters to be canisters to be collected and then sent off to various clients.
