Two types of forgery exist in the Trade Spine. The first, and less common, is the creation of false credit tokens used in the economy of some backwater worlds.The forger creates molds of the tokens and uses various metals or plastisynths to make the coin.It is usually not worth the effort or time for criminals to forge them.More common, more profitable, and more readily available on Cloud City is electronic forgery.
