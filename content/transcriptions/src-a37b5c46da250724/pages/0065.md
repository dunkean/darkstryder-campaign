## Bespin

The gas giant Bespin is located along the Ison Corridor, a natural spacelane located just off the Corellian Trade Spine. The corridor consists of four systems, Bespin, Anoat, Hoth and Ison, in a straight line.

Bespin is a lightly-traveled world, with twin moons, H'Gaard and Drudonna, orbiting high above. Unlike other gas giants, Bespin has a region of habitable space within its cloud layer. It is in this layer that Cloud City, along with other floating mining platforms, works to ex­ tractvaluabletibannagas from the lower cloud layers.

The planet has a fast period of rotation, providing citizens of Cloud City with two days and nights for everyone. Aside from Cloud City, the next major mining platform is a long­ abandoned mining city called Tibannopolis.
