## Too Much Punishment

The point of the asteroid field encounter is to provide an exciting encounter, not to slay the characters in their ship. If it looks like the characters are getting hit a bit too hard, back off, and fudge a few rolls. You might also help the characters shake pur­ suit. Maybe an especially thick asteroid showercuts off the hunters, to the interven­ tion of a massive space slug. Remember, it isn't cheating if it makes for a better story.
