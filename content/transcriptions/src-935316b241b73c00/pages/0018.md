There is nothing else to be found in the cavern.  As soon as you have found the medallion and read the inscription, the torches are extinguished, and growling sounds begin to echo throughout the cave.  It seems as if something is inside the cavern with you.  However, the growling fades.

## "Trust in the Force."

If they do not wish to leave, you can spend the night there without being attacked, despite the growling which fades.
