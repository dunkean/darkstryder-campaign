|   7 | Prisoner Escort An Imperial officer accompanied by two Imperial Army troopers is leading three Rebel prisoners toward a shuttle. You recognize one of the prisoners as Commander Straken, head of Rebel Intelligence. Across the starport, a squad of stormtroopers advances quickly toward, the group with the prisoners. Goad: The Imperials have Commander Straken. You must rescue him. What do you do? • You approach cautiously to attempt to get Streken's attention: Commander Straken recognizes you and signals for him to step back. If you don't comply, the Imperial officer walks over and quickly explains the situation; that they are in fact Rebel agents. The stormtroopers open fire and you and the other Rebels hurry on to the shuttle • If you wait and do not approach the prisoner escort , the stormtroopers demand that the group stop. They run for the shuttle, taking two rounds of stormtrooper fire as they do so. • Attack : You only fails this test by attacking the escort group or by doing nothing to aid their escape. Imperial Stormtroopers Combat Difficulty: 28 |
