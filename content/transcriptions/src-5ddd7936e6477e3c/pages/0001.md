# Sergeant Randall Vallens

## Chief Security Officer, New Republic Starship *FarStar*

Played by  [Jamie Thom](credits.html#jamie)

 

![](../images/730c387fb88e6e4b571f251ae38fdd71462b48b5e18c3c319cc065e788a05ae4.jpg)

"Men are dirty, sir. Blasters are clean."

 

Randall Vallens was born in the Delgado System in the Dardanelle Sector, on the heavily forested world of Delgado II. He led a quiet life in the family business of logging, piloting walkers which transported the felled trees through difficult terrain, and learning how to survive in the wilds for days at a time. Randall grew strong and tall, and was accounted a great catch by the local women. He wanted for little, but as he watched the stars glimmer in the night skies, far above the canopies of the tallest trees, young Randall longed to travel there, and behold the wonders of the galaxy, as his big sister Corla did when Randall was just nineteen.

 

At the age of twenty-one, Randall finally realised his dream, and left Delgado II. He signed on with a group of soldiers-for-hire under the command of one Tyler Haabon, a sixty-year-old veteran of the Clone Wars and a close family friend, also a native of Delgado II. Randall had little military training, but had been in the HaBora City Militia Reserves for three years, and knew a little about how to fight and how to keep order. Under Haabon, Randall travelled to worlds such as Coruscant, Chandrila, Kessel and Bespin to lend military aid in riots, intercontinental scuffles and external invasions. He became quite experienced. Then, at the age of twenty-seven, Randall (and Tyler) got word that the Empire had annexed Delgado II and had started butchering citizens who would not co-operate. Tyler immediately mobilised the unit, and went to Delgado II. The world was in ruins, a victim of orbital bombardment and strip-mining. The two men set up a resistance to help free the citizens of the planet from slavery and indenture, but this venture failed when Tyler was captured. 

 

Randall continued to fight, but Tyler must have been tortured and interrogated. The Imperials announced that they would start burning members of the Vallens family in the HaBora City plaza until Randall Vallens, leader of the terrorist resistance, gave himself up. Over the next few days, Randall lost his father, three brothers, grandfather and aunts, as he desperately looked for a fighting chance. On the night before the final day, when only his mother was left, he rigged the plaza with explosives and marched alone into the square just as his mother was being placed on the pyre which had claimed so many of his family. He demanded to speak with the Imperial Governor and his aides, and when the Governor marched out to meet him, Randall, prepared for death, looked his mother in the eyes. When he saw that she understood, he detonated the explosives, incinerating the entire plaza, a platoon of Stormtroopers, the Governor, his mother and himself. 

 

Somehow, he survived. he regained consciousness weeks later aboard the Rebel Alliance Medical Frigate     Sudden Restoration    , in orbit of Delgado II. He had lost an arm and a leg, but these had been replaced with state-of-the-art cybernetic models. He had also lost an eye, but this was beyond the capabilities if the cybersurgeons. His skin was scarred, and he had lost all his hair - scar tissue prevented any hope of it growing back. But he was alive, and in the caring hands of the Rebels. It seemed that he had scored the victory his resistance cell needed to go on and win the war, and the Rebels showing up had helped immensely. His former resistance associates convinced the Rebels to repair his critically injured body, and thus Vallens got a second chance.

 

Ever since, he has felt like he’s living on someone else’s time. It’s a good feeling, and it’s made him regard his life as insignificant when compared to galactic events. He has much more of a sense of humour now, and loves a good joke, practical or otherwise. He doesn’t feel much regret for the loss of his good looks, because he has his health. After the plaza incident, Vallens convalesced for several months, and then went on active duty with the Rebel Alliance. He was posted to the flashpoint Brak Sector, where the fight against the Empire was hardest. He soon was promoted to Sergeant and was put in charge of Brak Garrison’s Security operations. His commando training held him in good stead, and he performed many commando ops too. He was particularly effective when teamed with another Sergeant named Keleman Ciro, who became his friend - and Ciro’s friendship did not come easily. Randall once rescued Ciro from the Brak sector Governor’s citadel, after Ciro had been held and tortured for two days. The two men saw the Brak Sector trouble through, and Vallens was wounded many times in the line of duty, due to his disregard for his own personal safety. His lungs are now cybernetic after a corrosive gas attack ate them away, and his left arm has been replaced four times - once, he ripped it off himself and used it to brace open a blast door so his team could escape a high-security compound!

 

After Brak, Vallens was assigned to *Home One*, the Alliance flagship of Admiral Ackbar. He participated in relatively few missions thereafter, but was responsible for hangar bay security and some training of new personnel. He did participate in the Battle of Endor, as one of the crack commandos under General Solo and Major Derlin on the moon’s surface.

 

When the furore of Endor died down, Vallens took mostly desk jobs aboard various ships due to his failing health. He also took a three-month leave of absence to visit his only remaining family member, his sister Corla, who lived in Kathol Sector on the Rim. On arrival, Vallens found the sector to be in control of an Imperial Moff named Sarne. He slipped in-system in the guise of a free-trader, made contact with Corla (who worked in the starport as an administrator), and gathered information on the planet Kal’Shebbol, the sector capital. He then left, and made his report to his superiors.

 

Three months after that, the New Republic decided to pacify Kathol sector, and sent a task force of ships under Vice-Admiral Hagen Kolaador to the Rim. Vallens asked for a post, and was transferred to the frigate *Anvil* to lead the ground crews. He wanted to make sure his sister would be all right.

 

After the attack on Kal’Shebbol and Moff Sarne’s getaway, the troops moved in to see to the civilians and mop up. Enquiring as to the fate of his sister, Vallens was told by a local Twi’lek information broker by the name of Loh’khar the Finder that his sister had been missing for several weeks. She had last been seen making a report to Sarne’s administration at the citadel with the rest of her colleagues from starport control. Vallens found no trace of her on Kal’Shebbol, and so assumes she may have been taken by Sarne. He was in the process of requesting leave to pursue Sarne when he learned of the existence of a mission to do just that - led by his old friend Ciro! Ciro had already requested the presence of Vallens on the *FarStar*     mission and the old partners were together again! 

 

So far on the mission, Vallens has had very little to do. Ciro’s abduction soon into the mission depressed him, although he had confidence in Adrimetrum’s abilities to replace him. He was less impressed by Gorak Khzam’s security performance, however - Vallens, a security veteran, believed he could do better, but kept silent, being very respectful of the chain of command. When some of the crew rebelled against Gorak later on, though, Vallens was one of the more outspoken critics of the Rodian. He has become friendly with Gorjaye and Defano, the pilots, and plays Sabacc with them once a week - there’s some good-natured rivalry between the ‘ground-pounders’ and ‘hotshots’. He also owes Loh’khar a favour for helping delve into his sister’s fate. He kind of likes the Twi’lek, but is annoyed at his mercenary nature. Vallens is a bit nervous of Kl’aal, but very respectful of his strength and abilities - and a little envious, too. He’s convinced that Jessa Dajus might know more than she’s telling about Sarne and his administration, and especially about his sister’s fate. So far, however, he’s reluctant to approach or even challenge her about this. He keeps a close eye on her all the same. There’s not much contact between Thyte and Vallens, but Thyte’s stuffy, by-the-book approach rattles Vallens a bit - he believes you can get things done quicker by stepping over the line now and again. But there’s always respect for those in authority- there must be. And now Ciro’s back, Vallens is his most staunch ally and most loyal supporter. In his current state, the captain needs all the help he can get.
