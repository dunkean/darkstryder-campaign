Capsule: Kuat is a system filled with the lamed Kuat Drive Yards space construction docks orbiting the system's single sun. Its primary employers are Kuat Drive Yards and the Imperial Navy, which main­ tains an ominous presence overseeing and protect­ ing both Kuat Drive Yards and naval shipyards.

Kuat itself is a privileged world used by the· system's elite as a residence. A world of rolling forests dotted bysmall cities and estates, its terrain is broken only by an immense city or two, local Imperial government complexes, Kuat Drive Yards administrative offices, and Kuat Control Central, the system's main traffic control station which maintains the extensive starship traffic network bringing millions of ships, passengers and tons of supplies to and from Kuat every day.
