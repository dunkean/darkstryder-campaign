Mostcustoms officers in Byblosare pretty forgiving, especially for free-tradgrs with small ships. They realize it'sdifficult to make a living against the larger shipping conglom­ erates, and they don't mind these spacers stopping over at Byblos it gives the place a bit of color. Most class four and five infrac­ tions are ignored or liberally interpreted.

However, customs officials are pretty par­ ticular about datawork documentation. A good way to make a good impression with your customs inspector is to make sure all your documentation is legal and in order. The customs inspector insists each ship's operating license and each captain's flight license be in order, and inspects the ship's cargo manifest and documentation allowing placement of heavy weapons or import of restricted goods. Although the customs team has 11 to 23 other ships in the docking han­ gar to worry about, checking documenta­ tion is the easiest way to do a thorough check of a ship.
