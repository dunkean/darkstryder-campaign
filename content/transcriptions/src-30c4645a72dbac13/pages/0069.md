## Nofre Repair Bay

This immense repair hangar was built in the midst of the docking platforms of the main starport. It is large enough to hold one bulk freighter or up to four light freighters for repairs.

The Nofre Repair Bay is run by Loro Eels's younger sister, Nofre Eels. She keeps a large staff of mechanics (all Sullustans) on duty night and day. The repair bay itself is equipped with heavy equipment for all kinds of starship repair and modification jobs, and Nofre keeps a large stock of spare parts in locked storage bins scattered around the bay.
