## Sergeant Reggis Thine

Sergeant Thine is a roving security officer patrolling Kuat Central Plaza. His job is to keep an eye out for anything suspicious. But most of his time is spent watching passen­ gers running through the port. Occasionally he stops somebody who looks suspicious, evicts loitering vagabonds trying to fleece passengers, or settles some dispute between passengers and plaza business clerks.

Thine is a good source of information, since he's pretty knowledgeable about ev­ erything going on in Kuat Central Plaza.
