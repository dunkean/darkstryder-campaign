**Baz writes:**

*Darkstryder:Resurrection* was the first ever spin-off campaign birthed from the Darkstryder Campaign. I ran it over the summer of 1997, in the break between seasons 2 & 3 of Darkstryder, as a lot of  the regular players, being students, had done boring stuff like go home for the summer and live off mummy and daddy's bounty, etc etc. Resurrection followed the story on from "Endgame", the last episode of season 2, and explored the aftermath of the Battle for Kathol. As downed pilots, FarStar refugees and baseless Stormtroopers who survived (just) the huge space and land battles, and as the people from NR High Command sent to look for survivors, the players united in a disparate but basically good-hearted bunch. At first driven only by survival, they scaled new heights of bravery and dramatic tension week by week over the eleven episodes of the Campaign, solving the mystery of the planet Kathol, stopping the newly-resurrected Darkstryder entity, preventing the takeover of Yvara by the Qektoth Confederation, and rescuing the FarStar's security chief, Vallens, abandoned and left for dead after the Battle of Kathol. 


Corporal Palme swiftly emerged as their leader, Speaker as his cocky but faithful sidekick. Tist was the requisite traitor, Qrygg the enigma, Zod Duer the resident madcap MonCal. Brelon Mattius and Bruuk formed the most interesting bonded hive-mind in galactic history. Rounding out the team was Rojam Mot, the honourable Stormtrooper Sergeant, whose demise still, to this day, chokes us up.


Arrayed against them was an impressive set of enemies - Darkstryder herself, inhabiting the body of a clone of the FarStar's former captain, Kaiya Adrimetrum. The psychotic Commander Glave, a Stormtrooper whom Mot had had arrested for killing one of his men - Glave used the Battle of Kathol as a distraction to break out of prison. The Qektoth Commander Varko, a bio-engineered man with the endurance and tenacity of ten Gundarks, captaining a small fleet of Bio-Cruisers. The shapeshifter who masqueraded as Corporal Peters of FarStar security whom the team rescued from a remote mountain base. All of these were overcome.


These few unfortunate people were thrown together in extreme circumstances, and for the most part they overcame their differences and learned to work together for the good of the galaxy, creating new myths and legends along the way. And if that's not what Star Wars is all about, I must be missing something.
