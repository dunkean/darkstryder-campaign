## Xishel

Xishel is a self-exiled Falleen who has chosen to pursue his fortune in space piracy-a skill he developed evading the Imperial blockade of his home system.Xishelis charming and calculating though he is not above violence when necessary),rising quickly among the loosely collected Nanth'ri pirate lords.Recently,he hired a survey scout, Durn, to follow up a lead on one of Celis Mott's treasure troves. Durn located a signal from an old beckon call, and returned to collect his fee. He overheard a conversation, realized what the beckon call indicated, and decided to squeeze Xishel for more money, with his data disc as protection.Xishel had him shot anyway as he'd always intended to.
