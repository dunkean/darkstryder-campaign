## THE TEST

The Test is a show of bravery, luck and cleverness. The candidate is put, unarmed, in a pit with six large jars and a club.One of the jars contains a captive-and angry-Maoi.The candidate must shatter at least two jars, with the club, without freeing the caged Maoi. The candidate may not open the jars or otherwise test the jars, although use of natural or Ta-Ree senses (a form of "magic" the Yapi practice) is perfectly fair.
