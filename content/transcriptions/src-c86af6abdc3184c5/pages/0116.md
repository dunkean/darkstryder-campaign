Renegade in his command. Ten years ago,Sarne won an appointment as Moff of the remote Kathol sector, somewhat to the disappointment of his peers.

Sarne quickly established an iron grip on Kathol sector,keeping Rebel activity to a minimum by staging "Rebel atrocities"and crushing nascent rebellion wherever it rose.Only one world, Charis, attempted an outright rebellion under Moff Sarne, and it suffered devastation.

Sarne made contact with the alien bio-construct DarkStryder during his scouting mission in beyond the Kathol Rift, and made a deal to provide the creature with Force-sensitives in return for DarkStryder's exotic technology.Jessa Dajus was carefully cultivated by Sarne for this,as were certain other Force-sensitives.Sarne planned to de liver them all at once,but the New Republic assault on Kal'Shebbol changed that.
