The Kathol  sensing the massive energy surge thrown off by the Rift would wreak untold destruction on their home planet-activated an ancient contingency plan. Leaving behind a single caretaker who had been genetically engineered to live for centuries, the Kathol planned toexistin thereceptacleuntil the danger passed.Their guardian was supposed to release them after a period of time, but the creature instead decided it preferred to rule, rather than serve.
