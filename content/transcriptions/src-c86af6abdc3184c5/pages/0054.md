## LESSEN ANOTHER'S FATIGUE

Required Powers:Block pain 5D, heal 3D, lessen another's pain 5D, lessen fatigue 5D,speed healing 3D.

Difficulty: Moderate.Modified by proximity and relationship.

Effect: This power allows the Ascendant to remove the effects of fatigue in another.However,unlike the basic power,theAscendant must wait until the target is actually fatigued, before offering assistance.Hence the penalties for failing a stamina check can be counteracted, but must be addressed as they occur.
