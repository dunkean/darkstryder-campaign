My Convention Timeline

| **Ron's Convention Timeline**  |
| --- |
| I go to sci-fi and gaming conventions as often as possible. It is one of
<br>    my many obsessions. There is nothing like them. This is as a complete as I can remember of
<br>    all the Cons I have been to. Click on the Link to see some pictures I took at the Con. i'm
<br>    no photographer. |

**Date**

**Convention**

**Highlights**

**April, 1989 **

**DragonCon 1989 - Atlanta, Ga.** 

First convention ever. Made a lasting impression.

**March, 1990**

**[MOC 5 - Greenville, SC](http://users.vnet.net:80/seawolf/ron/moc5.htm) **

Guild ran a "Prisoner" party that was visited by Steve Jackson.
    It was quite merry.

**July 12-14 1991**

**DragonCon 1991 - Atlanta, Ga.** 

Piers Anthony, Betty Page Look-A-like Contest

**1992**

**Con-Spiracy 1992 - Durham, NC**

Accompanied Ken Richardson, friend and room-mate, as a guest. Ken wrote
    the RPG *Reishstar*

**March 26-29, 1992**

**MOC 7- Greenville, SC**

Guild ran a *ReichStar* LARP party in the Party Battles. The Nazi
    flag definitely hurt our chances of winning.

**1993**

**Hexcaon 93 - Greensboro, NC**

Got my first taste of the Greensboro gaming and convention crowd. It was
    quite fun and I felt quite welcome.

**April 1-4, 1993**

**MOC 8 - Greenville, SC**

This ones a blurr. We had too much fun at this one. killed too many brain
    cells. I remember Meeting Bruce Campbell and Patricia Tallman. I remember most of all
    Bruce hitting on Diana.

**March 24-27, 1994**

**MOC 9 - Greenville, SC**

This one was killer. The last of the great MOC cons. Spice Williams,
    Patricia Tallman, David Prowess.

**June, 1995**

**Hero-Con 95 - Charlotte, NC**

Good comic con.

**July 13-15, 1995**

**MOC 10 - Pine Mountain, Ga.**

The Con that was also a summer vacation. Nice try, but low turn out. It
    was great seeing Bruce Boxleitner, Robert Picardo, Chris Potter, and watching one Guilder,
    Russel Briggs, play in the all-star softball game.

**July 16, 1995**

**Dragon Con 1995 - Atlanta, Ga.**

We left MOC 10 to spend the last day at DragonCon. We realized we had
    missed out.

**February 15-18, 1996**

[**MOC 10A - Charlotte, NC**](http://users.vnet.net:80/seawolf/ron/moc10a.htm)

ROC of Ages "Cyber-Goth" Party and Hope....ahhh...Hope.

**May, 1996**

**ROC of Ages Con - Charlotte, NC**

James "Scotty" Doohan, Larry Niven, and the Hubble Space
    Scientists.

**June 20-23, 1996**

**DragonCon 1996 - Atlanta, Ga.**

Star Wars: Men-Behind-The-Mask, Richard Hatch, Kevin J. Anderson and the
    Star Wars Author Panel, great costumes, and always a great dealers room.

**September 20-22, 1996**

[**Sci-Fi Carolinas - Rock Hill, SC**](http://users.vnet.net:80/seawolf/ron/sfc.htm)

Men-Behind-The-Mask, and Richard Hatch again. Guild function. 

**November, 1996**

**ROC 96.5 - High Point, NC**

Guild put on a good Star Wars Larp Party. Quite fun all weekend.

**June 26-29, 1997**

[**DragonCon 1997 - Atlanta, Ga.**](http://users.vnet.net:80/seawolf/ron/dc97.htm)

"Morden" from Bab5 (can't remember the actor's name), Clive
    Barker, John Reys-Davies, great costumes, and the biggest dealers room yet.

**November 7-9, 1997 **

**MACE 1997 - High Point, NC**

Great gaming con, gamed and partyed with the Greensboro crowd. Quite a lot
    of fun.

**March  20-22, 1998**
**

StellarCon 98 - High Point, NC

**
Tim Zahn, my Star Wars RPG game, and Winston's Purple Jesus.

**March 29, 1998**
**

[X-Files Expo, Atlanta, Ga](http://users.vnet.net:80/seawolf/ron/x-filesExpo.htm)

**
Nick "Krycek" Lea, Steven "Mr X" Williams,
    "Skinner", and "Langley, the Lone Gunmen"

**June 27, 1998**
**

Phenomicon '98 - High Point, NC

**
Jason Carter, Lou Ferrigno, and $tuff.

**July 3-5, 1998**
**

HeroCon '98 - Charlotte, NC

**
Worked the new Gaming area.  Ran Cthulhu, and met a few people.
      It was OK.

**Sept 3-6, 1998**
**

Dragon Con 98 - Atlanta, Ga

**
Good but not great.  A little too crammed together. Peter Jurrasik,
    Jeff Conaway, and quite a few other B5 stars. Little bit of partying with the Con-Tour
    crowd, and $tuff again.

**Nov 13-15, 1998**
**

MACE '98 - High Point, NC

**
Highlander: The Phantom Menace LARParty, Gaming all weekend, Babylon 5 CCG
    tournament. Fun little Con.
